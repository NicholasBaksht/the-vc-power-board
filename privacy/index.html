1 Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how personal data is handled in connection with the website at thevcpowerboard.com (the "Site"), which is operated by Nicholas Baksht ("the Operator"). It applies to visitors to the Site and to individuals whose professional information appears in the Site's published data.

The Site is a static website. It has no backend application, no database, and no server-side code that executes on your requests. Every page is a file delivered to your browser, and all interactivity is computed locally on your device. This architecture is the reason the answers below are as short as they are.

This Policy does not apply to third-party websites linked from the Site. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

2 What Is Not Collected

The Operator does not collect, receive, store, sell, share, rent, or otherwise process any of the following:

  • names, email addresses, postal addresses, or telephone numbers of visitors;
  • account credentials, passwords, or authentication data — the Site has no accounts;
  • payment card numbers, bank details, or any financial account information — the Site processes no payments;
  • search queries, filter selections, comparisons run, firms viewed, or any other record of how you use the Site;
  • prices or other values you type into editable fields;
  • behavioural, advertising, demographic, or interest profiles;
  • precise or approximate geolocation data;
  • biometric data, or any category of sensitive personal information.

The Site contains no analytics software, no tag manager, no advertising or conversion pixel, no session recording or heatmap tool, no A/B testing tool, and no third-party marketing script. It sets no cookies of its own, and no first-party cookie is written by any page.

There are no newsletter signups, contact forms, comment systems, or other mechanisms by which you could submit personal data to the Operator through the Site. The only way to send the Operator personal data is to email the address in Section 14, in which case the Operator will hold your message and address for the purpose of responding.

3 Data Stored on Your Device

The Site uses your browser's local storage to remember one thing: the firms you have added to your Shortlist. This is a convenience feature so your selections survive a page reload.

Storage key Purpose Contents
Shortlist Remembers which firms you have shortlisted A list of firm identifiers already published on the Site
Shortlist details Remembers notes and settings attached to your shortlist Values you entered in the Shortlist interface

The following is true of both entries:

  • They are stored by your browser on your own device. They are not cookies and are not attached to network requests.
  • They are never transmitted to the Operator or to any third party. The Operator has no ability to read them, and no copy of them exists anywhere else.
  • They persist until you clear them.

You can delete them at any time by clearing site data for thevcpowerboard.com in your browser settings, or by using your browser's private browsing mode, in which nothing is retained after the window closes. If your browser blocks local storage, the Site continues to work normally — the Shortlist simply will not persist between visits.

4 Hosting and Server Logs

The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (a subsidiary of Microsoft Corporation). When your browser requests a page, that request necessarily reaches GitHub's servers and includes your IP address, the page requested, the time of the request, and your browser's user agent string.

GitHub collects and processes this information as the hosting provider, under its own privacy practices and for its own purposes, principally security, abuse prevention, and service operation. The Operator does not receive these logs, has no access to them, and cannot query them. Nothing in them is available to the Operator in any form, aggregated or otherwise.

GitHub's handling of this data is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement and the GitHub Pages documentation.

5 Third-Party Resources

Two of the Site's assets are loaded from third-party servers rather than being served from thevcpowerboard.com. Because your browser must connect to those servers directly to fetch the files, each of them receives your IP address, your user agent, and the address of the page making the request. This happens automatically when the page loads and is not something the Operator can observe.

Google Fonts

Typefaces used throughout the Site (Inter and IBM Plex Mono) are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, operated by Google LLC. Google states that it does not use these requests to set cookies or to build advertising profiles, but it does log the requests. Google's handling is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare (cdnjs)

The D3.js visualisation library, used to draw the Site's graphs and maps, is loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com, operated by Cloudflare, Inc. Cloudflare's handling is governed by the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Neither provider is sent any information about what you do on the Site. They receive only the technical metadata inherent in serving a file. The Operator receives nothing from either of them and has no account-level visibility into these requests.

Aside from prices, which are stored in a file served from the Site's own domain, the Site makes no other outbound network requests. It does not call any third-party API while you browse.

6 Individuals in the Data

Separately from visitor privacy, the Site publishes information about identifiable people: venture capital partners and executives. This section explains that processing.

What is published

Professional information only — name, current job title, current and former employers, professional history, education, board service, and dated career milestones, together with links to the public sources those facts came from.

What is not published

The Site does not publish home addresses, personal telephone numbers, personal email addresses, dates of birth, government identifiers, family or relationship information, health information, financial account information, compensation, or any special category of personal data.

Sources

All such information is obtained from sources already public: firms' own websites and team pages, regulatory filings and adviser disclosures, and published journalism. Nothing is obtained from private databases, purchased lists, data brokers, or non-public sources. Where a fact cannot be confirmed in a public source, the Site's editorial policy is to leave the field empty rather than infer a value, which is why many profiles are deliberately sparse.

Purpose and lawful basis

The purpose is to publish accurate reference information about the venture capital industry and the professionals who lead it, in their public professional capacities. To the extent the UK GDPR or EU GDPR applies, the Operator relies on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) — the public interest in accurate, sourced information about the professional activities of people acting in a public business capacity — balanced against the limited, professional-only scope described above. To the extent the processing constitutes journalism, the Operator additionally relies on the exemptions available for journalistic and academic expression.

Retention

Published professional information remains on the Site while it remains accurate and relevant, and is corrected, updated, or removed in line with Section 7.

7 Correction and Removal

If you are described on the Site and something about your entry is wrong, out of date, or you believe should not appear, write to the address in Section 14.

A request is handled fastest when it includes the page address, the specific field at issue, the correct value, and where available a public source confirming it. The Operator will acknowledge and review every such request in good faith and will correct any demonstrable factual error promptly. Where the Operator declines to remove information — for example because it is accurate, publicly sourced, and concerns a public professional role — the Operator will say so and explain why, and will inform you of your right to complain to a supervisory authority if one has jurisdiction.

You do not need to invoke any statute for a correction request to be taken seriously. Accuracy is the Site's stated purpose, and a correction is useful to the Operator regardless of who asks.

8 Children's Privacy

The Site is a professional research tool intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and because it collects no personal data from visitors of any age, it does not knowingly or unknowingly collect personal data from children under 13, or under 16 where that is the applicable threshold.

9 Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers transmit a "Do Not Track" header or a Global Privacy Control signal. Because the Site performs no tracking, runs no analytics, serves no advertising, and does not sell or share personal information, there is no tracking behaviour for such a signal to change. The Site's behaviour is identical whether or not you send one.

10 Rights in the EEA and United Kingdom

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, data protection law gives you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability in respect of personal data concerning you, and the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

As a visitor, the Operator holds no personal data about you and therefore has nothing to disclose, correct, delete, or export. A request would be answered to that effect. Any data arising from your visit is held by GitHub, Google, and Cloudflare as described in Sections 4 and 5, and requests concerning it should be directed to those companies. The Operator has no means of identifying a visitor or of linking any record to you.

As an individual described in the Site's data, these rights apply to your published profile, and Section 7 sets out how to exercise them. You may object to the processing described in Section 6 at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

The Site is operated from the United States and hosted on infrastructure located in the United States and elsewhere. Accessing the Site from outside the United States necessarily involves an international transfer of the technical request data described in Sections 4 and 5.

11 Rights of California Residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended, California residents have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

The Operator does not collect personal information from visitors to the Site, and has not collected any category of personal information from visitors in the preceding twelve months. The Operator has never sold or shared personal information, does not do so now, and does not use personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising. There is accordingly no "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" mechanism, because there is no sale or sharing to opt out of.

California residents whose professional information appears in the Site's published data may exercise their rights as described in Section 7.

12 Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. Because it holds no visitor data, maintains no database, and operates no server-side application, the categories of breach that most commonly expose personal information — credential theft, database compromise, and injection against an application backend — do not apply to it. No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed completely secure, and no absolute guarantee is made.

13 Changes

This Policy may be revised. The "Last revised" date at the top records when the current version took effect. If the Site ever begins collecting visitor data — for example by adding analytics, accounts, or payments — this Policy will be updated before that change goes live, and the summary at the top will be corrected to reflect it. The Operator will not quietly begin collecting data under a policy that says otherwise.
