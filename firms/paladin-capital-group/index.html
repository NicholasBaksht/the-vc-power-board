Paladin Capital Group

Paladin Capital Group is a Washington, DC based investment firm founded in 2001 that backs cyber, AI and deep tech companies. Its About page describes the firm as "a leading global investor that supports and grows the world's most innovative AI, cyber and deep tech companies through venture investment, expansion and growth capital" (the firm's own claim), and its homepage describes it as "a multi-stage investment firm that pursues compelling technology, product, and service opportunities" (the firm's own claim). It is therefore not a single early-stage venture partnership: it invests across venture, expansion and growth capital through multiple funds and investment vehicles, and its published team is organised into investment professionals, management, operations, capital development, venture partners, and a large Strategic Advisory Group drawn largely from national-security and intelligence backgrounds. The firm also runs the Paladin Global Institute, a policy arm with its own president and staff. Offices are listed in Washington DC, New York City, Silicon Valley, London and Luxembourg.
