Company Ventures

Company Ventures states on its homepage that it is "a first check venture firm" that provides "founders daily access to a community of excellence, embedded in world class physical space", and that it "pioneered the Residency model with Grand Central Tech in 2014". Its stated current interest is "founders focused on building generational AI companies at the application and data infrastructure / model augmentation layers". It is more than an investor: alongside its funds it runs the Grand Central Tech Residency (four months full time, a $10K per month stipend, six to eight founders a year), the Boost Founder Fellowship, and, from February 2025, Terrarium, which the firm describes as an embedded healthcare incubation studio and which is separately incorporated as Terrarium Inc. and separately funded through Company Ventures Terrarium Fund I, LP. In a Tech:NYC interview its Managing Partner said the firm invests at pre-seed and seed and typically invests $1 million.

Firm Timeline

2014
Grand Central Tech begins. The firm's homepage states "We pioneered the Residency model with Grand Central Tech in 2014", and its October 2024 cohort post states "This year marks the 10 year anniversary of the Company Ventures Founder Residency program!"
2017
The firm partnered with Milstein Properties to launch Company and The Company Building at 335 Madison Avenue, per its own 17 February 2021 post.
2018
Company Ventures raised a $34M Fund I, per its own 17 February 2021 post; the vehicle Company Venture Capital Fund I, LP filed a Form D on 13 September 2018.
2021
The firm published "From GCT to Company Ventures" on 17 February 2021, marking the move from the Grand Central Tech brand to the Company Ventures brand.
2022
Company Ventures Fund II, LP filed a Form D on 9 March 2022; its 8 March 2023 amendment reports $16,375,000 sold against a $50,000,000 offering, with a first sale on 10 March 2022.
2025
Terrarium launched on 19 February 2025 as a healthcare venture studio; Terrarium Inc. filed a Form D on 30 January 2025 reporting $2,750,000 sold against a $5,500,000 offering.
2026
Company Ventures Fund III, LP filed a Form D on 9 January 2026 for an $80,000,000 offering, signed by Matthew Harrigan, with nothing sold at the filing date.
2026
The amended Form D for Company Ventures Terrarium Fund I, LP filed 23 January 2026 reports $13,662,500 sold against a $25,000,000 offering.
