Peak Capital

Peak invests at pre-seed and seed stage in European SaaS, marketplace and platform companies, writing initial tickets of €250k to €4M and following on through Series B and beyond. It screens deals against a stated four-part framework of Teams, Traction, Thesis and Timing. The firm's funds are backed entirely by entrepreneurs rather than institutional limited partners. Sourcing is concentrated on the Benelux, DACH and Nordic markets from offices in Amsterdam, Berlin, Stockholm and Munich.
