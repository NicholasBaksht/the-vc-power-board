Gaorong Capital

Gaorong Capital, originally named Banyan Capital, was founded in January 2014 by three former IDG Capital investment professionals - Zhang Zhen, Gao Xiang, and Yue Bin - who left one of China's most established venture firms to build their own platform, part of a broader wave of spinout funds founded by veterans of China's earliest institutional VC generation. That shared IDG pedigree gave the trio genuine credibility and deal-sourcing advantages from day one, and Gaorong has built a strong track record concentrated in Technology, Media, and Telecommunications (TMT), consumer internet, enterprise software, and healthcare, backing companies including Pinduoduo and Kuaishou through to public listings. The firm now manages approximately $4 billion, reflecting the continued strength of China's venture ecosystem despite a more challenging fundraising environment in recent years.
