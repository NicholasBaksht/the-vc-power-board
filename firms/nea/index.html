New Enterprise Associates

NEA was founded in 1977 by three men who each brought a different piece of the puzzle: Dick Kramlich, who had trained under legendary investor Arthur Rock; Frank Bonsal, an investment banker who had taken startups public; and Chuck Newhall, who had managed investment funds at T. Rowe Price. Deliberately built with offices on both coasts from day one - unusual for the era - NEA's first fund raised just $16 million, a fraction of the more than $25 billion it manages today.
