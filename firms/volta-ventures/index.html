Volta Ventures

Volta Ventures invests at pre-seed and seed stage in B2B software companies. It backs companies originating in Belgium, the Netherlands and Luxembourg. Initial cheques run from €100k to €1M, with capacity for follow-on investment. The firm operates from offices in Ghent, Antwerp and Amsterdam.
