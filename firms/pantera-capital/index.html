Pantera Capital

Pantera invests in digital currencies and blockchain-enabled companies across all stages, and is one of the oldest funds in the crypto space. Founder Dan Morehead previously ran macro trading at Tiger Management before launching Pantera in 2003 and later pivoting it fully to digital assets.
