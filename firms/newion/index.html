Newion

Newion invests at seed and Series A in European B2B software companies, with tickets of typically €1-10M in rounds up to €20M. Its geographic focus is the Benelux, the Nordics and Germany. The firm looks for an underlying market shift together with founders able to lead the company through it, and pressure-tests the coherence between problem, product and sales model before committing. After investing it stays closely engaged during the first 12 to 18 months.
