ACME Capital

ACME Capital is an early-stage venture firm that invests in frontier technologies before they become mainstream, backing deep-tech founders across AI and software platforms, defense and autonomous systems, healthcare technology and space. Its stated sweet spot is late seed and Series A, with checks from $500,000 to $15 million and a preference for $5-10 million positions that carry a board seat. The firm was co-founded in 2018 by Scott Stanford and Hany Nada and operates from San Francisco with networks across the US and Europe.
