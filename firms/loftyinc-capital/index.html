LoftyInc Capital Management

LoftyInc Capital Management is a pan-African early-stage venture firm investing across Nigeria, Egypt, Kenya and Francophone Africa. Its firm announcements name a sequence of funds: the LoftyInc Afropreneurs Funds (LAF2 and LAF3, the latter closing at $14.2 million after a $10 million target) and the LoftyInc Alpha Fund, announced with a $43 million first close. The same announcement records that the firm's principals launched the Wennovation Hub in 2010 and established the Afropreneur Angel Group and the Lagos Angel Network; those angel bodies are investor networks, not firm staff. LoftyInc Allied Partners is named as the parent of LoftyInc Capital Management. Stated focus areas are financial services, retail enablement, logistics, healthcare, climate tech and artificial intelligence.
