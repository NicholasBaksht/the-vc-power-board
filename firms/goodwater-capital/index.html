Goodwater Capital

Goodwater Capital describes itself as a consumer-focused venture capital firm and as a "next generation venture capital firm" whose mission is to "empower exceptional entrepreneurs who are changing the world." It states that it invests in consumer technology platforms and utilities that people cannot live without across housing, healthcare, food, financial services, transportation, education and entertainment, and it has described itself as 100% focused on consumer tech. It runs early stage and growth stage funds, and it publishes consumer research, including an annual US consumer survey. It operates from Burlingame, California.
