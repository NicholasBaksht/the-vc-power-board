henQ

henQ invests in B2B software companies led by European founders, with initial tickets up to €10M. It targets seed and Series A rounds and makes roughly two new investments per year. The firm states a preference for companies in unglamorous markets or with atypical business models, and emphasises low dilution and founder autonomy rather than board control. henQ 5 is expected to back 8-12 companies over a five-year deployment period.
