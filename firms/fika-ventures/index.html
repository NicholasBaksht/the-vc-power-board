Fika Ventures

Eva Ho was a senior product marketing manager at Google and YouTube, then a founding executive at Factual and a founding general partner at Susa Ventures, before co-founding Fika Ventures in 2016 around the idea that founder service — business development, recruiting, and capital strategy help — is itself the product a firm sells. That approach helped Fika back SGNL, which CrowdStrike acquired for $740 million in 2026, along with earlier exits like Openpath Security to Motorola Solutions and Berbix to Socure. Fika closed its $160 million Fund IV in 2024.
