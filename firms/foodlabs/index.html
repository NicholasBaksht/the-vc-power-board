FoodLabs

FoodLabs invests at pre-seed and seed stage in European companies working on food, agriculture, sustainability and health. The firm also runs a venture studio that builds companies alongside its investing activity. Stated focus areas include food supply chains and infrastructure, agriculture and livestock farming, precision health, climate and resilience, and synthetic biology. First cheques are stated at $100k-$2M.
