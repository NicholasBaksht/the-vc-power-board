Bowery Capital

Bowery Capital is an early-stage venture firm investing at pre-seed and seed in B2B software and marketplace companies, and states a preference for leading rounds. Michael Brown founded the firm in New York in 2013; its debut fund closed at $33M and its third fund at $70M in 2021. The firm describes its funds as deliberately modest in size and pairs capital with an in-house Acceleration Team covering growth, talent and business development. It remains actively investing, with portfolio exits recorded through 2026.
