DST Global

DST Global's distinctiveness is concentration plus timing. Founded by Yuri Milner in 2009, the firm was built to invest into already-obvious but not-yet-publicly-liquid internet winners, becoming famous for writing large checks into companies like Facebook, Alibaba, Twitter, and Spotify before many institutional investors were comfortable with that strategy. The firm's official site remains notably sparse, which is itself a signal: DST still behaves more like a private compounder than a brand-heavy platform venture franchise, and continued to back growth-stage technology as recently as 2023's Reka AI financing.
