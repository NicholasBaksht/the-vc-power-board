Quest Ventures

Quest Ventures was founded in 2011 by James Tan and Wang Yunming, both co-founders of the NASDAQ-listed group-buying platform 55tuan, who launched a venture fund in Beijing specifically to back digital commerce and marketplace platforms across China and Southeast Asia. That founder-operator background shaped Quest's positioning as the 'first institutional investor' in many regional marketplaces, backing Carousell at Series A years before it became a Southeast Asian unicorn, alongside ShopBack and 99.co. The firm has since relocated its primary base to Singapore, becoming the largest anchor tenant at JTC Launchpad, and continues to focus on Seed and Series A investments in digital commerce, fintech, and consumer platforms across Singapore, Malaysia, Vietnam, and Indonesia.
