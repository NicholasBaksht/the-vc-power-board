VMG Partners

VMG Partners is a San Francisco investment firm founded in 2005. Its own January 2026 announcement describes the firm as running two core investment funds, a consumer fund and a technology fund, rather than a single blended strategy; the firm's team page groups its people under Consumer, Technology, Ecosystem and Firm Operations. The consumer strategy backs consumer brands and the technology strategy is led separately, with Mike Mauze and Robin Tsai co-leading the firm as Managing Partners. No further fund families were confirmed on first-party pages, so any additional growth or catalyst vehicles are recorded as unverified rather than named.
