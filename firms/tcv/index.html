TCV

TCV was founded in 1995 by Jay Hoag and Rick Kimball as the original crossover investor — the first firm to invest in both public and private technology companies from the same fund, a structure the 'Crossover' in its original name (Technology Crossover Ventures) still reflects. That willingness to follow winners across the private-to-public transition, rather than exiting at IPO, is what produced Jay Hoag's three-decade board seat at Netflix, which he joined in 1999 when TCV owned roughly 43% of the company pre-IPO. The firm has since invested more than $17 billion across 350-plus technology companies worldwide, supporting more than 145 IPOs and strategic acquisitions including Facebook, Airbnb, Spotify, Zillow, and Expedia, and now manages approximately $24 billion.
