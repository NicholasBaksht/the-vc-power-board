Titan Capital

Titan Capital describes itself as "Operator-led early stage capital" and says it "invests behind world class entrepreneurs looking to create wide-scale positive impact on India and the World." Its portfolio page states "Our portfolio includes 200+ companies worldwide across consumer internet, direct to consumer brands, SaaS, Fintech and Healthtech." The brand covers two distinct vehicles that the firm staffs separately on its own team page: a seed activity (Titan Capital Seed) funded by co-founders Kunal Bahl and Rohit Bansal rather than by an outside corpus, and Titan Capital Winners Fund, which the firm states is "an SEBI registered CAT-II AIF which invests in the early stages of technology-enabled startups across sectors" and which "invests in pre-Series A, Series A and Series B rounds of the portfolio companies showing high potential."
