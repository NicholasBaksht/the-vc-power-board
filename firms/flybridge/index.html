Flybridge

Flybridge is an early-stage venture firm investing at pre-seed and seed. Its stated focus areas are AI infrastructure and developer platforms, agentic business applications, and native AI for human potential. The firm states check sizes of up to $250K at pre-seed through its Next Wave funds, $1M-$3M at seed, and $10M+ post-Series A, and says it keeps funds around $100M. It operates from Boston and New York.
