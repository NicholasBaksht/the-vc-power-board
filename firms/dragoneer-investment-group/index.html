Dragoneer Investment Group

Dragoneer Investment Group was founded in 2012 by Marc Stad, who built the firm on long-duration, conviction-driven capital rather than the shorter recycling cycles typical of growth equity. That patience has produced a genuinely rare crossover track record: Dragoneer backed both DoorDash and Airbnb ahead of their landmark 2020 IPOs, pioneered using SPACs to take portfolio companies like CCC Information Services and Cvent public, and co-led Anthropic's massive $30 billion funding round. The firm now manages more than $30 billion across public and private technology investments, with a flexible mandate that lets it move fluidly between growth-stage private rounds and public-market positions in the same category-defining companies.
