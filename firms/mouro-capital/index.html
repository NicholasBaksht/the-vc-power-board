Mouro Capital

Mouro Capital invests in fintech and financial-services-adjacent companies from seed through growth stage across Europe, North America and Latin America. The firm leads rounds with initial cheques of up to $15 million and holds reserves for follow-ons. Its stated focus areas include capital markets, wealth management, insurance, real-time payments and programmable money movement, identity and data tooling, and AI-native software. It began in 2014 as Santander InnoVentures and was renamed and spun out as Mouro Capital in September 2020; Banco Santander has remained its sole limited partner.
