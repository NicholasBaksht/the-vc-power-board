Silverton Partners

Silverton Partners is an early-stage venture firm based in Austin, Texas. Its philosophy page states that it focuses on Seed and Series A financing rounds, that it funds and mentors early-stage startups, and that it invests where technology can be used to build significant barriers over time. The firm describes itself as the most active venture capital investor in Texas and says all of its partners are former entrepreneurs with experience as both operators and investors. Its homepage states it is typically the first institutional investor and the lead investor. The firm sorts its portfolio into four categories on its own site: consumer, enterprise, fintech and healthcare.
