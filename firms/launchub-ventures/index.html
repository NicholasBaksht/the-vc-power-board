LAUNCHub Ventures

LAUNCHub Ventures is a seed-stage venture capital fund investing in Central and Eastern Europe, described on its own site as a seed-stage VC fund for Central and Eastern Europe with 90+ companies backed and an initial ticket of up to 3M euro. In a May 2026 post on its own Medium publication the firm set out its focus areas as AI security, AI-native infrastructure, health and longevity diagnostics, B2B software for the European mid-market, and founders with domain expertise in regulated or under-digitalised European industries, and described first cheques of 300K to 3M euro with board engagement. The firm's Fund II closed at 74M euro in December 2021. The website's team page is rendered client-side and its roster could not be read, so the leadership list below is a floor rather than a complete roster.
