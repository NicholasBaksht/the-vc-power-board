4 Nature of the Data

The Service is built on an explicit editorial policy: figures are sourced from public reporting, and where a value cannot be verified it is left empty rather than estimated. That policy governs how the data is compiled, but it is not a guarantee of accuracy, and the following limitations apply.

  1. The data is compiled manually. Except for daily market prices, all information on the Service is researched and entered by hand by a single individual. It is subject to human error, omission, and delay.
  2. The data is a snapshot, not a live record. Assets under management, leadership, headquarters, sector focus, and portfolio composition change continuously. Figures may be out of date at the moment you read them. Dated figures reflect the date shown, not the present.
  3. Coverage is incomplete and uneven. The Service does not track every venture capital firm, every partner, or every holding. The depth of detail available for any given firm or individual reflects the research completed to date and nothing else. It is not a measure of that firm's or that individual's size, quality, or prominence.
  4. Holdings are limited to currently traded public companies. Portfolio listings show only companies that were invested in and that still trade publicly. Private holdings, acquired companies, delisted companies, write-offs, and failed investments are not shown. A portfolio listing on the Service is therefore not a complete or representative picture of any firm's investment record, and cannot be used to assess a firm's performance.
  5. Underlying sources may themselves be wrong. Information is drawn from firm websites, regulatory filings, and press reporting. Errors in those sources may be reproduced here.
  6. No obligation to update. The Operator has no obligation to update, correct, or continue publishing any data point, and may add, revise, or remove any content at any time.

6 Market Price Data

The Service displays share prices for publicly traded portfolio companies. This data is updated by an automated process approximately once per day.

  1. Prices are delayed, are not real-time, and are not quotes. They are not suitable for trading, order routing, valuation, accounting, or any time-sensitive purpose.
  2. Prices are obtained from third-party sources. The Operator does not control those sources and does not independently verify the values they return. The automated update may fail, stall, or return incorrect values.
  3. Where the Service compares a current price against a historical baseline, the resulting change reflects only the movement of that share price between those two points. It does not represent any firm's actual return, entry price, cost basis, holding period, position size, or realized gain or loss. The Operator does not know any of those figures, and they are not published by the firms.
  4. Certain price fields on the Service are editable in your browser so that you may substitute a value of your own. Any figure you enter is used only for on-screen calculation on your device, is not transmitted to or stored by the Operator, and does not alter the underlying data for any other visitor.
