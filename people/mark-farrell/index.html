Biography

Mark Farrell co-founded the San Francisco venture capital firm Thayer Ventures in 2009, which invests in travel and transportation technology. He led the firm's Series A investment in Viridis Learning in 2016 and joined that company's board of directors, and he was described as Managing Partner of Thayer Ventures at the firm's 2022 annual meeting. He served as interim Mayor of San Francisco from January to July 2018, taking a leave of absence from Thayer Ventures for that term and returning to the firm afterward. He holds a B.A. from Loyola Marymount University, an M.A. from University College Dublin and a J.D. from the University of Pennsylvania Law School.
