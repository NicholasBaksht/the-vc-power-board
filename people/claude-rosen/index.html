Biography

Claude Rosen is listed as Head of Operations on Third Derivative's team page and on the Third Derivative team in RMI's own staff directory. His RMI bio states that he leads strategic operations at Third Derivative, "RMI's startup accelerator program for climate technology companies", overseeing strategic planning, monitoring and evaluation, continuous improvement, data and technology products, investment fund operations, and administrative functions. It records over a decade as an operations and finance leader, with prior roles as director of operations at Homeless Garden Project and at The Future Project, and consulting work at Nonprofit Finance Fund with more than 50 organisations. He holds an MBA in Sustainable Global Enterprise from Cornell University. He is included here as part of the programme's senior leadership tier; his role is operational rather than investment decision-making, although his bio does list investment fund operations among his responsibilities. No join year, board seat or individually attributed investment could be sourced for him.
