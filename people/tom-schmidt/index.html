Biography

Tom Schmidt is a General Partner at Dragonfly, listed with that title on the firm's team page. His speaker biography on the Alternative Investment Management Association site describes him as 'a General Partner at Dragonfly Capital, a global crypto venture fund', says that before joining Dragonfly he was product lead at 0x, that he was previously a product manager at Instagram, and that he holds a Computer Science degree from Stanford. Fortune's February 2026 profile of the firm says he was brought on as a junior investor from 0x. No joining year is stated in any source read, so joinedYear is null.
