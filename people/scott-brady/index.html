Biography

Scott Brady is a partner at Innovation Endeavors, which he started in 2010 with Eric Schmidt, Dror Berman and Rick Scanlon; the firm's own team page lists him as a Founding Partner. Before venture capital he co-founded four technology companies, serving as CEO and co-founder of Slice and of FiberTower and as co-founder and chief technology officer of Clarus Corporation and SQL Financials. He serves on the boards of Aro Homes, Third Wave Automation, Afresh, Skylo, Trunk Tools and Machina Labs. He is also a Lecturer in Management at the Stanford Graduate School of Business, where he earned a master's degree in management.
