Biography

Colin Anderson is a Partner at Maven Capital Partners, based in Glasgow, specialising in property. His Maven profile says he is one of two Partners managing the Maven property team, responsible for originating, transacting, managing and exiting property investments across the UK, and that he serves on the Property Investment Committee. Before Maven he spent over 10 years with The Royal Bank of Scotland in real estate roles within Corporate Banking, establishing and leading the Corporate Development & Property Finance team for the Aberdeen and North of Scotland region and later leading distressed debt workouts with the Real Estate Restructuring team in Edinburgh and London. Before RBS he worked six years with EY in Glasgow and New Zealand in Audit, Transaction Services and Corporate Recovery. He holds a BSc Honours in Manufacturing Engineering & Accountancy from the University of Strathclyde and is a Chartered Accountant (ICAS). No joining year is published on his Maven profile. His profile names his recent investment experience as student accommodation developments in Stirling and Edinburgh, a Hampton by Hilton hotel development in Manchester, Horizon Ceremonies crematoria, Maven Ambassador residential developments and Goldcrest Communities retirement villages.
