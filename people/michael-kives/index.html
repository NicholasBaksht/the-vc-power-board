Biography

Michael Kives is listed on K5 Global's current team page as Co-Founder & Managing Partner, and the firm's homepage states that K5 Global was founded in 2018 by Michael Kives and Bryan Baum. Before K5 he spent fifteen years at Creative Artists Agency, rising to agent, and earlier worked as an aide in Senator Hillary Clinton's Washington DC office and as an interim spokesperson at former President Bill Clinton's Harlem office. He attended Stanford University and graduated Phi Beta Kappa.
