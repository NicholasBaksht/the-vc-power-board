Biography

Johan Bosini is a Partner at Quona Capital based in Cape Town, focused on Africa. He joined Quona in 2016 as a Venture Partner and became a Partner in January 2018, working on new investment assessment and expansion strategy for portfolio companies. Quona says he has more than a decade of experience in African consumer finance, insurance and fintech. He created what Quona describes as the continent's first automated mobile-only unsecured lending product and negotiated pan-African partnerships with major mobile network operators serving unbanked customers. Earlier in his career he worked at Goldman Sachs and at a Swiss Re subsidiary. Quona's site does not publish his education or board seats.
