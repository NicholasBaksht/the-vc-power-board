Biography

Ma Nan is listed on the Shenzhen Capital Group about page as 副总裁, Vice President. In an article published on the group's own site on 15 January 2026 he stated that the group then managed funds of more than RMB 510 billion (超5100亿元), had cumulatively invested in 1,700 projects, had 288 portfolio companies listed as at the end of the prior year, that hard technology accounts for 90 per cent of its investment, and that it manages five national level funds totalling RMB 100 billion (1000亿元) including the RMB 50 billion Greater Bay Area venture guidance fund. No education or prior employment for him is published on the group's site.
