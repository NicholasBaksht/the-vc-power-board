Biography

Zenas Hutcheson is a Venture Partner at Rally Ventures and joined the firm at its inception in 2012. His Rally bio states he co-founded and has co-managed Vesbridge Partners since 2004 and served as co-Managing Partner of SPVC, formerly St. Paul Venture Capital, which he joined in 1997. He was CEO of Vivo Networks, CAECO and Control Automation, and started his career at Bain & Co. and BNY. He received an MBA from the University of Chicago and a BA from Dartmouth. He is listed as a director of Ottometric, Inc. in that company's 2025 Form D filings.
