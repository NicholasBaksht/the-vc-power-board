View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Fabricio Pettena is one of three Investment Partners listed on Global Founders Capital's own website, alongside Cedric Asselman and David Sainteff. He was named as a Partner at the firm in the November 2019 announcement of Kovi's 30 million dollar Series A, which Global Founders Capital led, and commented there on the difficulty gig economy drivers in Latin America have owning and maintaining a car. TechCrunch reported in April 2024 that he was one of five partners remaining after the firm stopped raising capital from outside limited partners and became the venture arm of Rocket Internet.
