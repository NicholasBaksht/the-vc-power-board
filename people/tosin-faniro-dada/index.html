Biography

Tosin Faniro-Dada is a Partner at Breega, where she joined in 2023 and leads the firm's Africa activity for West and North Africa, working from the Lagos office opened with the Breega Africa Seed I fund in 2024. She started her career in accounting at PwC in Boston, then moved to Nigeria in 2010 as an investment professional at ARM Investment Managers and worked in the corporate banking division of Skye Bank, now Polaris Bank. She then joined the Lagos State Employment Trust Fund supporting entrepreneurs, and later served as managing director and CEO of Endeavor Nigeria, supporting growth-stage companies. She holds a BSc and an MSc in accounting. Breega does not publish sector focus or board seats for its investors.
