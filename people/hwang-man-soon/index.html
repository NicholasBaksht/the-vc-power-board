Biography

황만순 is Korea Investment Partners' 대표이사, the firm's representative director and chief executive. 딜사이트 reported on 9 December 2020 that he had been designated incoming 대표이사, succeeding 백여현 after twelve years, and that he had until then been 상무 and the firm's chief investment officer; the report also notes he was the first venture capitalist in the industry to come from a pharmacy background. The Korea Investment Holdings group personnel announcement of 16 December 2020 lists him under [신임] as 대표이사. 한국금융신문 interviewed him in March 2025 in that role.
