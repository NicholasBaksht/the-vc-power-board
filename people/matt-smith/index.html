Biography

Matt Smith is an Operating Partner at Haatch. His Haatch biography describes him as a recruitment and technology consulting leader who worked at Harvey Nash as Managing Director and CEO across the UK, Ireland, Australia and Japan for over 23 years. He joined The IN Group as Group CRO in 2020 and is currently Managing Director at Investigo and InX, part of The IN Group, across the UK and EU for all technology, CIO and Transformation brands. His biography states he has also held NED and advisory roles with Data Clarity and BLOK Bioscience; because it does not say those are current, they were not recorded as board seats. He was named in Haatch's announcement appointing a trio of industry veterans as Operating Partners. Haatch's Operating Partners are portfolio-support roles rather than investment decision-makers. No education, join year, IPO count or exit count could be sourced, and no investment is attributed to him.
