Biography

David Fialkow co-founded General Catalyst with Joel Cutler and serves as a Co-Founder and Managing Director on the firm's investment team. General Catalyst lists his focus as financial services, digital health, AI and data analytics, and names PathAI, Casana, Wonder and Resilience among his investments. Before General Catalyst he built and sold four companies, including National Leisure Group, Alliance Development Group, Retail Growth ATM Systems and Starboard Cruise Services, which was sold to LVMH. He is also a documentary producer and won the 2018 Academy Award for Best Documentary Feature as a producer of Icarus.
