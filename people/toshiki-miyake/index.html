Biography

Toshiki Miyake (三宅 俊毅) is a Senior Director at DEEPCORE Inc. A 23 August 2023 interview by 品川スタートアップナビ prints his details verbatim as "三宅 俊毅さん / 株式会社ディープコア / Senior Director" and states that he is in charge of investment at DEEPCORE, having previously worked in corporate management accounting and then as an equity sell-side analyst in the equity research department of UBS Securities. His profile on the yumeplanning investor directory gives his title as "DEEPCORE Inc. Investment Senior Director", says he joined in July 2022 after M&A advisory at PwC and a role at an EdTech startup, names Keio University and an in-progress Hitotsubashi University MBA, and lists him as a certified public accountant. That profile also lists the companies recorded here under notableInvestments; it introduces them with the wording 主な投資先か支援先 (main investees or supported companies), so it does not distinguish an investment he led from a company he supported, and no board appointment is stated for any of them.
