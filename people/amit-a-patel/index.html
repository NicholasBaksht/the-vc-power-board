Biography

Amit A. Patel is Co-founder and Managing Partner of Owl Ventures. He began his career in education as founder of Personal Academic Trainers, a tutoring company, then helped build quality assurance and sales training programs at Mathnasium Learning Centers and served as Director of Technology at Success Academy Charter Schools in New York City. At Owl his investments include Codecademy, acquired by Skillsoft, WhiteHat Jr, acquired by Byju's, and current portfolio companies such as Apna, Panorama Education, and SplashLearn. He serves on the national board of the college affordability nonprofit uAspire. He holds a B.A. in Mathematical Economic Analysis from Rice University and a joint M.B.A. and M.A. in Education from Stanford's Graduate School of Business and Graduate School of Education.
