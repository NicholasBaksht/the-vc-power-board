Biography

Jonathan Becker is a General Partner at Headline, based in the firm's Berlin office, and joined in 2011. He is a computer scientist who moved into venture capital, having held several engineering roles and sold websites while still in school. He focuses on SaaS, application software, mobile, infrastructure and energy transition. His portfolio includes Staffbase, Graylog, Podimo, Monta, Gorilla, Aptoide and Blinkist (acquired by Go1). He says he works for founders rather than the other way around, and looks for performance, ethics, clarity of thought and ambition rather than traditional pedigree.
