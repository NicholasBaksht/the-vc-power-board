Biography

Elaine Weavers-Wright is a Co-Founder of Haatch. Her Haatch biography states her role "has evolved to support the wider team, focusing on the overall health and smooth running of Haatch as a business", drawing on a commercial and customer-experience background rather than an investing one. Before Haatch she was a founding partner at Kiddicare, directing buying, product sourcing, product design and customer service. She holds a founder title rather than a partner title and Haatch does not describe her as making investment decisions. No education, join year, board seat, IPO count or exit count could be sourced, and no investment is attributed to her by any source found.

Previous Experience

  • Kiddicare - founding partner; her Haatch biography states she was "a founding partner involved in directing all parts of the business with a particular focus on buying, product sourcing, product design, and customer service across thousands of products and millions of orders".
