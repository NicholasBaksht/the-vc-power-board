Biography

Fred Ellis is a Partner at N47 covering Europe, focused on the firm's Applications category and on founders with an obsessive focus on product. His N47 profile describes him as an operator and investor who previously helped build private deal distribution and execution infrastructure for large financial enterprises at a startup, but it does not name the company or his title. His listed portfolio includes Aforza, Encord and Sona. He has published N47 pieces on operational foundations for the frontline economy and on European AI startups. N47 does not publish his education, board seats or the year he joined.
