Biography

Andrew Jenkins is a Partner at Passion Capital and has been venture investing since 2001. In 2006 he led the seed funding for cybersecurity business Mimecast and operated as its lead financier across four funding rounds. In 2017 he founded Conviction VC, an early stage venture fund focused on backing SaaS founders, and he previously set up and ran private client teams at GAM, Barclays and Ansbacher Bahamas.
