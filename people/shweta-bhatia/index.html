Biography

Shweta Bhatia is Senior Partner and Head of India TCF Ventures at Eight Roads. Her profile states she joined the firm in 2018 and leads the Technology, Consumer and Financial Services team in India, focusing on enterprise technology, consumer internet and fintech. Before Eight Roads she spent 12 years at Goldman Sachs across New York and Mumbai, most recently leading Asia investments for the Venture Capital and Growth Equity team, and before that worked at Warburg Pincus on growth-stage financial services and consumer transactions.
