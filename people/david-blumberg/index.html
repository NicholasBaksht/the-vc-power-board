Biography

David Blumberg built a rare combination of operating and international investing experience before founding his own firm - one of the first four senior executives at Check Point Software, followed by international investment roles at T. Rowe Price, Apax Partners, Adler & Co., and the Bronfman Family Office. He founded Blumberg Capital in 1991, building it into an early-stage investor specializing in AI, big data, fintech, and cybersecurity, with a hands-on approach as active board members and advisors from seed through exit. That approach has produced five unicorns and seven IPOs across the firm's history, including DoubleVerify's 2021 NYSE listing, which delivered a reported 98x return after Blumberg's early 2008 investment. Blumberg Capital now manages more than $750 million with offices spanning San Francisco, Tel Aviv, Miami, and New York.
