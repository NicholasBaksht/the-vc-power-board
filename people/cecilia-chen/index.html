Biography

Cecilia Chen is a Partner, Asia at SoftBank Vision Fund. Before joining SoftBank Investment Advisers she was an executive director in Goldman Sachs' Asian Special Situations Group covering growth equity investment in China, and previously worked at UBS and began her career as an investment banking analyst at China International Capital Corporation. She holds a BBA from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and an MBA from the Wharton School.
