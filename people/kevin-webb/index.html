Biography

Kevin Webb is a co-founder and Managing Director of Superorganism, the first venture capital firm dedicated to biodiversity. He brings more than 15 years of venture and angel investing experience and previously helped establish and operate the Webb Investment Network, where he was involved at the early stages with companies that became unicorns, including Ironclad, Turo and Verkada. He studied Human Biology at Stanford University with a focus on humans, environmental change and infectious disease, and holds an MS in Sustainability Science from Columbia University. Before launching Superorganism in 2023 he spent several years angel investing in biodiversity startups to test the thesis, and the firm has pledged 10 percent of its profits to future conservation efforts.
