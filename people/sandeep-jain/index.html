Biography

Sandeep Jain is a Venture Partner at Ananda Impact Ventures, listed on the firm's advisory board section. He holds a bachelor's and a master's degree in computer science from Stanford University, the latter combined with human-computer interaction. He has more than 25 years of Silicon Valley experience spanning desktop systems, supply chain logistics, social media, enterprise mobility, IoT security and AI, mostly at startups, including two self-funded ventures. Most recently he worked at Google in eCommerce on data science and UX experimentation. He grew up in a family of Indian diplomats and was educated in India before Stanford. Ananda publishes no joining year, board seats or portfolio attributions for him.
