Biography

Matt Wichrowski is a partner at Fly Ventures, the Berlin-based seed firm investing in European enterprise and deep tech companies, where he covers the United Kingdom from London. He moved from Chicago to the United Kingdom in 2014 to study for an MBA at London Business School and joined Entrepreneur First in 2016, running its Launch programme in Europe and spending 18 months in Boston leading US investor relations. He confirmed his move to Fly Ventures in December 2019 and started in early 2020. Before joining Fly he angel backed CloudNC.
