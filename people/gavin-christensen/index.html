Biography

Gavin Christensen is Founder and General Partner of Kickstart, which he founded in 2008 and which his Kickstart biography page describes as establishing the region's first seed-stage venture fund. He holds a BA from Brigham Young University and an MBA from the Kellogg School of Management. He is named as an Executive Officer on the Form D filings of Kickstart Seed Fund VI, L.P. (2022) and Kickstart Seed Fund VII, L.P. (2026), and as the Manager of the General Partner on the Form D of K6 Collective Fund, L.P. (2023). NVCA announced on 14 May 2026 that he had been appointed 2026-2027 Chair of its Board of Directors. His Kickstart biography page groups his company involvement under the headings Exits, Board Director and Investor.
