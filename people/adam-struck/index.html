Biography

Adam Struck left a law career at Kirkland & Ellis to co-found a beverage company that went public on NASDAQ, then founded Struck Capital in 2015, becoming an early backer of Postmates, Nutanix, Ramp, and Whatnot.

  • Georgetown University Law Center, J.D.
