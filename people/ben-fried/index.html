Biography

Ben Fried is a Venture Partner at Rally Ventures, announced in the firm's post of 10 August 2023. The Rally team page states that from 2008 to 2022 he worked at Google, where he was Vice President, Chief Information Officer, and New York office Site Lead. Rally's announcement adds that before Google he spent over a decade at Morgan Stanley as a technology platform architect and leader of software development infrastructure and business intelligence teams. Rally's April 2025 Incept AI post identifies him as an Incept AI board member. Rally's Fund V announcement of December 2023 names him as a venture partner addition to the firm.
