Biography

Brian Smith is Partner, CFO and COO at Multicoin Capital, leading finance and operations. He has over 22 years of experience in investing and corporate finance, including roles at Tiger Management, Fox Paine, and Greenhill. He also served on the finance team at Bazaarvoice through its IPO. He serves on the board of the Texas State Teachers Retirement System (TSERS).
