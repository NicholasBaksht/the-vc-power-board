Biography

Carlos Conti, whose full name in Spanish corporate filings is Carlos Conti Walli, is listed as General Partner on Inveready's team page. The CNMV prospectus for Inveready First Capital II, S.C.R., S.A. records him as a vocal on that vehicle's board in his own name rather than as the representative of a company. He is the author of an explanatory article on venture debt published on Inveready's own Medium publication, which associates him with the group's venture debt strategy, although that article is an explainer and does not attribute any specific investment to him. No education, prior employment, portfolio company board seat or attributed investment could be sourced from an acceptable first-party page, because Inveready's own individual biography pages were unreachable throughout this research; those fields are left empty.
