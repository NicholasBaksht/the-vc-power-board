Biography

Haseeb Qureshi is a Managing Partner at Dragonfly, listed with that title on the firm's team page. On his own site he writes 'I'm a managing partner at Dragonfly, a crypto fund' and describes his path into the industry: a sponsored professional poker career with Full Tilt Poker through 2011, returning to university to finish an English and philosophy degree, the App Academy coding bootcamp in San Francisco from April 2015 where he was later promoted to Director of Product, a software engineering role on Airbnb's anti-fraud team which he left in June 2017, and then work for Balaji Srinivasan at Earn.com, which was acquired by Coinbase. He writes that Naval Ravikant persuaded him to join MetaStable as a general partner, which is how he started as an investor. Fortune reports he joined Dragonfly in 2019. He is named in the Form D of Dragonfly Ventures IV, L.P. as a manager of the fund's general partner. His own site does not name the university at which he took his degree, so no institution is recorded here. Business Wire's 11 June 2026 release on Avalanche Treasury Corp lists him within a 'board and advisory group'; he has filed no Section 16 ownership form for the issuer, so no board seat is recorded.
