Biography

Logan Langberg is a Partner at Imaginary Ventures, the consumer focused venture firm founded by Natalie Massenet and Nick Brown, where he invests in early stage consumer companies from seed through Series B. He was promoted to Partner in April 2022 alongside the announcement of the firm's $500 million third fund. He began his career in consumer and retail investment banking and then joined Alliance Consumer Growth, where he worked on investments in restaurant and digitally native consumer brands such as Shake Shack, Blaze Pizza, OUAI Haircare, and Harry's, before joining Imaginary to help build the firm with its founders.
