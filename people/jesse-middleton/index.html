Biography

Jesse Middleton is a General Partner at Flybridge, focused on the future of work, native AI applications and serial entrepreneurs. He joined the firm in June 2016 after co-founding WeWork Labs, where he helped scale WeWork's operations across multiple continents. Before joining Flybridge he spent over a year as an angel investor. At Flybridge he has led investments in Arcee, CarEdge, Chief, HiFi (acquired by Square), Imperfect Foods (acquired by Misfits Market), Splice and Teal.
