Biography

Olivier Tonneau is a Partner at Quantonation, where he invests in quantum computing, quantum sensing, and deep physics companies across Europe and North America. A graduate of ESSEC Business School, he spent a decade as a management consultant at Kearney before serving as CEO of Gravitation, the investment holding company of entrepreneur Charles Beigbeder. He serves on the boards of Quantonation portfolio companies including Multiverse Computing, Qnami, KETS Quantum Security, EvolutionQ, QC Design, and Kipu Quantum, and he is a member of Class 28 of the Kauffman Fellows program.
