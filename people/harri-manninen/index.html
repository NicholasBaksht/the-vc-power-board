Harri Manninen

Founding Partner at Play Ventures · Joined 2018

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Harri Manninen is a Founding Partner of Play Ventures and is based in Helsinki. His Play Ventures team page states he has over 18 years of experience in gaming, that he previously co-founded Rocket Pack, which was acquired by Disney in 2011, and that he co-founded the influencer marketing platform Matchmade, also known as Shark Punch. The page states his angel portfolio includes Mighty Bear Games, Starberry Games, Matchmade, Vizor Bloom, FitXR and Transfluent, and that he built the Nordic XR Startups pre-seed accelerator in Helsinki with gumi and Nordisk Film, investing in ten early-stage Nordic VR and AR startups. He is the Play Ventures partner most frequently quoted in the firm's investment announcements, appearing by name on Cypher Games, Metica, Social First and Kek Entertainment. No current board seat could be sourced; none of those announcements states a board appointment.
