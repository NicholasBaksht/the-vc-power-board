Brian Matthews

Biography

Brian Matthews is a cofounder and General Partner of Cultivation Capital, where his firm profile says he works with software and geospatial technology founders. He spent 12 years at McDonnell Douglas before beginning an entrepreneurial career in 1993. He founded River City Internet Group and cofounded Primary Network (sold to Mpower Communications in 2000), Primary Webworks (sold to Perficient in 2001), CDM Fantasy Sports (sold to Liberty Media in 2006) and IntraISP (sold to Clearwire Communications in 2007). He is also a cofounder of the SixThirty FinTech Accelerator. He holds a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Missouri University of Science and Technology. His firm profile lists board seats at Perficient, SimpleRose, Noonlight, S4, LockerDome, BenchmarkONE (Hatchbuck), Tunespeak and Aisle411, plus the non-profit boards of TechSTL and Friends of T-REX. SEC Form D filings name him as a managing director or manager of the general partners of the firm's Tech Fund IV, Geospatial Tech Fund I, Seed Fund III and Cultivation Twain Seed Fund II, and as a manager of Missouri Founders Technology Fund, LLC in 2012.
