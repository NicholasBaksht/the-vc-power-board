Biography

Jake Brukhman is listed on the CoinFund team page as Founder, CEO, and Managing Partner. CoinFund's homepage attributes a 2015 statement to "Jake Brukhman, CoinFund Founder, 2015" and says the firm started in 2015 as a Slack channel among private investors. He is quoted as CEO in CoinFund's September 2021 announcement of David Pakman's appointment and as CEO and Co-Founder in the July 2023 announcement of the $158M Seed IV close. On CoinFund's author index he is credited on posts covering open neural networks and the intersection of AI and web3, and he co-authored the firm's announcement of its investment in Pluralis. No education or pre-CoinFund employer could be sourced from CoinFund or another acceptable source in this session.
