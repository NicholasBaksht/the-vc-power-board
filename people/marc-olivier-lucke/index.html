Biography

Marc-Olivier Lücke is co-founder of Atlantic and is named in the firm's Impressum as a managing director of Atlantic Labs Manager GmbH. Atlantic's approach page states he joined Christophe Maire to establish Atlantic.vc as an institutional fund manager in 2016 and describes his background as investment banking and finance, without naming employers. He appears under 'Investors' on the firm's team page. The firm publishes no individual title, education or board seats for him. The approach page spells his surname 'Luecke' while the team page and Impressum use 'Lücke'; the umlaut spelling is used here.
