Biography

Dr. Mingu Lee is Managing Partner at Cleveland Avenue, where his firm bio says he launched and leads the CA Tech Fund, a venture fund focused on AI, robotics and digital experience. Before Cleveland Avenue he was Chief Business Officer and Senior Vice President at Samsung SDS America, leading U.S. business across enterprise mobility, digital signage, blockchain, AI, high-performance computing, smart factory solutions, IoT and 5G infrastructure. Earlier he was Chief Technology Officer for Environmental and Technology Services at Lockheed Martin and held senior engineering and project leadership roles at Northrop Grumman supporting enterprise systems integration for the U.S. Department of Defense. He is Global Governing Body Chair of XSamsung, the Samsung Global Alumni Network. His firm bio says he "serves as a board member or board observer" of Bear Robotics, Aigen, NeuReality, Believer and bemyfriends, and has served on the boards of SparkCharge and Better Foods; because the bio does not distinguish which of those are full board seats and no portfolio-company filing confirming any of them was read, boardSeats is left empty. He holds a Ph.D. in Systems Engineering from George Washington University, an M.S. in Information and Telecommunication Systems from Johns Hopkins University, a B.S. in Business Administration from The Ohio State University, and is a graduate of the Advanced Management Program at Harvard Business School.
