Biography

Luben Pampoulov is a Partner at GSV Ventures and a co-founder and Partner of GSV Asset Management, with experience as a growth investor in consumer technology and education technology. At GSV he led early investments in companies including Coursera, Photomath, Spotify, Lyft, Snap, and Dropbox. He serves on the boards of Simplilearn and the ATP Tour, and began his finance career as an analyst at ThinkEquity Partners, a San Francisco investment bank. Before investing he was a professional tennis player on the ATP Tour and captained the UCLA tennis team that won the 2005 NCAA Championship. He holds a BA in Economics and International Studies from UCLA.
