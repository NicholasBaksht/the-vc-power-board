Biography

Takahiro Suzuki is General Partner for Southeast Asia at Genesia Ventures, Inc., which he joined in September 2018. His Genesia Ventures profile page states he joined CyberAgent, Inc. in April 2007, took part in founding the influencer marketing subsidiary CyberBuzz while still a student, later worked in the group's gaming business and managed mobile social applications at the subsidiary CyberX, then joined CyberAgent Ventures (now CyberAgent Capital) in June 2011 and became its Indonesia office representative in October 2011, overseeing Southeast Asia investment operations. The same page names Tokopedia (Indonesia) and CodaPayments (Singapore) among the companies he invested in during that period at CyberAgent Ventures. He studied at Waseda University's Faculty of Sport Sciences. At Genesia Ventures he is named as the responsible capitalist (担当キャピタリスト) on the firm's announcements of Manabie, BuyMed, Vietcetera and Runchise, and is described as lead investor in KAMEREO's 2018 seed round. The firm's Fund IV press release of 27 May 2026 gives his title as 海外投資責任者／General Partner. No current board seat could be sourced.
