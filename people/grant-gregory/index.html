Biography

Grant Gregory is a Partner at Cantos, based in the firm's Los Angeles office. He joined in 2024 from Andreessen Horowitz's American Dynamism team, where he led the firm's investments in Castelion and Salient Motion. He is a Board Director at Shinkei and at The Lumber Manufactory. Before a16z he was a Susa Fellow and worked on MetLife's Private Capital team.
