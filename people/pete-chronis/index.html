Biography

Pete Chronis is listed by Cyberstarts as Operating Partner at the cybersecurity focused venture firm founded by Gili Raanan. Cyberstarts states that he has mentored its founders for nearly a decade as they developed products, refined go-to-market execution and scaled cybersecurity technologies, and that he currently serves on the board of directors at Cyera. Before Cyberstarts he held CISO roles at Paramount, WarnerMedia, Turner Broadcasting and EarthLink. He holds a master's degree from Georgia State University in Atlanta and is the author of The Cyber Conundrum and Hidden Lessons from History and co-author of Modern DSPM for Dummies.
