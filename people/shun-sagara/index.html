Biography

Shun Sagara is Country Director of India at Genesia Ventures, Inc., which he joined in February 2019. His Genesia Ventures profile page states that while still at university he joined the US customer data platform company Treasure Data, was the first inside sales hire at its Japanese entity and helped build its sales organisation, then worked in direct enterprise sales and oversight of upselling to existing customers. He is a graduate of Keio University's Faculty of Commerce. He relocated to Bangalore in July 2023 to launch the firm's India investment operations, and Genesia Ventures' press release states he is the representative of Genesiaventures India Private Limited, the subsidiary established in Bengaluru in November 2023. No individual portfolio company is attributed to him on the Genesia Ventures site, and no current board seat could be sourced.
