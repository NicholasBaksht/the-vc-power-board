Biography

Ludovic Denis is a Venture Partner at Seventure Partners based in Paris, France, and is described by the firm as a fintech expert. His Seventure bio says he joined the firm in November 2015 to strengthen the Digital Technologies team in the sourcing of, investment into and support of innovative start-ups, particularly in fintech. He built his career developing banking and financial software, beginning at Segin (now Atos Worldline) in electronic banking. He founded LineDataCoref, a risk and marketing software business acquired by the Experian Group, where he served as European President until 2008. He then launched Monext, a card transaction processing platform with Payline for e-commerce, which was sold to Credit Mutuel Arkea, and afterwards established Samarkand, a private investment vehicle supporting entrepreneurs in French and international markets. His bio names Orchestra (travel agency SaaS), Web Geo Services (web-to-store), Fiduceo (personal finance management, acquired by Boursorama in 2015) and SnapElite (mobile payment) as part of that personal portfolio, which pre-dates his Seventure role. He holds a PhD in Management Science from Paris Dauphine.
