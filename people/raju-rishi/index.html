Biography

Raju Rishi is a General Partner at RRE Ventures, which he joined in 2015. Before RRE he was a Venture Partner at Sigma Prime Ventures in Boston from 2012 to 2015, founded several venture-backed companies, and held senior roles at AT&T, Lucent Technologies and Bell Laboratories. He has served on the boards of more than 30 companies, among them Bitly, Sailthru, Hyla, Latch and Tive, and served as lead independent director of Latch, Inc. He holds B.S. and M.S. degrees in Materials Science and Engineering from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
