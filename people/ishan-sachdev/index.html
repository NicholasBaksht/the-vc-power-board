Biography

Ishan Sachdev is a General Partner at Deciens Capital. The firm states he is involved in everything from fund operations to identifying, growing and supporting fintech companies including beatBread, Pippin and Tint. Before Deciens he was a growth equity investor and an early member of the portfolio operations team at PSG Equity, and previously a venture capital investor at Bain Capital Ventures, a proprietary trader at Goldman Sachs, and a co-founder of MIT Alumni Angels. He also served as an executive at iPay Technologies, a payments company backed by Bain Capital Ventures and Spectrum Equity, where the firm states he played a role in its acquisition by Jack Henry & Associates. He earned an M.B.A. with distinction from Harvard Business School, where he also served as a policy advisor in the Office of Capital Markets at the U.S. Department of the Treasury, and holds M.Eng. and S.B. degrees in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from MIT. He is named as a Managing Member of the general partner in the Form D filings of Deciens Capital Fund 2023, L.P. and Deciens Capital Fund 2023-A, L.P. He is based in Boston. Deciens publishes no year for when he joined the firm, and no board seat is stated for him.
