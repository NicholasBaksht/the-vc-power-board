Biography

Tae Hea Nahm is described on Storm Ventures' site as co-founding Managing Director of the firm, an early stage B2B focused venture firm in Palo Alto, and the firm's team listing shows him as Partner. His profile states that he focuses on B2B AI applications and tools and that he co-authored the Survival To Thrival book series, which focuses on building enterprise leaders. Previously he was founding CEO of Airespace, later sold to Cisco for $450m, and a partner at Wilson Sonsini Goodrich & Rosati and Venture Law Group. He was born in Seoul, Korea.
