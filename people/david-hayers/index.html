Biography

David Hayers is Head of Growth Debt at Salica Investments. He is quoted under that title in the 1 October 2025 announcement of the first close of the GBP 150m Salica Growth Debt Fund II and again in UKTN's report of 12 November 2025 that Barclays is a cornerstone investor in the fund. His title is not a Partner title; he is included because he is the named senior decision-maker for one of the firm's distinct mandates.
