Biography

Scott Addonizio is a General Partner of Cultivation Capital. His firm profile records thirty years in the medical device industry with specialisation in balloon, stent and chronic total occlusion devices across coronary and peripheral markets, quality systems and regulatory environments. He is Chief Operating Officer of MedAlliance LLC and a board member of Simpson Intervention, and was previously Chief Operating Officer and a board director of OrbusNeich Medical. He is the primary patent holder for a stent having dual helical design, and his profile credits him with securing four IDE approvals for the SELUTION drug coated balloon and introducing the first 1.0mm balloon catheter to the US market. He holds a B.S. in Packaging and Materials Engineering from Michigan State University and a Master's in International Business from the University of Miami. The Form D for Cultivation Capital Life Sciences Fund IV, LP names him as a Managing Director of the fund's general partner.
