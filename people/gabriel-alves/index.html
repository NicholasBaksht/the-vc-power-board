Biography

Gabriel Alves is a Partner at Headline, based in Sao Paulo and covering Latin America. He describes his path as an M&A and capital markets lawyer who became an investment banker and then a venture capitalist, having worked through a range of economic cycles. He invests in fintech, ecommerce, SaaS, health and media. His portfolio includes Fiibo, Smart Break, Horuss AI, Trinus Co and Turn2C. He says he looks for founders possessed by fiery ambition with the skill and intelligence to match.
