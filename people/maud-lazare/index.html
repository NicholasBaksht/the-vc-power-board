Biography

Maud Lazare is a Partner with Seroba, the Dublin-based life sciences venture firm, with responsibility for Investor Relations, a role she took up when she joined in 2023. She has a background in private equity and asset management, and was previously a Partner responsible for Investor Relations at the French life science venture firm Truffle Capital. Earlier she worked at the family office Massena Partners and its private markets spin-off Essling Capital, and held business development roles at asset management firms including La Francaise and Richelieu Finance. She holds a Masters in Fundamental Mathematics from Universite Pierre et Marie Curie and a Masters in Modelling, Optimisation, Decision and Organisation from Universite Paris Dauphine.
