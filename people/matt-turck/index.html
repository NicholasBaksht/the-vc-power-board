Biography

Matt Turck is a Partner at FirstMark investing in enterprise software across the US and Europe, with a focus on data, machine learning and AI. Before FirstMark he co-founded TripleHop Technologies, an enterprise AI search startup acquired by Oracle, worked as an executive at Oracle after the acquisition, and was a Managing Director at Bloomberg Ventures. He organizes Data Driven NYC, hosts the MAD Podcast and publishes the annual MAD Landscape market map of the data and AI industry. He graduated from Sciences-Po Paris and holds an LL.M. from Yale Law School.
