Biography

David Neustaedter, Ph.D., is a Venture Partner on Deerfield's Medical Technologies team and joined the firm in 2022. Before Deerfield he spent seven years as a Vice President at Medtronic Ventures and, before Medtronic's merger with Covidien, a further seven years with Covidien including five years as a Vice President of Venture Capital. He has also held positions at Stryker Development, Leerink Swann/MEDACorp Strategy Consulting, Fletcher Spaght and Braun Consulting. He holds a B.Sc. in Cellular, Molecular, and Microbial Biology from the University of Calgary, an M.B.A. from Yale University and a Ph.D. in Molecular Biology from the University of British Columbia. SEC Form D filings name him as a director of Lunair Medical, Inc., Reprieve Cardiovascular, Inc., R3 Vascular Inc. and SpectraWAVE, Inc., all of which appear on Deerfield's portfolio page.
