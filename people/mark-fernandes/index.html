Biography

Mark Fernandes is a Managing Partner at Sierra Ventures, which he joined in 2002 and where he focuses primarily on software. He began his career on the founding team of a display company and then held product roles at Seagate and Cisco. After his MBA he covered the software sector as a Wall Street research analyst at Robertson Stephens and Merrill Lynch. His firm bio names Sourcefire, which went public and was acquired by Cisco for 2.7 billion dollars, among his prior investments.
