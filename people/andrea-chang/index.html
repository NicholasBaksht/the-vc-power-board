Biography

Andrea Chang is listed as Venture Partner on the NGC Ventures team page, where her bio text describes her as 'a partner at NGC, having started off on the investment team'. Before joining NGC she held investment research analyst positions at Goldman Sachs in Singapore and at Trans-Pacific Technology Fund, where the firm says she participated in analysis and investments in consumer, e-commerce, healthcare consumer apps and AI. The page says that in web3 she is interested in infrastructure, DeFi and metaverse projects. She graduated from National Taiwan University with a Bachelor's Degree in Finance and holds an MBA from Berkeley Haas. NGC's research index credits a March 2022 post titled 'Why The Metaverse Matters' to Andrea Zhang; that byline name differs from the team page name and was not treated as a match.
