Biography

Marc Rougier is a Partner at Elaia with more than 30 years of entrepreneurial experience. He began his career as an international business developer at Thales across Australia, Asia, Africa and America, then spent two years in IBM's corporate development division. He founded and ran several companies, including Logic Controller Management, Meiosys (acquired by IBM), Goojet and Scoop.it (acquired by LinkInfluence). He remains active in the startup ecosystem as a mentor and angel investor. Elaia lists his focus as IT infrastructure, big data, social media and SaaS. The firm does not publish his joining year.
