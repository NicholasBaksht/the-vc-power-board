Biography

David Norwood was a director of Oxford Science Enterprises plc from its incorporation as Oxford Sciences plc on 19 June 2014 until 31 January 2019, according to Companies House records. Before that he was a director of IP2IPO Limited from 2000 and of IP Group plc from 2001 to 2008, and held board seats at Oxford Nanopore Technologies plc, Tissue Regenix Group Ltd and Ilika Technologies Ltd, all of which he left at the end of 2008. Wikipedia describes him as the founder of both IP Group and Oxford Sciences Innovation, the company that was renamed Oxford Science Enterprises plc on 30 September 2021. He is not listed on the firm's current team or board page.
