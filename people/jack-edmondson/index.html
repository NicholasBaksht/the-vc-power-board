Biography

Jack Edmondson is Chief Investment Officer of Oxford Science Enterprises, where the firm's own bio says he leads OSE's investment strategy, oversees the investment process and manages all of OSE's investment activity across Life Sciences, Deep Tech and HealthTech. He joined in 2023 with more than 20 years of investment experience, having spent 14 years as Deputy CIO at Oxford University Endowment Management and held earlier roles at Exponent Private Equity, McKinsey and Merrill Lynch. He holds an MBA from the University of Oxford and a BA from Durham University and is a CFA Charterholder. Companies House records his appointment as a director of Oxford Science Enterprises plc on 1 September 2023.
