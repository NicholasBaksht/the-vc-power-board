Biography

Heather Wood is a General Partner at Cultivation Capital working with software technology companies. Her firm profile records nearly 20 years of mergers and acquisitions experience at Cequel III, Charter Communications and Monsanto. Most recently she was Senior Vice President of Corporate Development at Cequel III, where her teams acquired and integrated companies in cable television and communications towers, raised debt and equity financing and managed exits including an IPO and private equity sales. She holds an MBA from the Olin School of Business at Washington University. Her published board and advisory service is with non-profit and community organisations rather than portfolio companies, including Great Circle, The College School, the Spirit of St. Louis Women's Fund, the Arch Grants judging committee, the Washington University Olin Alumni Board and the Danforth Plant Science Center leadership committee, so boardSeats is empty of portfolio company seats. The Form D for Cultivation Capital Tech Fund IV, LP names her as a Managing Director of the fund's general partner.
