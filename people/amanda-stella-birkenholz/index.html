Biography

Amanda-Stella Birkenholz is a Principal at UVC Partners focused on early-stage deep tech and climate tech. She holds an industrial engineering degree from RWTH Aachen University and co-founded VISN, an award-winning B2B startup, before moving into venture capital. UVC states she serves as a board member or board observer at several portfolio companies, though it does not name them individually; her portfolio attributions are HQS Quantum Simulations, STABL Energy, DeepDrive, planqc, Molten Industries, Cyclize, Radical Dot, Certivity and Alqem. Her professional experience spans Germany, Australia, the UK, Spain and Thailand. She is also the author of 'CHAMP – Jagd auf den Trojaner', a children's book about entrepreneurship and technology. Her joining year is not published, so it is left null.
