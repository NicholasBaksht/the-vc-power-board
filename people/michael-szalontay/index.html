Biography

Michael Szalontay is Co-founder and Managing Partner of Flashpoint. His firm profile records an advanced BA in Economics with honours from MSIIR, a CFA charter, and a career that ran from Audit Consultant at PricewaterhouseCoopers through Analyst and Associate roles at Renaissance Capital to Vice President and then Director at Renaissance Private Equity / Elbrus Capital. In the firm's August 2022 announcement of the US$105 million VC III close he is styled Co-founder and General Partner, a variant of the title his current profile carries.
