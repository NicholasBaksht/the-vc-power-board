Biography

Gaurav Jain is listed on Afore Capital's own site as co-founder and managing partner of the firm, which invests at pre-seed. He and Anamitra Banerji were named as managing members of the general partner of Afore Capital, L.P. in that fund's Form D filed with the SEC in August 2016. Afore's site describes the firm as a 500 million dollar fund that invests 500 thousand to 2 million dollars or more in pre-traction companies from a San Francisco base.
