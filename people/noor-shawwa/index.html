Biography

Noor Shawwa is listed as a Partner at Global Ventures, appearing in the Partners section of the firm's team page. He founded Endeavor UAE in 2013 and led it as Managing Director, building it into one of the top-performing affiliates in Endeavor's global network, with a portfolio of supported companies including Checkout.com, Careem, Kitopi, Property Finder and Mumzworld. Earlier he was Head of Strategic Initiatives at Bayt.com and Head of Content at Zawya, and he lectured on entrepreneurship and business strategy at Concordia University in Montreal. Global Ventures does not publish an individual biography for him, so his prior roles here are drawn from his published Endeavor UAE profile; his education is not publicly stated and has been left empty.
