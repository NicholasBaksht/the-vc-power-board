Biography

Lucrezia Lucotti is a Partner at 360 Capital, the Milan and Paris based early-stage European venture firm. She joined the firm in 2020 as an analyst covering Italian deal flow after working in strategic consulting, then moved into portfolio management and deal execution across energy transition, circular economy, logistics and B2B and B2C software. She now focuses on early-stage digital investments in Italy and managed the launch of the firm's 360 Digitaly fund, which held a first close of EUR 30 million in October 2024.
