Biography

Ian Chiu is a Managing Partner at Owl Ventures, which he joined in 2018 after roughly a decade at Warburg Pincus, where he led the firm's education sector investing. Earlier he was a technology investor at Silver Lake, a consultant at Bain & Company, and a co-founder and early employee of BigMachines, a SaaS company later acquired by Oracle. At Owl he serves on the boards of Degreed, BenchPrep, LeLe Ketang, Sanjieke, and Abl, and works closely with MasterClass. He holds a B.S. and M.S. in Industrial Engineering from Stanford University and an M.B.A. from Stanford Graduate School of Business, where he was an Arjay Miller Scholar and Arbuckle Leadership Fellow.
