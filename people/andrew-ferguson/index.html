Biography

Andrew Ferguson is a Partner at Maven Capital Partners, based in the Birmingham, London and Nottingham offices. His Maven profile says he joined in September 2003, handles deal origination and execution of management buyout investments across the Midlands and the South of England, and serves on Maven's Investment Committee with oversight of the Maven UK Regional Buyout Fund. Before Maven he directed European operations for Freedom International Inc, a Canadian investment firm, worked at CIBC's investment bank structuring debt and derivative packages for European proprietary investments, and began his banking career at Lloyds TSB in commercial and corporate lending. He holds an MBA with a specialisation in corporate recovery strategies. Maven lists his key sector experience as manufacturing, support services, TMT and financial services, and his geographic focus as UK wide. Maven's own case studies name him as the Partner on GEV Group, which was sold to Bridges Fund Management, and on Cursor Controls.
