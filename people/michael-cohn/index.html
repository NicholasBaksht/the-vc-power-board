Biography

Michael Cohn is Co-Founder and Partner at GSV Ventures, the education and workforce technology venture firm launched in 2016, where he works on fund management, investment sourcing, capital raising, and diligence. He has worked with the GSV platform since 2011, serving as a Director at GSV Advisors from 2011 to 2016, where he managed an angel portfolio and helped scale the ASU GSV Summit into an annual event with thousands of attendees. He serves on the boards of portfolio companies including BEGIN, QuillBot, Lightneer, and Toucan, and holds observer roles at several others. He holds an MBA from Northwestern's Kellogg School of Management and a BBA from the University of Michigan.
