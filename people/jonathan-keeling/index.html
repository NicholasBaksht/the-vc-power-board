Biography

Jonathan Keeling is a Partner at Haatch, where his Haatch biography says he is dedicated to driving fundraising across the firm's multiple funds and seeking new growth opportunities. He was previously Chief Growth Officer of Crowdcube, joining as an early employee and, per his biography, helping scale it to £14 million in annual revenue and 150 staff across the UK and Europe over nearly a decade, working with consumer brands including Monzo, Revolut, Moneybox, Zoe, Brewdog and What3Words. Alongside Haatch he founded edge, a growth consultancy working with consumer startups from Seed to Series A on crowdfunding rounds. He is described as a two-time TEDx speaker, an active early-stage investor and a non-executive director, though no specific company board is named. He is the named Haatch voice on the firm's CybaVerse Series A announcement and on the launch of the Loughborough Venture Investor Network. No education, join year, IPO count or exit count could be sourced. His biography's reference to being a non-executive director does not name a company, so no board seat was recorded.
