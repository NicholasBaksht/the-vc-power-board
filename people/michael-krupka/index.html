Biography

Michael Krupka joined Bain Capital in 1991 and co-founded Bain Capital Venture Partners in 2000, where he was a Managing Director from its founding, according to Vonage proxy statements. His investing focused on application software and SaaS companies across insurance, real estate, travel and entertainment, auto, education and HR. He served on the board of Vonage Holdings Corp from July 2007, becoming its Lead Independent Director in February 2010, and joined the boards of Reonomy and LogicSource in 2015. Bain Capital's site now lists him as a Senior Advisor, and he holds a B.A. in chemistry from Dartmouth College.
