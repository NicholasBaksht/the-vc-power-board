Biography

Josh Chapman is one of the managing partners and founders of Konvoy Ventures, a thesis driven venture capital firm headquartered in Denver, Colorado. Before Konvoy he worked at BlackRock and Morgan Stanley in New York City. He co-founded the firm in 2018 with Jason Chapman and Jackson Vaughan and has been named as the Konvoy partner on the firm's lead investments in Nerd Street and GoSats.
