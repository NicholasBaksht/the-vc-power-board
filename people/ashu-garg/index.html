Biography

Ashu Garg is a General Partner at Foundation Capital, where he works on the firm's Enterprise practice and invests across AI-enabled business applications, AI infrastructure, the data stack, cyber-security and online video. He joined the firm in 2008 after serving as general manager of Microsoft's online-advertising business, and was promoted to general partner in 2011; earlier roles included McKinsey & Company, Unilever, Cadence Design Systems and founding the search company TringTring.com. His firm bio identifies him as an early investor in Databricks, Cohesity, Eightfold, Amperity, Turing, Anyscale and Alation, and in companies later acquired including Aggregate Knowledge, Custora, FreeWheel, TubeMogul and Tubi.tv. He holds a bachelor's degree from the Indian Institute of Technology in New Delhi and an MBA from the Indian Institute of Management Bangalore.
