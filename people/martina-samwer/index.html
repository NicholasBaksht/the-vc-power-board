Biography

Martina Samwer is a Venture Partner at Ananda Impact Ventures, listed on the firm's advisory board section. She co-founded 21Dx GmbH and built it into an established healthcare service provider for government authorities. Before that she spent ten years as a manager at Schön Klinik and began her career at Bain & Company. She holds a PhD in Law & Economics from the University of Hamburg and Columbia University in New York and has a background in economics. She advises health and life sciences startups and emphasises culture and people as drivers of sustained growth. Ananda publishes no board seats, portfolio attributions or joining year for her.
