Biography

Darian Shirazi is a General Partner at Gradient Ventures focused on seed-stage AI companies. He founded Radius, a B2B customer data platform acquired by Kabbage, and was an early software engineer at Facebook. He studied computer science and philosophy at UC Berkeley.
