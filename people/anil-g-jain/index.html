Biography

Anil G Jain is listed as Co-Founder on the Venture Catalysts team page, where his name is rendered "Mr. Anil G Jain". Forbes India names him as one of the four co-founders of 9Unicorns, launched in August 2019 alongside Apoorva Ranjan Sharma, Anuj Golecha and Gaurav Jain. Indian corporate filing records for Anil Gulabchand Jain, DIN 00020074, show him as a Designated Partner of Venture Catalysts Accelerator LLP from 27 August 2020, of Venture Catalysts Angel Growth Fund Manager LLP from 20 October 2022 and of 9Unicorns Advisory Services LLP from 27 December 2022, and as a director of Venture Catalysts Proptech Advisors Private Limited from 19 October 2022; that identification rests on a name match plus the fact that the entities are Venture Catalysts group entities, and it should be confirmed before use. He is not among the three current directors of Venture Catalysts Private Limited itself. Venture Catalysts publishes no biography, education or prior employment for him, and none could be verified from an acceptable source, so those fields are empty.
