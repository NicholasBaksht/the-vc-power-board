Biography

Seth Bannon is founding partner of Fifty Years, which he co-founded with Ela Madej in 2016. A Y Combinator alumnus and two-time Forbes 30 Under 30 honoree for social entrepreneurship, he built his career advocating for technology addressing sustainability, food and digital-divide challenges before turning to venture investing to back deep-tech founders tackling civilization-scale problems.
