Biography

Sam Hallene is an Investment Partner at CMT Digital. He was named in the firm's June 2023 announcement as one of the people leading CMT Digital alongside its two co-founders, and Fortune quoted him as investment partner in June 2024 on the firm's plan to raise $150 million for a fourth fund and again in November 2025 on that fund's $136 million close. He is the bylined author of four of CMT Digital's published investment posts. CMT Digital's team page shows him only as 'Sam H.'; the full name is confirmed by Fortune and by the firm's June 2023 announcement. No education or prior employer was found in an acceptable source.
