Biography

Zach Goldstein is a Partner at Operator Partners. His bio page on the firm's site states: "Zach joined Operator Partners in March 2020 from USV, where he was an investor responsible for new thesis development and portfolio company management across areas including fintech, consumer, and enterprise SaaS." The page states he was previously the first business hire at Slice, a New York startup providing pizzerias with online ordering technology, where he was a Product Manager, and before that worked at BlackRock in institutional investment consulting. It states he holds a degree in economics and psychology from Northwestern University and is a CFA Charterholder. The page contains no statement of any current board seat.
