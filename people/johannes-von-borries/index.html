Biography

Johannes von Borries is a Managing Partner of UVC Partners and one of three registered managing directors of Unternehmertum Venture Capital Partners GmbH, appearing in the commercial register from 2015. He holds an industrial engineering degree from TU Berlin and an MBA from U.C. Berkeley, and previously co-founded and exited Nanda Technologies, a semiconductor inspection company. UVC credits him with scaling the firm from a four-person team since 2015 and with expertise in scaling hardware-heavy deep tech and climate tech. His portfolio attributions include KONUX, Isar Aerospace, Lilium, Reverion, STABL Energy, 3YOURMIND, Molten, Metafuels, Patronus and Innoactive. He also serves as a Venture Director at UnternehmerTUM Venture Labs.
