Biography

Ayal Shiran is a General Partner at Viola Growth with more than 20 years of experience in telecommunications and service operations management. He was President of the Customer Business Group at Amdocs (NASDAQ: DOX) and previously a Division President there overseeing the AT&T group of companies. Earlier he was Acting Vice President at TTI Team Telecom International and served as an Israeli Air Force officer developing avionic systems for F-15 fighter jets. His work centers on building high-performance executive teams and handling transactions in the $100-500 million range. His active portfolio includes Optimax, Juganu and Applicaster, with exits including Cyberint and SundaySky. His year of joining Viola is not disclosed on the fund's site.
