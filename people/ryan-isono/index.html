Biography

Ryan Isono is one of the six people listed on the Maverick Ventures investment team page. The firm's own announcement of 28 January 2025 states "Ryan Isono has joined Maverick Ventures as a Managing Director" and that he is based in San Francisco. The same announcement says he "graduated from Harvard College and began his career in investment banking at Credit Suisse" and that he has "invested in all forms of B2B software, most recently as a Partner at Felicis". The announcement names PermitFlow and n8n as companies he worked with at Felicis, not at Maverick, so they are not recorded as Maverick investments here.
