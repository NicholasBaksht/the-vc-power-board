Biography

Scott Casto is a Co-Founder of CMT Digital. CMT Digital's about page states that in 1997 Scott Casto and Jan-Dirk Lueders established the CMT Group, a global electronic trading firm focused on developing low-latency electronic trading infrastructure technologies, and that CMT Digital is an early-stage web3 venture firm with the backing of that group. He is named as an executive officer of CMT Digital Ventures Fund IV LLC in the fund's SEC Form D/A of 6 May 2025. CMT Digital's team page shows him only as 'Scott C.'; the full name is taken from the firm's own about page and from the Form D. No education is published by the firm, and no prior employer other than CMT was found in an acceptable source.
