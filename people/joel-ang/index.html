Biography

Joel Ang joined Wavemaker in 2016 and is now a Principal co-leading the firm's investment team. He focuses on deep tech opportunities that offer what the firm calls a 10x improvement over the status quo, including hardware investments, and his work covers sourcing, running deals end to end and portfolio management. Before Wavemaker he worked on M&A advisory at PwC Singapore and in the finance team at Infocomm Investments, now SG Innovate. He graduated Summa Cum Laude from Singapore Management University with a double degree in Business Management and Accountancy.
