Biography

Hussein Kanji is founding partner of Hoxton Ventures. Educated at Stanford and London Business School, he was on the founding team of a web-design startup acquired by KPMG in 1999, then spent around four years at Microsoft in Seattle before joining Accel Partners in London in 2005. He co-founded Hoxton Ventures with Rob Kniaz in 2013 and appeared on the Forbes Midas List Europe in 2023.
