Biography

Mike Dudas is Managing Partner of 6th Man Ventures, the New York crypto venture firm, and is named by the firm's counsel Lowenstein Sandler as one of its two co-founders alongside Serge Kassardjian. Before 6MV he founded The Block, the crypto news and research outlet, and CoinDesk reported in May 2022 that he had left the role of The Block's chief executive the previous year for the stablecoin firm Paxos. He gave the 6th Man Ventures product keynote at Solana Breakpoint 2025. Delphi Digital's podcast page for its episode with him describes him as Managing Partner at 6th Man Ventures and says he shares 'the inside story of backing Pump.Fun years before it became a cultural phenomenon'. No education record could be sourced from the firm's site, which publishes names and titles only and carries no biographies.
