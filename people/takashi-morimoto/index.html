Biography

Takashi Morimoto is listed as 取締役社長 (Representative Director and President) of MITSUI SUMITOMO INSURANCE Venture Capital on the firm's own company profile page. He signs the firm's president's message, in which he states that the firm's investment areas change with the times but its policy of selecting startups through the three lenses of innovation, uniqueness and foresight does not, and that the firm has extended from IT-driven digital transformation into AI, space technology and Climate Tech. He also signs the firm's notice of its 35th anniversary on 6 December 2025. The firm publishes no biography, education or prior employment for him.
