Ken Shi

Founding Partner at 5Y Capital

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Ken Shi is listed as a Founding Partner of 5Y Capital on the firm's own team page, which shows him in Chinese as 石建明, Founding Partner. 5Y Capital identifies itself on its site as the firm formerly known as Morningside Capital. Under the name Jianming Shi he is named in SEC Form D filings as a director of the general partner entities behind Morningside China TMT Fund II, Fund III and Fund IV between 2011 and 2015. Schedule 13G filings for XPeng Inc. and ATRenew Inc. name him as one of the individuals on the board of TMT General Partner Ltd holding voting and dispositive power over the shares reported.
