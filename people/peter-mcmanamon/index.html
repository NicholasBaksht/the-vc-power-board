Biography

Peter McManamon is Chairman of Atlantic Bridge. His Atlantic Bridge bio states that he co-founded Parthus Technologies plc, now CEVA Inc., in 1993, that he was Chief Financial Officer from 1993 to 2001 and EVP, Corporate Development from January 2001 to October 2002, and that "He is currently Chairman of the board of directors of CEVA Inc., a NASDAQ listed company." That claim was verified independently against CEVA's own board of directors page, which prints his role as "Chairman", so the seat is recorded. The bio further states he was a member of the Board of the National Development Finance Agency from 2003 to 2013 by Irish Government appointment and a member of the Audit Committee at the National Treasury Management Agency, that he is a member of the Governing Board of Technology Ireland, and that he is a Chartered Director and a member of the Institute of Directors in Ireland. No Atlantic Bridge portfolio investment is attributed to him individually by any source read, so notableInvestments is empty.
