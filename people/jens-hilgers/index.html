Biography

Jens Hilgers is Founding General Partner of BITKRAFT Ventures. According to the firm's own team page he founded the esports organisation ESL in 2000 and started BITKRAFT in 2015 after ESL was acquired. BITKRAFT describes itself as having grown from the original esports venture capital fund into an investment platform for gaming, Web3 and immersive technology.
