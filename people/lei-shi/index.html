Biography

Lei Shi is a Senior Partner and Co-Head of Eight Roads Ventures China Healthcare. The firm's profile states he has led investments in Nanos Medical, RedPine, EurekaBio, ingeDx, Precision Robotics and SonoSemi, and that he generated returns from Cytek Biosciences (NASDAQ: CTKB) and MDDF. Listed prior employers are McKinsey & Company and Baxter International.
