Biography

Zach Weinberg is a General Partner at Operator Partners. His bio page on the firm's site states he was "co-founder, president and COO of both Flatiron Health (acquired by Roche in 2018 for $2b) and Invite Media (acquired by Google in 2010 for $81m)." The page lists his areas of operating focus as product management, product marketing and communications, people and recruiting and culture, technical culture, and sales process. It states he studied economics and entrepreneurship at the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania and did not graduate. He signed the SEC Form D/A for Operator Partners LLC filed 13 April 2022. The page contains no statement of any current board seat.
