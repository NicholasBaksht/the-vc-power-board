Biography

Luke Zaientz is an Operating Partner at Rally Ventures, announced in the firm's post of 5 August 2024. The Rally announcement states that in this role he works with portfolio companies on go-to-market, customer success, product prioritisation, new market entry, mergers and acquisitions, and pricing and product marketing. It names his prior roles as founder, COO and CEO of Reigning Champs, co-founder of RMG Networks, and roles at Match.com, where he launched and scaled Match.com Events, as well as BriteVision, i2 Technology, Ryder and UPS, and says he attended Northeastern University and MIT. He is listed as Lucas Zaientz among the related persons of Agent Sport, Inc. in that company's 2025 Form D filings, as an executive officer, director and promoter.
