Biography

Jennifer Savage is a Partner at Illuminate Ventures, where she identifies and leads new investments with a particular emphasis on Pacific Northwest companies. She supports due diligence and provides strategic and operational guidance to portfolio companies, and she manages relationships with Illuminate's B2B enterprise network and Advisory Council. Her bio cites more than 25 years of B2B software experience building new categories, with prior executive roles at PlaceWare, which was acquired by Microsoft, as well as DocuSign, Smartsheet and Flowroute. She holds an MBA from UCLA's Anderson School and a bachelor's degree in computer science from the University of Oregon. She sits on the boards of Hoopla Software and Pyze.
