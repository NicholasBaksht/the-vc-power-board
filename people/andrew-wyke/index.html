Biography

Andrew Paul Dyson Wyke is the longest-serving current officer of the Salica entities on the Companies House register. He was appointed an LLP Designated Member of SALICA INVESTMENTS ADVISORY LLP on 18 September 2017 and a director of SALICA INVESTMENTS LIMITED on 1 December 2017, when both were still named after Hambro Perks. He and the corporate member SALICA INVESTMENTS LIMITED are the only two active designated members of the advisory LLP. Private Equity Wire, reporting the rebrand on 17 June 2024, states that Andrew Wyke currently leads the organisation. The title recorded here is his exact title in the Companies House filing because the firm's own website was unreachable; Wikipedia describes him as the firm's CEO, but per the source hierarchy Wikipedia is not usable as the sole source for a current team title, so that description is recorded as a conflict rather than as his title. Salica's own website (salicainvestments.com) returned HTTP 403 to every page request on 2026-09-07, so no biography, education or joining date could be taken from the firm's own profile page for this person; everything recorded here comes from a public register or a named third-party publication.
