View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Patricia Kemp co-founded Oak HC/FT in 2014 and served as a Managing Partner of the firm before becoming an Advisor in 2023. She has more than 20 years of experience in fintech investing and previously spent over 13 years in senior management roles at Cendant, formerly CUC International. She led Oak HC/FT investments including Feedzai's $17.5 million Series B in 2015, Kasisto's $17 million Series B in 2017 and Rapyd's $100 million financing announced in 2022, and she joined the Paxos board in 2021. Oak HC/FT states that she currently serves on the boards of Feedzai, Kasisto, Paxos and Rapyd. She holds a Bachelor of Arts degree and an MBA from Stanford University.
