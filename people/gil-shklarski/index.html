Biography

Gil Shklarski is a General Partner at Operator Partners, which his bio page on the firm's site says he joined in 2020. The page states he was previously CTO at Thirty Madison and spent nine years as CTO at Flatiron Health, which he grew to over 350 professionals before its 2018 acquisition by Roche, and that earlier he worked on large scale data systems at Facebook and Microsoft and held R&D leadership positions in Israel's Ministry of Defense. It states he holds a Ph.D. in scientific computing from Tel Aviv University. The page contains no statement of any current board seat.
