Biography

Brian Devaney is a Partner at Underscore VC focusing on AI and enterprise software for legacy business processes. He joined Underscore in 2020 from First Round Capital. Before that, he led GTM for Strava Metro and started in fintech banking. He earned a B.A. from the University of Vermont.
