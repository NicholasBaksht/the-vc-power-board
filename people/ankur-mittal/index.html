Biography

Ankur Mittal is a Co-Founder of Inflection Point Ventures. His IPV profile page states he is a Chartered Accountant from India with an MBA from Georgetown University in Washington D.C., and describes him as an active angel investor with more than 20 investments in the five years before the page was written. The Physis Capital site lists his prior roles as Head of Asia and Middle East for Training The Street, Associate in Investment Banking M&A at Credit Suisse, and Vice President at Citigroup Pension Investments. He is named as a Partner of Physis Capital, the separate $50 million fund run by the IPV founders. His IPV profile page carries the title 'Co-Founder & COO at Inflection Point Ventures', while the firm's about page lists him simply as 'Co-Founder - IPV'; the about page title is used here as the firm's current roster listing.
