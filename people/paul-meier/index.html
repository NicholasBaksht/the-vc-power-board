Biography

Paul Meier is a Partner at Cultivation Capital focused on the firm's software and geospatial technology investment strategies. His firm profile records over fifteen years of experience in company finance, growth and investment, working with early-stage and global public companies across the geospatial, industrial automation, information technology, manufacturing and travel industries. He holds an MBA from The University of Chicago Booth School of Business and a bachelor's degree with dual majors in Finance and Economics from the University of Wisconsin-Madison. No previous employer, board seat or attributed portfolio company is published on his firm profile. He is the only person on the roster whose title is Partner rather than General Partner.
