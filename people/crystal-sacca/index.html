Crystal Sacca

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Crystal Sacca co-founded Lowercarbon Capital, where the firm's own team page lists her title as Founding Partner. She is also a partner at Lowercase Capital, and her Lowercarbon bio states that she helped lead that fund's early investments in Uber and Blue Bottle Coffee. Before venture capital she worked as an advertising creative on campaigns for brands including Audi, Intel and HBO, work that won a Cannes Lion. She studied at Georgetown University's Edmund A. Walsh School of Foreign Service and serves on the National Board of the Smithsonian, the Board of Trustees of Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum, and as a trustee of the United States Olympic & Paralympic Foundation.
