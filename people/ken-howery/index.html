Biography

Ken Howery co-founded PayPal in 1998 and served as its first chief financial officer until the company's sale to eBay in 2002, after which he spent a year at eBay in corporate development and then joined Peter Thiel's Clarium Capital Management. In 2005 he co-founded Founders Fund with Thiel and Luke Nosek, where published profiles credit him with leading the firm's investments in Facebook, Quantcast, Geni and ZocDoc. He holds an economics degree from Stanford University. He has since served in United States government posts, as Ambassador to Sweden from 2019 to 2021 and as Ambassador to the Kingdom of Denmark from November 2025, and he no longer appears on the Founders Fund team page.
