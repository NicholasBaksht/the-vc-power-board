Biography

Benjamin Erhart joined UVC Partners as Partner in August 2017 from High-Tech Gründerfonds, where he was Investment Director and led more than 50 venture capital transactions and sat on the fund's investment board. He holds an industrial engineering degree from the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology. Before UVC he held board positions at Cumulocity GmbH (acquired by Software AG), Synapticon GmbH, Compositence GmbH (acquired by Quingdao GON Holdings), Till GmbH (acquired by Krones AG) and ZIM Plant Technology GmbH (acquired by Yara ASA). At UVC he leads early-stage investments in deep tech and engineering software with a focus on robotics, quantum technologies and climate tech, and his portfolio attributions include DyeMansion, Synera, FAZUA, HQS Quantum Simulations, Tacto, TWAICE, Fruitcore Robotics, planqc and Proxima Fusion. He also drives the firm's internal tech stack and co-leads its value creation work.
