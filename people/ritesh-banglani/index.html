Biography

Ritesh Banglani is a co-founder and partner at Stellaris Venture Partners. He invests in companies solving uniquely Indian problems across fintech, mobility and software. Before Stellaris he was a partner at Helion Venture Partners, where he backed TaxiForSure, LifeCell and TrulyMadly, and earlier worked as a product manager on online and mobile consumer products. He holds a B.Tech from IIT Delhi and an MBA from INSEAD, and has served as adjunct faculty at IIM Bangalore. His Stellaris portfolio includes Propelld, Turno, Kiwi, mfine and Ayu Health.
