Hian Goh

Founding Partner at Openspace Capital · Joined 2014

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Hian Goh co-founded the firm in 2014 alongside Shane Chesson and is based in Singapore. He sits on the investment committees of the Openspace Ventures and Openspace Growth funds and on the Executive Committee. He founded the Asian Food Channel and sold it to Scripps Networks Interactive, now part of Warner Bros. Discovery. Before becoming an entrepreneur he was a technology investment banker at SG Warburg, now UBS, and at Salomon Smith Barney, now Citi. He sits on A*STAR's portfolio management committee, formerly served on the board of the Singapore Science Centre, and is a Kauffman Fellow. He holds an MBA from INSEAD and a law degree from Trinity College, Oxford.
