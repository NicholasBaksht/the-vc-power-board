Biography

Hank Vigil is Founder and Managing Partner of Acequia Capital, which the firm's About page says he founded in 2010. The team page describes him as SVP Strategy & Partnerships at Microsoft, recognised for Microsoft's early investment in Facebook, its partnership with Yahoo! and many other transactions, over 25 years at Microsoft. He appears in SEC filings under his legal name Henry Vigil or Henry P. Vigil: as a director and manager of the manager of Acequia Capital Pinterest LLC in 2012, as a managing member of the ultimate general partner of Acequia Capital Origin SBIC LP in 2025, and as the signatory of the Acequia Capital OCN LP Form D in August 2026. No education, no current board seat, no IPO count and no exit count could be sourced for him.
