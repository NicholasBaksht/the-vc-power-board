Philippe von Klitzing

Partner at Peak Capital · Joined 2023

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Philippe von Klitzing is a Partner at Peak, an early stage venture capital firm headquartered in Amsterdam with offices in Berlin and Stockholm that invests in SaaS, marketplaces and platforms at Pre-Seed and Seed. He joined Peak in January 2023 and covers the DACH market, and has been investing since 2019. He co-founded a self-storage startup in France that served over 300 customers and was sold to a competitor in 2017, and from 2019 to 2023 he worked at Join Capital in Berlin on fund administration, brand building and LP fundraising, leading four investments there.

Previous Experience

  • Join Capital, Berlin (2019-2023): fund administration, brand building, LP fundraising, led four investments
  • Co-founder, self-storage startup in France (sold to a competitor in 2017)
  • Consultant to private equity firms and healthcare companies
