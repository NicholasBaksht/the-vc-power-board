Biography

Timothy Stern is a General Partner at Cultivation Capital. He cofounded Lenders One in 2000 and served as its President until its 2010 acquisition by Altisource Portfolio Solutions, where he joined the global leadership team and served as President of the Origination Services business group, growing it to over $100 million in annual revenue with 2,500 employees. He is a cofounder of the mortgage technology companies VidVerify and FinLocker. He attended the University of Wisconsin-Madison. No board seat is published on his firm profile, and VidVerify and FinLocker are companies he cofounded rather than investments attributed to him, so notableInvestments is empty. The Form D for Cultivation Capital Tech Fund IV, LP names him as a Managing Director of the fund's general partner.
