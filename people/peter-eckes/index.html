Biography

Peter Eckes is listed as Venture Partner, Europe, on the SVG Ventures team page and as Senior Advisor on THRIVE's team page. His bio states he is passionate about innovation in agriculture and biotechnology and brings 25 years of global executive experience from BASF SE across agriculture, nutrition, care, specialty chemicals and industrial biotechnology, having spent 32 years at BASF in total with 15 years working internationally. It states he served as Chief Technology Officer of BASF's Agricultural Solutions business, with responsibility for an R&D budget, and was a member of BASF's global Technology Leadership Team. He holds a PhD in Chemistry from the University of Frankfurt, spent two years in post-graduate studies in Biological Chemistry at Harvard University and MIT, and completed executive training at INSEAD, Harvard University and the University of Michigan. No board seat and no individual investment attribution was found for him.
