Biography

Brad Burnham is a partner at Union Square Ventures, which he created with Fred Wilson in 2003, according to his bio on the firm's site. He started working in information technology with AT&T in 1979, spun Echo Logic out of Bell Laboratories in 1989, joined AT&T Ventures in 1993 and co-founded TACODA in 2001. He has written the USV posts announcing the firm's investments in Duolingo, Tucows, Protocol Labs and Simulmedia. He majored in political science at Wesleyan University and lives in New York City.
