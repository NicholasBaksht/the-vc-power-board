Biography

Darren Bechtel is the Founder and Managing Director of Brick & Mortar Ventures, a San Francisco venture capital firm founded in 2015 that invests exclusively in early stage technology for the architecture, engineering, and construction industry. A fifth generation member of the family behind Bechtel Group, he built the firm after making roughly 40 angel investments in construction technology startups, including early checks into PlanGrid and BuildingConnected, both later acquired by Autodesk. In 2019 the firm closed a $97.2 million fund backed by industry investors such as Autodesk, CEMEX, and Ferguson Ventures. He holds a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering/Product Design and an MBA from Stanford, and serves on several industry advisory and leadership boards.
