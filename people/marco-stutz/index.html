Biography

Marco Stutz is Partner Switzerland at Redstone. His Redstone profile page states that he joined in 2021, has more than a decade of venture capital experience across more than fifty investments, and executes investments end to end including strategy, sourcing and post-closing portfolio management, supporting European and international investments as part of the Swiss team. Redstone's own September 2023 announcement of the Redstone Quantum Fund names him as the person leading that fund and describes him as having 14 or more years of experience and 60 or more investments, a count that differs from the 50-plus figure on his current profile page. No degree, board seat or individually attributed portfolio company is published for him.
