Biography

Bruno Rivet is a Senior Partner in Seventure Partners' Digital Technologies/Sport team. His Seventure bio says he joined the firm in 1999 as a Venture Capital Investor in the Digital Technologies team and has since made numerous investments, follow-ons and divestments, particularly in software and internet. It states he has worked for Seventure Partners on the boards of directors of Calendra (sold to BMC Software), Glowria (sold to Netgem) and Microwave Vision (introduced on Alternext); all three are described in the past tense in connection with completed exits or listings, so none is recorded as a current board seat. He began his career as a credit analyst at Banque Populaire du Centre and then as an internal auditor at Spef Technology. He holds a Master's degree in Applied Economics from Paris Dauphine University and a Master's degree in Business Administration from the IAE of Bordeaux University School of Business Management.
