Biography

Walter Baddoo is co-founder and managing partner of 4DX Ventures. Born and raised in Accra, Ghana, he holds a BA in international relations from Tufts and studied art history at the Sorbonne. Before founding 4DX he spent roughly a decade in Morgan Stanley's Institutional Equity Group prime brokerage division across New York and San Francisco. He serves on the boards of mPharma and Tizeti.
