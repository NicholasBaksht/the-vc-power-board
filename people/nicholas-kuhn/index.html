Biography

Nicholas Kuhn is Vice President of Hardtech at Elevate Ventures, one of the firm's sector-specific senior investment roles. His team-member page states he graduated from Purdue University with a degree in Construction Management, worked in the commercial and real estate industry and obtained an MBA from Loyola University - Chicago. It says he founded a real estate management firm that he grew over seven years to an exit, and that his other entrepreneurial activity includes ventures in hospitality and e-commerce plus consulting across industries in product development, process management, strategy and marketing. No individual company has been tied to him by a first-party source, so notableInvestments is empty.
