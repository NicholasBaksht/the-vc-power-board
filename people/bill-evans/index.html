Biography

Bill Evans is Founder and General Partner of Rock Health Capital, a seed and early stage venture fund investing in entrepreneurs working at the intersection of healthcare and technology. He joined Rock Health in 2016 as CEO and Managing Director, and in 2022 transitioned to lead Rock Health Capital full time while remaining Chairman of the board of Rock Health Advisory, which he founded. Before Rock Health, he was Senior Director at Roche Diagnostics, where he launched the Navify oncology decision support unit, and spent more than a decade at Genentech, most recently as head of Health Technology Labs. He began his career as a programmer analyst at Goldman Sachs before working as a software engineer in Silicon Valley. He holds a BA in economics from Harvard University.
