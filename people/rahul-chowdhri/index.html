Biography

Rahul Chowdhri is a co-founder and partner at Stellaris Venture Partners, focusing on commerce, consumer tech and deeptech. Before Stellaris he was a partner at Helion Venture Partners, where his investments included BigBasket, Simplilearn, Livspace and iD Fresh. Earlier he worked in product management at Microsoft and in consulting at BCG, MarketRx and i2 Technologies. He studied at IIT Kanpur and IIM Calcutta. He was an early backer of Mamaearth, which listed as Honasa Consumer in 2023, and his portfolio also includes Nestasia, Zouk, Lumio and Dashverse.
