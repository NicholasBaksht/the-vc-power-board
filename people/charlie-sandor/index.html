Biography

Charlie Sandor is an Investment Partner at CMT Digital. The Block's profile of him states that he began his career at Bain & Company, working on large-scale transformations and in the Private Equity Group, that he joined CMT Digital in April 2021 at the launch of Fund II to concentrate on venture investments, and that he holds an A.B. in Economics from Harvard University. CMT Digital's team page shows him only as 'Charlie S.'; the full name and background come from The Block, since the firm publishes no bio. No CMT Digital investment announcement read for this record carries his byline, so no notable investments are attributed to him.
