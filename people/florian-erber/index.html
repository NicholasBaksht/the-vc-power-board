Biography

Florian Erber is General Partner and co-founder of Ananda Impact Ventures and a registered managing director of Ananda Ventures GmbH in Munich. He trained in electrical engineering and has more than 25 years in business, previously working as a venture capitalist at Wellington Partners, as Managing Director EMEA for WhereNet (acquired by Zebra Technologies) and as CFO of the Munich software startup Aloqa (sold to Motorola Mobility). At Ananda he oversees investment strategy, transaction structuring and fund finance. His portfolio attributions include AIRMO, Decade, EcoG, IESO Digital Health, Jua, OroraTech and Pulsetrain, and his stated interests span space tech and planetary intelligence, AI for global challenges, education and environmental solutions. He was the partner quoted on the firm's EUR 50m third fund in October 2018.
