Wang Bo (王博)

Biography

Wang Bo is deputy general manager of Shenzhen Capital Group's Information Technology Investment Department with the title of Executive General Manager. The group's team page designates him a group gold medal investor, states that he holds a bachelor of engineering and a master of business administration and joined the group in 2012, that he was an early practitioner in China's semiconductor advanced packaging field and entered the venture capital industry in 2008 with fifteen years in it, and that since joining the group he has focused on hard technology and led or participated in more than fifty rounds across more than forty companies. It records his inclusion in the Zhongguancun Equity Investment Association's 2017 list of young investors with the greatest development potential in China's hard technology field and CVInfo's 2022 supernova investor list. The page names none of the companies, so none are recorded here.
