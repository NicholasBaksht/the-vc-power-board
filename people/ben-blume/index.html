Biography

Ben Blume exemplifies Atomico's newer applied-AI posture, having led the firm's Series A investment in Spacemaker before later backing Onna and Automation Hero and being promoted to Partner in 2021. His focus on automation in core enterprise functions and real-world verticals reflects exactly where European technical depth can translate into durable software moats - an area of the portfolio that has grown alongside Atomico's original consumer and enterprise scale-up thesis.
