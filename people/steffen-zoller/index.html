Biography

Steffen Zoller is a Venture Partner at Ananda Impact Ventures, listed on the firm's advisory board section. He is an entrepreneur and investor who founded several digital ventures, including the care services marketplace Betreut.de, which was acquired by Care.com. He currently serves as CEO of DCI Digital Career Institute and is an investor and advisor to companies including voiio and Media4Care, where he is chairman. He holds a master's degree from HHL Leipzig Graduate School of Management and a PhD from Universität Witten/Herdecke. Ananda publishes no joining year or portfolio attributions for him. His profile page is reachable at ananda.vc/team/dr-steffen-zoller rather than the name-only slug used for other team members.
