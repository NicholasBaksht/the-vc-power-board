Biography

Jerry Murdock co-founded Insight Venture Partners with Jeff Horing in 1995 and was a Managing Director of the firm until April 2011, according to Insight Partners' own team page. Before Insight he founded Aspen Technology Group, which he ran from 1988 to 1995. Forbes credits him with investments including Twitter, Nest, Docker, Flipboard, Hortonworks, Truecaller, Klout, Path and AdColony, and ranked him 88th on its 2014 Midas List. He is a trustee of the Santa Fe Institute, the Aspen Center for Environmental Studies and the Aspen Institute.
