Biography

Shripati Acharya is Founder and Managing Partner at Prime Venture Partners, an early stage firm in Bangalore. Prime's own bio for him lists a business lead role at Cisco, the co-founding of Snapfish, which was acquired by HP, and time at Transarc, which was acquired by IBM. The same bio says he worked with Nandan Nilekani on the UIDAI project and interned for Boston Consulting Group in India. In June 2025 he co-authored Prime's announcement of a $2.5 million investment in Illumine, a vertical SaaS platform for preschools and childcare centres.
