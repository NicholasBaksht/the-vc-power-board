Biography

John Chui is an Investor at Openspace and a member of the Openspace Growth investment team in Singapore, focused on growth-stage opportunities. He previously worked at the M&A advisory firm BDA Partners. He holds a Bachelor of Business Administration with Honours from the National University of Singapore. The firm does not publish his joining year, board seats or individual investments.
