Biography

Tijl Hoefnagels is a Partner at Rubio Impact Ventures, where he leads agrifood and food-system investments. Rubio's own impact report describes him as a creative investment professional who had been investing at several stages of the food chain in Europe and South America for more than ten years before joining; the firm does not name his earlier employers, so they are not recorded here. At Rubio he has been the public face of investments in NoPalm Ingredients and Novameat and of the firm's agrifood partnership work. Rubio's current team page lists him as Partner; an earlier version of the firm's team listing gave him the title Venture Partner. His education is not published by the firm and is left blank rather than inferred.
