Biography

Harj Taggar co-founded Initialized Capital and is now a Managing Partner at Y Combinator. He was previously founder and CEO of Triplebyte and of Auctomatic, which was acquired by Live Current Media in 2008, and he graduated from Oxford in 2006 where he studied Jurisprudence. He first joined Y Combinator as a partner in 2010, left in 2014 to start Triplebyte, and rejoined in 2020. His own site lists Coinbase, Instacart, Rippling, GOAT, Flexport and Opendoor among the companies he invested in through Initialized.
