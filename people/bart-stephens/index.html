Biography

Bart Stephens is Founder and Managing Partner of Blockchain Capital, which his firm bio dates to an inception in 2013 and describes as having invested in more than 100 companies and crypto-assets. He is a three-time founder whose earlier career included corporate development and advanced products roles at E*TRADE, co-founding Oncology.com, serving as Executive Vice President of Venture Capital at Ivanhoe Capital Corporation, and founding Stephens Investment Management. He holds a B.A. in Political Science from Princeton University. Blockchain Capital's portfolio page carries a testimonial from Anchorage founder Diogo Monica describing him as an investor and board member.
