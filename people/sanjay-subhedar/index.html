Biography

Sanjay Subhedar is described on Storm Ventures' site as a founding Managing Director Emeritus of the firm, and the firm's team page lists him as Partner Emeritus. His profile states a focus on mobile and SaaS and lists his exits as AdMobius, Catamaran, iML, Lightera, Netscaler, Risesmart, SMI and Telera, with no active investments shown. He holds a B.S. from the University of Bombay and an M.B.A. from Indiana University, and his profile lists board roles with the Palo Alto Medical Foundation, the Indian School of Business and Filoli. He was born in Mumbai, India.
