Biography

Ryan Moore is a co-founder and Founding Partner of Accomplice and the founding investor in DraftKings, Plastiq and Unsplash. He was DraftKings' only institutional backer until November 2013 and helped arrange the company's 2012 partnership with Major League Baseball before it went public in 2020. He joined Atlas Venture in September 2011 after 12 years as a general partner at GrandBanks Capital. He sits on the boards of DraftKings, Workhuman, Caribou, Plastiq and Omaha National, and previously backed Skillz from its Series B through its 2020 public listing.
