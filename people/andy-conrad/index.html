Biography

Andy Conrad is a General Partner at S32, the venture capital firm founded by Bill Maris; the firm's own team page lists him as Andrew Conrad, PhD. He founded Verily Life Sciences, previously named Google Life Sciences, and served as its CEO and Executive Chairman, and S32's appointment release says he held positions at GV and X and continues to serve as an advisor to Google. Before Google he was Chief Scientific Officer of Laboratory Corporation of America and co-founded the National Genetics Institute, which was acquired by LabCorp. S32 announced his appointment as General Partner on 22 February 2024, and he earned a PhD from the University of California, Los Angeles.
