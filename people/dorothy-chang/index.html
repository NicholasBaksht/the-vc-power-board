Biography

Dorothy Chang is a Venture Partner at Flybridge, investing in pre-seed and seed stage AI startups, and is a partner on Flybridge's pre-seed vehicle Next Wave NYC. She told Tech:NYC that she joined Flybridge full time at the end of 2024, having begun her career at a technology public relations agency representing IBM and then held roles in communications at Foursquare and marketing at Paxos. She co-founded Liberty City Ventures, was a communications partner at Primary Venture Partners, served as President of the coding nonprofit Kode With Klossy, and co-founded Lynx Collective in New York.
