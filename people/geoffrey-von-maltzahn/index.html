Biography

Geoffrey von Maltzahn is a General Partner at Flagship Pioneering, which he joined in 2009, and Flagship describes him as an inventor, entrepreneur, CEO and co-founder of multiple companies that integrate biology and data science. Through Flagship Labs he co-founded Lila Sciences, Quotient Therapeutics, Tessera Therapeutics, Generate:Biomedicines, Indigo Agriculture, Sana Biotechnology and Seres Therapeutics. He is currently chief executive officer and a board member of Lila Sciences, board chair of Tessera Therapeutics, and chief innovation officer and a director of Indigo Agriculture. He holds a Ph.D. in biomedical engineering and medical physics from MIT and previously served as chief technology officer of Seres Therapeutics.
