Biography

Dr. Bilal Baloch is a Partner at Shorooq Partners, where he leads the firm's AI investments and its value creation practice. He was co-founder and co-CEO of Enquire AI, which was acquired by Uzabase. He has spent over 15 years working at the intersection of technology, consulting and research, and is the author of When Ideas Matter, published by Cambridge University Press in 2021. He holds a doctorate from the University of Oxford, a master's from the Fletcher School at Tufts University and a bachelor's from the London School of Economics.
