Biography

Jeff Hinck is a Managing Director and co-founder of Rally Ventures, which he co-founded in 2012. His Rally bio states he has been an entrepreneur, executive and venture capitalist for 30 years and has been a founder or co-founder of more than ten businesses across AI, insurtech, payments technology, sportstech, fintech, cybersecurity, human security and specialty finance. He began his professional career at McKinsey & Company and was CEO of Sistina Software, which Red Hat acquired in 2003. He received a JD from Harvard Law School and a BA from Northwestern University, and his bio states he has twice served as President of the MVCA. He is named as a Managing Director of the general partner of Rally Ventures Fund II and Fund IV in those funds' Form D filings.
