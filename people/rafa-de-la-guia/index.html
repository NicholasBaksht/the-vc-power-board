Biography

Rafa de la Guia is a Partner at Quona Capital focused on sourcing, executing and managing investments in Latin America. He joined Quona in 2019 as a Principal and was promoted to Partner in 2022. Quona says he brings 15 years of experience across venture capital, impact investing, private equity and investment banking. Before Quona he spent six years at Omidyar Network investing in education, emerging technologies and financial inclusion in Latin America and the US, and he also worked at Acumen Fund, Adobe Ventures and the European subordinated debt manager MezzVest. He began his career at UBS Investment Bank in London. He holds an MBA from Berkeley Haas and honors degrees in law and business administration from ICADE in Madrid.
