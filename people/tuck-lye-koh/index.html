Biography

Tuck Lye Koh, known in Chinese as Xu Dalai, is Founding Partner and CEO of Shunwei Capital in Beijing, which he co-founded with Lei Jun. The firm states he is responsible across its investment and management affairs and leads its work in mobile internet, deep technology, ESG and sustainability, enterprise services, retail and consumer, and biopharmaceuticals. Shunwei names him as an early investor in Kingsoft Software, Xiaomi, NIO, Agora, Kingsoft Office, SouthChip, Momenta and PatSnap, and its team page also cites DXY, Yiqizuoye, 1MORE and Xingda International. He graduated from the National University of Singapore and Stanford University, and previously held management positions at C.V. Starr, GIC, AIG and Deutsche Bank.
