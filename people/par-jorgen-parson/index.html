Biography

Pär-Jörgen Pärson is the canonical franchise investor at Northzone: with the firm since 2004, he was early enough to matter in the Spotify era and remains active in newer waves like climate and AI investing today. That breadth is an important reason Northzone still feels current rather than nostalgic decades into its history. His track record spans Spotify, iZettle, Avito, fuboTV, Spring Health, and Tandem Health, and he continues to serve on the board of fuboTV.
