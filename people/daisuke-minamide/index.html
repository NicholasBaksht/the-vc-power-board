Biography

Daisuke Minamide is Head of International Investment at CyberAgent Capital and representative of its US office. The firm's own profile states that at a major Japanese mobile carrier he worked in the international business and marketing divisions on venture investment and business development, that he handled the investment in Evernote and served as a board observer there, that he joined サイバーエージェント・キャピタル in July 2013, and that from November 2013 he has invested from the Bay Area as US representative. His profile lists REMOTIUM under M&A and Silvair under IPO. He is a graduate of Purdue University's graduate school of management. The Evernote board observer role is stated in the past tense and relates to his previous employer, not to CyberAgent Capital, so no current board seat is recorded for him.
