Biography

Alex Mittal is co-founder and CEO of FundersClub, an online investment platform and venture fund manager based in San Francisco. His FundersClub profile gives his title as "Co-Founder and CEO of FundersClub" and states that before starting FundersClub he was the founding CEO of Innova Dynamics, a venture-backed touchscreen hardware company acquired by TPK Holding (TPE:3673), and the founding CTO of an enterprise software company. It states that he holds degrees in economics and engineering from the Wharton School and Penn Engineering via the University of Pennsylvania's M&T Program, and that he is an alumnus of Y Combinator. The same profile states that he was previously active in science research, has published in peer-reviewed scientific journals, is an inventor on multiple issued patents, and presented at the 2002 Nobel Prize Ceremonies. He is named as "Alexander Mittal", CEO of FundersClub Inc., in the SEC Form D filed by FCVC Fund III L.P. in June 2022, and he authors FundersClub's quarterly portfolio follow-on blog posts. No source read for this record ties him personally to a specific portfolio investment or names him as a director of a portfolio company, so notableInvestments and boardSeats are empty.
