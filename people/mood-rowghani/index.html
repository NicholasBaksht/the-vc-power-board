Biography

Mood Rowghani is a general partner at Bond, which he co-founded in 2019 with Mary Meeker, Noah Knauf and Juliet de Baubigny after the group's Digital Growth team spun out of Kleiner Perkins. He joined Kleiner Perkins in 2011 from Summit Partners, where he had been a vice president, and earlier worked at Highland Capital Partners, Yahoo and McKinsey & Company. His Bond bio states that he focuses on emerging technology companies that are driving innovation at global scale, and lists active investments including Atoms, Canva, Cockroach Labs, Ironclad, Kalshi, Revolut, SpaceX and Whatnot. Bond's site lists his current board seats as Carbon Robotics, inDrive, Partiful, Passes and Substack.
