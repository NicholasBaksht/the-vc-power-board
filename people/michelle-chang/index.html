Biography

Michelle Chang is a Partner at Comcast Ventures, the corporate venture arm of Comcast NBCUniversal. She has co-authored the firm's investment announcements in digital health companies including HealthSnap, Sword Health and Wellth, writing that the firm focuses on digital health and on technology solutions that enable aging in place and remote patient monitoring. In June 2026 she co-announced the firm's participation in the Series A financing of Hydra Host, a GPU infrastructure and orchestration platform.
