Biography

Craig Blair is a co-founder and Partner at Airtree Ventures in Sydney, where the firm's team page gives his full title as Co-founder & Partner, Chair of Investments and records that he joined in 2014, the year Airtree was founded. His firm bio describes him as a former technology founder who raised capital, built teams, shipped products and had exits before becoming a venture investor. The same bio names Canva, Brighte, Pet Circle, Design.com, Pawshake, Chemist2U, Archistar, Go1, Employment Hero, DroneDeploy, Prospa and Fetch as portfolio companies associated with him.
