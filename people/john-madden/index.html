Biography

John Madden is a Non-Executive Director and Company Secretary of Animoca Brands Corporation Limited. The company's own board section states that he has "considerable experience leading work programmes on compliance, due diligence, governance and finance", that he worked at Rio Tinto for 24 years in various disciplines, and that he subsequently worked with small market capitalization companies, listing entities on multiple exchanges. He is a governance officer rather than an investing decision-maker. No education, join year or other current board seats are published.
