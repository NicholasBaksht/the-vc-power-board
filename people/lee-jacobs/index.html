Biography

Lee Jacobs is Co-Founder and Managing Partner of Long Journey Ventures. Before Long Journey he was, per the firm's own team page, one of the first outside investors to complete a syndicate on AngelList, where he later became a Partner. He raised a micro-fund called Edelweiss with Brian Balfour, Elaine Wherry and Jonathan Bruck, and in 2020 renamed that fund Long Journey Ventures, expanding the partnership with Pascal Levy-Garboua and Cyan Banister. He is the named related person on the Forms D for LJ Ventures Fund II, LJV Fund III, Long Journey Fund IV and Long Journey Fund V. He writes most of the firm's published posts, including its accounts of investing in Crusoe, Substrate and TrueMed. The team page states he lives in San Francisco.
