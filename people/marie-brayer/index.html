Marie Brayer

General Partner at Fly Ventures · Joined 2022

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Marie Brayer joined Fly Ventures as a Partner in February 2022, based in Paris, after a five year stint at Serena Capital where she invested in and sat on the boards of more than 10 French B2B startups including Descartes Underwriting, Lifen, Malt and Kili Technology. She had worked with Fly through Serena on the board of Inato before joining. In December 2024 Fly Ventures described itself as operating an equal-partner model of four partners distributed across Berlin, London, Paris and Zurich, with Brayer covering Paris. She is no longer listed on Fly Ventures' team page.
