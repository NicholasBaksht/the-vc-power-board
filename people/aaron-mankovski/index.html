Biography

Aaron Mankovski is listed by Pitango Venture Capital as a Managing Partner on the firm's team page. His firm bio states that he serves on the boards of DriveNets, Riskified, Tailor Brands, Tulip, Silk, Tomorrow.io, Aquant and Tabit, and is an observer at Formlabs, and that he formerly served on the Board of Directors of Chromatis Networks, which was acquired by Lucent for 4.5 billion dollars. He is the Founder and former Chairman of IATI, the Israel Advanced Technology Industries association. A Riskified filing with the SEC records that he tendered his resignation as a director of Riskified effective March 17, 2026, so that seat is no longer current despite still appearing on his firm bio.
