Biography

Tom Ferguson is the founder and Managing Partner of Burnt Island Ventures, a venture firm dedicated to early stage water companies. He holds an MA from the University of Edinburgh and an MBA from Harvard Business School. He served as lead author of the first CDP Water Disclosure Report in 2010, then spent years at the water accelerator Imagine H2O, where he built its programming and supported portfolio companies that went on to raise more than $600 million. He launched Burnt Island Ventures in 2020 and has since closed a $30 million debut fund and a $50 million second fund, both anchored by the water technology company Xylem.
