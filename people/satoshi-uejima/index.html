Biography

Satoshi Uejima is an Executive Director of UntroD Capital Japan. The firm's biography states that he joined in 2022 as Head of Value-Up, responsible for designing and implementing growth support programs for portfolio companies, was appointed Executive Officer in 2023 and Executive Director in 2024. It states that before joining he worked at three early to late stage deep tech startups, Rapyuta Robotics, Synspective and RFLocus, in sales and marketing, management and business development, and that he also has experience in product development at Global Logistic Properties and in insurance sales planning and real estate management at Nippon Life Insurance Company. The firm's biography names those companies as employers, not as investments, and they are recorded here as prior experience only.
