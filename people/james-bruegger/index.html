Biography

James Bruegger is CIO and General Partner at Seraphim Space, and is described on the firm's site as co-founder and CIO of Seraphim Space (Manager) LLP and co-founder of Generation Space LLC, the Seraphim Space Accelerator and the Generation Space Accelerator. Seraphim Space (Manager) LLP is the investment manager for both the private early-stage fund Seraphim Space Venture Fund II and the London Stock Exchange listed Seraphim Space Investment Trust plc, which IPO'd in 2021 and trades under the ticker SSIT. His firm bio states he was an early venture capital investor in Arqit, ICEYE, LeoLabs and D-Orbit and led investments in Spire Global and AST SpaceMobile. He was previously at YFM Equity Partners and Burlington Consultants, and holds a first-class degree in History from University College London.
