Biography

Tomas Roggio is described on his own author page at latitud.com as 'General Partner, Head of Ventures at Latitud', with the line 'Working to transform Latin America's early stage tech ecosystem.' TechCrunch reported on 28 March 2022 that Latitud Ventures was led by Roggio and had by then invested in more than 80 companies. Latitud's own announcement of the Latitud Ventures Fund II first closing quotes him as 'general partner and head of Latitud Ventures', and Forbes Chile named him on 3 April 2024 as one of the firm's three general partners. He is the author of Latitud's published investment notes including 'Why Latitud invested in Darwin AI, bridging the AI gap for LatAm SMBs' and 'The Latitud Ventures First Round Manifesto'. No education or prior employer could be sourced for him from Latitud or another acceptable source.
