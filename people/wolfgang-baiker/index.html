Biography

Dr. Wolfgang Baiker is a Venture Partner at Wellington Partners, an advisory rather than full-time investing role. He holds a doctorate in medicine from the University of Ulm and an MBA from Pace University in New York. He spent more than 30 years at Boehringer Ingelheim, where he was Global Head of Project Management, Global Head of Development, a member of the Board of Managing Directors with global production responsibility, a member of the Executive Management Board from 2013, and President and CEO of Boehringer Ingelheim USA from January 2018. He joined Wellington Partners as a Venture Partner in 2016. Wellington publishes no board seats or named investments for him.
