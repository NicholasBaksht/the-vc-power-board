Biography

Noel Fenton co-founded Trinity Ventures in 1986. Before venture capital he worked at three venture-backed technology companies, serving as CEO of Covalent Systems and of Acurex, where his ten-year tenure was his longest and the company reached $65 million in revenues. His Trinity bio states that his focus areas are enterprise software and business-to-business commerce and that CEO mentoring is his greatest contribution, and Trinity's portfolio page attributes ServiceMax, VTS, LoopNet, Funnel, Taulia, MyNewPlace, BlueStripe Software, TrustID, DotLoop, ID Analytics, 24/7 Real Media, Bright Horizons, Forte, Fuego, Illustra, SciQuest, BlueTarp Financial and Rentlytics to him. Trinity Ventures' site continues to list him as Founding Partner.
