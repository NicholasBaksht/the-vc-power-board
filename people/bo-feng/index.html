Biography

Bo Feng is a Managing Partner at Dragonfly, listed with that title on the firm's team page. Dragonfly Capital Partners' 9 October 2018 announcement of its $100 million inaugural fund describes him as 'a managing partner of Dragonfly and a founding partner of Ceyuan Ventures', 'a first generation venture capitalist in China with over 20 years of VC experience', and states that 'In the '90s he launched the China business of Robertson Stephens, a leading high-tech investment bank based in San Francisco, and became one of the first Internet-focused VCs in China'. The same release states 'Feng is the largest investor in OKEx'; that is a 2018 statement and is not recorded here as a current position. He is named in the Form D of Dragonfly Ventures IV, L.P. as a manager of the fund's general partner. Fortune's February 2026 profile calls him the firm's founder and reports that Dragonfly began as a partnership between Alex Pack and Bo Feng.
