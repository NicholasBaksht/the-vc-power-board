Biography

Mack Healy, JD, is a Managing Partner at KdT Ventures, which he joined in March 2019 as the firm's second general partner. Before moving to venture capital he practiced technology and life science law at Fenwick & West and later at Austin based firms, advising on hundreds of venture financings representing more than $1 billion in aggregate value and working with companies including Airbnb, Cisco and WhatsApp. He earned his law degree from the University of Michigan, where he received a Dean's Scholarship, has made personal angel investments, and has guest lectured at law schools. At KdT he helps frontier science founders structure companies and financings from the first institutional check onward.
