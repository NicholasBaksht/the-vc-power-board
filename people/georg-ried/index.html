Biography

Dr. Georg Ried is a managing director of Bayern Kapital, the venture capital company of the Free State of Bavaria, a role he has held since 2013. Since April 2022 he has co-led the firm in a dual-leadership structure with Monika Steger, following the retirement of long-serving managing director Roman Huber. He is described in sourcing as a qualified biochemist; his earlier career history is not documented in available sources.
