Biography

Rick Ryan is a General Partner of Cultivation Capital. His firm profile records more than 35 years in business management and technology development in life sciences, across multinational corporations and early-stage ventures. He is Chief Executive Officer of Apertus Pharmaceuticals. He was Vice President of the Drug Discovery and Development business unit at Millipore from mid-2006 through 2010, President of LINCO Research, and held multiple Director-level positions at Monsanto in technology, business management, commercial development, strategic planning and international markets. He holds a B.S. in Chemistry from Central College in Pella, Iowa, a Ph.D. in Biochemistry from the University of Illinois, an American Cancer Society post-doctoral fellowship at the University of Wisconsin, and an Executive MBA from Washington University in St. Louis. No board seat or attributed portfolio company is published on his firm profile.
