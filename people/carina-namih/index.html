Biography

Carina Namih is a partner at Plural, which she joined in March 2023. She co-founded HelixNano in San Francisco in 2013 at age 23 and led it as Chief Executive Officer, applying AI to RNA and raising backing from investors including Sam Altman and Eric Schmidt, with the company now running trials in treatment-resistant cancers. Between 2018 and the summer of 2022 she did B2B seed-stage investing as a general partner with the UK fund Episode 1, and she serves on the board of The Alan Turing Institute. Her listed investments at Plural include Sunrise Robotics, Softmax, Molyon and Upside Robotics.
