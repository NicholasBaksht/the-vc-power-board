Biography

Justin Kaufenberg is a Managing Director at Rally Ventures, which he joined in 2019. He was previously co-founder and CEO of SportsEngine from inception through its acquisition by Comcast/NBCUniversal, and before that co-founded Third North. His Rally bio states that since joining the firm he has also been a co-founder of companies created, built and spun out of Rally, naming Revelstoke, Yardstik, JustiFi, Vertical Insure and Blank Metal. He is named as a Managing Director of the general partner of Rally Ventures Fund IV in that fund's Form D. He has a degree in Economics from the University of Wisconsin at Eau Claire.
