Biography

Jocelyn Doe is a General Partner at Builders VC. Her firm bio says she focuses on Modern Money and heads special situation investments, that she is "a graduate of Stanford University and The Anderson School of Management at UCLA", and that "Before joining Builders VC, Jocelyn was a senior investment banker at Morgan Stanley and Lehman Brothers." She is one of the Managing Members of the General Partner named in the Form D for Builders VC Fund II, L.P. and one of the related persons named in the Form D for Builders VC Fund III, L.P.
