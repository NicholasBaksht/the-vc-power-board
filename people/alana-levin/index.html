Biography

Alana Levin is an Investment Partner at Variant, where the firm's site says she focuses on infrastructure. Her bio states that her interest in crypto grew out of an interest in public goods and financial regulatory policy, that she spent a summer at the SEC, and that she graduated summa cum laude with degrees in Finance and Public Policy from Wharton. Variant's site says her work centres on token design, governance structures, scaling costs and privacy mechanics, and that she worked as a consultant at BCG before joining the firm in 2022. She has authored Variant investment announcements for delta and Pye.
