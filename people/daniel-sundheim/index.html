Biography

Daniel Sundheim founded D1 Capital Partners in 2018 after a 15-year tenure at Viking Global Investors, where he served as Chief Investment Officer. A Wharton economics graduate who began his career at Bear Stearns, he built D1 as a multi-asset firm investing across global public and private markets, with concentrated positions in growth companies including Instacart and Procore.
