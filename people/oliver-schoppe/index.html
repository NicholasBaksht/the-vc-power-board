Biography

Oliver Schoppe is a Partner at UVC Partners, which he joined in April 2021. His academic background spans electrical engineering, neuroscience and computer science/AI at TU Munich, Oxford and Harvard/MIT, and he previously led an AI research group at the Helmholtz Center. At UVC he focuses on artificial intelligence, enterprise SaaS, data-driven business models and digital health, and the firm describes him as a recognised AI expert who is regularly consulted by media and at events. His portfolio attributions include Aleph Alpha, Hypatos, Patronus, reel, paretos, Rencore, Biomatter, Sonia and Cirql One. UVC's team page does not publish board seats for him separately from these portfolio attributions.
