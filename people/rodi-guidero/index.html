Biography

Rodi Guidero is Executive Director of Breakthrough Energy Ventures, the climate investment fund founded within Bill Gates' Breakthrough Energy. He is named in that role in the fund's first SEC Form D, filed in December 2016 alongside board members including William Henry Gates III, Vinod Khosla, John Arnold, Mukesh Ambani, Jack Ma, Hasso Plattner and John Doerr. He was also named an executive officer and Managing Director of Catalyst Investment Partnership, L.P., the Breakthrough Energy Catalyst vehicle, in a 2022 Form D. A Form D filed on 11 August 2026 lists him, under the name Rodrigo Guidero, as a manager of Breakthrough Energy Ventures Management GP, LLC.
