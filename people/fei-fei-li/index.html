Biography

Fei-Fei Li is a Scientific Partner with Radical Ventures, a role she took on in February 2023 alongside her Stanford professorship. Stanford lists her as the Sequoia Capital Professor in the Computer Science Department and a founding co-director of the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence, and she directed the Stanford Artificial Intelligence Lab from 2013 to 2018. She was a vice president at Google and chief scientist of AI/ML at Google Cloud from 2017 to 2018, and she is the creator of ImageNet and the ImageNet Challenge as well as co-founder and chairperson of the non-profit AI4ALL. She is currently co-founder and chief executive officer of World Labs, and holds a B.A. in physics from Princeton University and an M.S. and Ph.D. in electrical engineering from the California Institute of Technology.
