Biography

Katsumasa Niki (仁木 勝雅) is Representative Director of DEEPCORE Inc. (株式会社ディープコア), the AI-focused Japanese venture capital firm whose company profile block names him in press releases from 2018 through June 2026. A June 2023 interview profile states that he served as head of SoftBank Group's investment division until 2016, working on the Vodafone Japan and Sprint acquisitions and on investments in domestic and overseas IT companies including Aldebaran Robotics, and that he founded DEEPCORE in 2017 as a wholly owned SoftBank Group subsidiary. DEEPCORE's 31 May 2018 fund announcement gives his title at that time as 代表取締役社長; the firm's June 2026 announcement gives it as 代表取締役. No education could be sourced. No source was found tying him individually to a named portfolio investment or to a current board seat.
