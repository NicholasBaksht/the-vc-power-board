Biography

Adam Stone is Chief Investment Officer at Perceptive Advisors and is tagged on the firm's team page against its Hedge Fund, Credit, Venture and Discovery strategies. SEC ownership filings show he became a director of Immatics N.V. (Nasdaq: IMTX) with an event date of 18 March 2026. He has previously filed Form 3s as an insider of Perceptive-sponsored special purpose acquisition companies ARYA Sciences Acquisition Corp II, III, IV and V and Perceptive Capital Solutions Corp, of LianBio, and of Adagio Medical Holdings, Inc.; on the Adagio Medical Form 3 of 1 August 2024 he was reported as a ten percent owner rather than a director. His Freenome, Inc. Form 4 of 22 July 2026 reports him as neither a director nor an officer.
