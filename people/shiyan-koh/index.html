Biography

Shiyan Koh is a Co-Founder and General Partner at Hustle Fund, a pre-seed venture firm, and is based in Singapore. She worked in finance and investing roles at JPMorgan, Institutional Venture Partners and Bridgewater Associates before joining NerdWallet as employee number 10, where over six years she led product teams and ran business operations and corporate development as the company grew from US$1M to US$150M in revenue. She left NerdWallet as Vice President of Business Operations & Corporate Development and returned to Singapore in 2018 to start Hustle Fund's Southeast Asian investing practice.
