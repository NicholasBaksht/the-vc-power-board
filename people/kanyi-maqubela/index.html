Biography

Kanyi Maqubela is a Managing Partner at Kindred Ventures, the San Francisco pre-seed and seed firm founded by Steve Jang. His work centres on frontier technology, digital health, e-commerce, financial technology and supply chain, and the firm's bio lists him as lead investor in Adyton, Basis Theory, Faircraft, Cloudtrucks, Corgi and Tala. He was previously a partner at Collaborative Fund, co-founded Heartbeat Health, and was growth lead at One Block Off the Grid, which was acquired by NRG. He studied Philosophy at Stanford University, has been an adjunct professor at the New York University Tisch School of the Arts, and sits on the board of directors of the UCSF Foundation Investment Company.
