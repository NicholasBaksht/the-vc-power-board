Biography

Eurie Kim joined Forerunner Ventures in 2012 and is listed on the firm's site as Managing Partner. Her stated areas of interest are health and wellness, financial services, career development and learning, consumer AI and AI infrastructure, and Forerunner lists her board seats as Oura, The Farmer's Dog, Leland, The Feed, A-Frame, Monarch Money, Joy, Granted and Andromeda Robotics. She led Forerunner's Series B in Oura in December 2019 and has served as Chairperson of that board since. Before Forerunner she developed strategy for Fortune 100 companies at Bain & Company and invested in growth-stage businesses as a private equity investor.
