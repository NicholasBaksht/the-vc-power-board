Biography

Rob Hadick is a General Partner at Dragonfly, listed with that title on the firm's team page and identified as 'Rob Hadick, General Partner at Dragonfly' in Avalanche Treasury Co.'s 11 June 2026 Nasdaq listing release. Fortune reports he signed the paperwork to join Dragonfly Capital in April 2022. The Block's profile of him states he was involved in multi-stage investments in public and private crypto companies at GoldenTree Asset Management, and that 'Prior to his work at GoldenTree, Rob held roles in fintech, technology, and crypto investments at firms such as Heritage Partners, Goldman Sachs, and PJT Partners', and that he holds an M.B.A. from Columbia Business School and a Bachelor's degree in Economics and Political Science from Washington University in St. Louis. He is a director of Avalanche Treasury Corp, the Nasdaq-listed AVAX treasury company that Dragonfly helped seed.
