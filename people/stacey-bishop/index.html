Biography

Stacey Bishop is a Partner at Scale Venture Partners, where her firm bio lists focus areas of the CFO stack and go-to-market technology applications. Her bio states that she has invested at Scale throughout her career, working with early-stage SaaS companies including BILL, ExactTarget and Omniture, and lists current board positions at Abacum, Motion, Airspace and Demandbase. Previous board roles listed on her bio include HubSpot, Bizible, Lever and Vitrue. She is a founding member of All Raise and holds an MBA from Columbia Business School and a BA from the University of Michigan.
