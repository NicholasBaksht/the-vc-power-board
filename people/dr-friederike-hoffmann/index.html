Biography

Dr. Friederike Hoffmann is a Partner at b2venture in its Direct Investments team. The firm's announcement, published 17 June 2026, states she joined as Partner as of January. Her b2venture page states she spent more than fifteen years at Swisscom, most recently as Executive Vice President and Head of its B2B telecommunications business, where she led commercial, product and delivery organisations and managed partner ecosystems including IoT and 5G activities. She holds a PhD from the University of St. Gallen with a research focus on business model innovation and organisational transformation, and is based in Zurich. Ivoclar's own website currently lists her, as Dr. Friederike Hoffmann-Sieg, as a member of its Supervisory Board; Ivoclar announced that appointment on 1 September 2022. Her b2venture page also lists a board seat at localsearch (Swisscom Directories AG), which could not be confirmed against localsearch's own site and is therefore not recorded here.
