Biography

Lee Ainslie is listed as Managing Partner on Maverick Capital's team page and is one of the six people shown on the Maverick Ventures investment team page. In the January 2025 Form D/A for Maverick Ventures Cycle 4 Serial Fund, L.P. he is named as "Lee S. Ainslie III", an executive officer identified as Managing Partner of Maverick Ventures Cycle 4 GP, LLC, with an address in West Palm Beach, Florida. He is also a reporting person on the Maverick group's Schedule 13D and Section 16 filings for Infleqtion, Inc. Those Infleqtion filings identify David B. Singer, not him, as the director, so no board seat is recorded for him here. No education or prior employment was sourced from an acceptable first-party page, so those fields are left empty rather than filled from secondary profiles.
