Biography

Morgan Beller was a General Partner at NFX, joining in September 2020 as the firm's fourth General Partner. Before NFX she co-founded Facebook's Libra project, later renamed Diem, and served as Head of Strategy for Novi, Facebook's digital wallet, having joined Facebook's corporate development team in 2017 to work on blockchain and decentralized technology strategy. Earlier she worked in corporate development at Medium, where she led the Embedly acquisition, and on the deal team at Andreessen Horowitz. She holds a bachelor's degree in Statistics and Information Science from Cornell University.
