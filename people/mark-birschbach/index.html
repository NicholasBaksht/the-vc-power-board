Biography

Mark Birschbach is listed as Managing Director on Second Century Ventures' own About page, and the SEC Form D filed for Second Century Ventures Fund I, LP on 31 July 2024 names him as a Managing Director of the fund's general partner, Second Century Ventures GP I, LLC. He holds a parallel role at the parent association: SCV's 14 May 2025 cohort announcement quotes him as "NAR's executive vice president, strategic business innovation and technology". NAR's own technology page states that its Strategic Business, Innovation and Technology group "operates Second Century Ventures (SCV), a wholly owned subsidiary and the strategic investment arm", so his SCV role sits inside the group he leads at NAR. No education or earlier employer is published by SCV or NAR and none has been recorded. No SCV announcement attributes a specific investment to him, so notableInvestments is empty.
