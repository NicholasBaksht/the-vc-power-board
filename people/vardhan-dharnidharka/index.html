Biography

Vardhan Dharnidharka is an investor at Stellaris Venture Partners and the firm's resident AI/ML specialist. He is an engineering leader who led Rider Product Engineering at Lyft, launched its delivery line of business and worked on marketplace pricing and driver utilisation. He later built the AI pillar at ClickUp during the generative AI wave, and earlier built search engines and recommendation systems for the Bloomberg Terminal. He studied engineering at RV College and holds a master's degree from Carnegie Mellon University. His portfolio work includes Arrowhead, Atlas, Pinegap and CARPL.
