Biography

Rory O'Driscoll is a Partner at Scale Venture Partners, where his firm bio lists focus areas of applications and robotics and states that he has been investing with the firm since the mid-1990s. He grew up in Ireland, trained as an accountant, attended the London School of Economics and moved to the United States in 1991, joining Bank of America's corporate M&A division and then the bank's venture capital arm, which relaunched as Scale Venture Partners in 2007. His firm bio names BILL, Box, DocuSign, WalkMe, Tavus, Klarity, Regie and Locus Robotics among his investments. He served on the boards of Box, DocuSign and Bill.com, and joined the Tavus board in connection with Scale's Series A investment.
