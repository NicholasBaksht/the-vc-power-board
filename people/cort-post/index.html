Biography

Cort Post is a Principal at Courtside Ventures, where he works across the firm's investment verticals with particular emphasis on the wellness category. He came to Courtside with a background in finance and early stage startups. He previously led Finance and Operations at High Alpha, a B2B SaaS venture studio, where he worked with dozens of early stage companies from inception through Series A, and before that worked in the Entertainment Finance Group at Wells Fargo covering the film and television sectors. He holds an MBA in Finance and Entrepreneurship from Northwestern's Kellogg School of Management and a BS in Finance from Indiana University's Kelley School of Business.
