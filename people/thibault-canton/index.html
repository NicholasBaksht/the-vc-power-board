Biography

Thibault Canton is a Partner at Seventure Partners. His Seventure bio says he joined the firm in July 2012 as part of the acquisition of Masseran Gestion, where he had begun his private equity career in January 2010 exploring investment opportunities in information technology and biotechnology, and that in April 2018 he joined the Quadrivium I Seed Fund as a Partner. With Philippe Tramoy he is responsible for the Quadrivium 1 Seed Fund's investments in the digital sector, reporting to the head of the Digital Technologies team, David Manjarres. He holds a Master's degree in Mechanics & Energy from University Pierre and Marie Curie, Paris, and a Master's degree in Technology & Innovation Management from Paris-Dauphine University. His Seventure bio names Actronika, Botfuel, Indexima, Intento Design, Open Ocean and Praditus among his portfolio companies. Seventure's French team page gives his title as "Directeur de Participations", a level below the English "Partner"; the English title is used here and the discrepancy is recorded in the notes.
