Biography

Noriaki Sakamoto is Representative Director and COO of UTEC and a Managing Partner. UTEC's profile states that at the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry he worked on SME finance and recycling legislation and on the Mito Software Project, then after leaving government joined a family apparel distribution business where he launched and wound down new businesses, and subsequently worked at McKinsey & Company on projects for pharmaceutical, medical device, automotive, high technology, consumer goods and financial services clients across Japan, Southeast Asia and Europe. The page lists past outside statutory auditor roles at ACSL Ltd. (TSE Mothers listing December 2018, code 6232) and Repertoire Genesis Inc. (acquired by Eurofins Scientific SE), and past outside director roles at Neural Pocket Inc. (TSE Mothers August 2020, code 4056), JDSC Inc. (TSE Mothers December 2021, code 4418), Finatext Holdings Ltd. (TSE Mothers December 2021, code 4419), Institution for a Global Society Corporation (TSE Mothers December 2021, code 4265) and ELEMENTS, Inc. (TSE Growth December 2022, code 5246). All of these are written in the past tense (務めた) on UTEC's own page, so none is recorded as a current board seat. He graduated from the University of Tokyo Faculty of Economics and holds an MBA from Columbia University.
