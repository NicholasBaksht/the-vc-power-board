Biography

Barak Kaufman is a partner at Vine Ventures and Head of Israel, splitting time between New York and Tel Aviv. He started his career cold-calling founders at Insight Partners alongside Eric Reiner. He then founded Intello, a SaaS company managing SaaS security, which he exited to SailPoint in 2021. After the acquisition he incubated two health tech businesses and ran founder communities. At Vine he specializes in working with founders at the pre-idea and idea stage, particularly post-exit founders navigating their next ventures.
