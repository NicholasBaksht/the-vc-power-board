Biography

Julien Cazor is a Venture Partner at Seventure Partners based in Berlin, Germany. His Seventure bio says he joined the firm in 2023 as a Venture Partner to strengthen the Digital Technology team for Germany in sourcing, investing in and supporting innovative start-ups, particularly in fintech and retailtech. It states he brings 20 years of experience across investment banking, investment management and fintech, including a role as an investment partner for an alternative investment fund and leadership positions in pan-European fintech companies. He holds an engineering degree from the Ecole Nationale Superieure de Techniques Avancees in Paris, part of Institut Polytechnique de Paris. His Seventure bio names Flexvelop and Wultra as his portfolio investments, and he is quoted as Venture Partner in the 29 June 2026 press release announcing Wultra's EUR 6.8 million Series A.
