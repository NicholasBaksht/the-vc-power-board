Biography

Lawrence Tse co-founded Gobi Partners in Shanghai in 2002 with Thomas Tsao and Wai Kit Lau, the three having previously worked together at the San Francisco venture firm WI Harper. He represented the firm publicly on China venture investing, addressing the CFA Institute Asia Pacific Investment Conference in 2012, where he was described as being with Gobi Partners, a venture capital firm based in Shanghai focused on early stage Series A investments. He is not listed anywhere on Gobi Partners' current site, whose own history page credits Thomas G. Tsao alone with founding the firm.
