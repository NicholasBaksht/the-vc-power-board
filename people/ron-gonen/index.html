Biography

Ron Gonen is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Closed Loop Partners, the New York based circular economy platform he started in 2014, spanning venture capital, private equity, catalytic capital and private credit, an innovation center and operating companies including Circular Services. He previously co-founded Recyclebank, a recycling rewards company he led as CEO, and served as Deputy Commissioner of Sanitation, Recycling and Sustainability for New York City during the Bloomberg administration. He holds an MBA from Columbia Business School, where he has taught as an adjunct professor, and is the author of The Waste-Free World. His recognitions include a UN Environment Programme honor and Columbia University's Medal of Excellence.
