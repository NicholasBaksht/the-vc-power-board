Biography

Shanbor Gupta is a Principal at Clean Energy Ventures, based in Europe, where he leads greenhouse gas impact assessment work and helped develop the firm's Simple Emissions Reduction Calculator (SERC) tool. He joined the firm as an Investment Manager before being promoted to Principal, and works on deal sourcing and portfolio company support alongside emissions quantification. Before venture capital he was a serial founder: he co-founded Webcel Solutions in 2010, an e-commerce and inventory management startup serving Indian SMEs, and co-founded SES Environmental in 2012, growing it from a five-person team to more than 100 employees across India providing asset management and lifecycle assessment services. He also worked in KPMG India's corporate finance (M&A) team. He holds an MBA from Babson College and a bachelor's degree in International Studies and Economics from the University of Birmingham.
