Biography

Tom Peterson is a Venture Partner at Rally Ventures and joined the firm at its inception in 2012. His Rally bio states he has been a venture capitalist for more than 30 years, focusing on enterprise software and infrastructure companies, that he was one of the founding members of El Dorado Ventures in 1986, and that he began his venture career in 1984 with Union Venture Corp., the venture capital arm of Union Bank. Before that he held engineering and management positions with Hewlett-Packard and General Electric. He received an MBA from UCLA and a BS from Iowa State University. His Rally bio states that he serves on the boards of 401GO, Appdome, Epoch and Rev and is a board observer at DecisionNext and PieEye; none of those seats could be confirmed against a portfolio-company filing or company board page, so boardSeats is left empty.
