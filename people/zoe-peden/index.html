Biography

Zoe Peden is a Partner at Ananda Impact Ventures, working from London. She holds an undergraduate degree from the London School of Economics and an MBA from Henley Business School, and has nearly two decades of experience in social mobility and equality. Before joining Ananda she founded and ran an impact technology company for around a decade; the firm does not name it. Her portfolio attributions are DrDoctor, Elio, Nature Metrics, Ocean Ledger, Ovom, Quantistry and Resistomap, and she is actively exploring biosecurity, nature tech and ocean tech. Ananda does not publish her joining year, so it is left null.
