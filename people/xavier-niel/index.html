Biography

Xavier Niel is a Co-Founder of Kima Ventures, the Paris investment fund he created in March 2010. He is the majority shareholder and historic leader of the Iliad Group and has worked in the telematics, internet and telecommunications industries since the late 1980s, creating 3617 ANNU, launching the internet access provider Free in 1999 and introducing the Freebox set-top box in 2002. Since 2010 he has been a controlling shareholder of the newspaper Le Monde alongside Pierre Berge and Matthieu Pigasse. He is also the founder of Station F, the Paris startup campus he bought the building for in 2013 and which opened to the public in 2017.
