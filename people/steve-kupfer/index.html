Biography

Steve Kupfer is a Partner at Reach Capital working across education, health and policy. Early in his career he led software implementations in urban school districts, including the first digital special education platform in post-Katrina New Orleans, and he spent several years recruiting and training school superintendents. He made early investments in Presence, Ellevation, Paper and Goalbook. Before Reach he was an operating partner on the Knowledge and Learning team at The Vistria Group. His Reach portfolio includes InquirED, MagicSchool, Marker Learning, Cartwheel and Swing Education. Reach Capital does not publish his degrees or board seats.
