Biography

Andrew Miklas is a Venture Partner at S28 Capital. He co-founded PagerDuty and served as its founding CTO, scaling the product and engineering organization past 70 people. He has also worked with the Y Combinator team. At S28 he focuses on startups building products for software and operations engineers, and more broadly on B2B companies with a strong product focus or deep technical insight. He holds a bachelor's in software engineering from the University of Waterloo and an M.Sc. from the University of Toronto.
