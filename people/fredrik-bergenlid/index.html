Fredrik Bergenlid

Co-Founder & General Partner at Fly Ventures

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Fredrik Bergenlid co-founded Fly Ventures, a Berlin-based seed firm that invests in European enterprise and deep tech startups, together with Gabriel Matuschka. He previously worked at Google on Google Assistant. He is registered with Matuschka as a managing director of Fly Ventures Management GmbH in Berlin. Fly launched its debut fund in December 2017, closed a 53 million euro second fund in 2020, and closed an 80 million euro third fund in December 2024.
