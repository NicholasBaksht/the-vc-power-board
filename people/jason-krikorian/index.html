Biography

Jason Krikorian served as a General Partner of DCM from 2010 to December 2022. Before joining the firm he co-founded Sling Media, the creator of the Slingbox, where he led operator partnerships and international expansion, and earlier was a partner at id8 Group and a consultant at Boston Consulting Group. During and after his DCM tenure he sat on the boards of Matterport, Augmedix, Shift Technologies and PLAYSTUDIOS, and he remains a director of PLAYSTUDIOS. He holds a B.A. in Psychology from the University of California, Berkeley and both an M.B.A. and a J.D. from the University of Virginia.
