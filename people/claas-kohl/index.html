Biography

Claas Kohl was named as Partner at Airbus Ventures in the firm's October 2022 announcement of its investment in the fintech Jiko, and earlier the same year was identified as Airbus Ventures' Financial Officer in the firm's April 2022 announcement of its investment in the Swiss natural fibre composites company Bcomp. He provided the firm's commentary on both investments. He is not listed among the twelve people shown on the Airbus Ventures team page as of 30 August 2026.
