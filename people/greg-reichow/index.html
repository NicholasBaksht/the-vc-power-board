Biography

Greg Reichow is a Partner at Eclipse Ventures, where he invests in technical teams tackling complex hardware and manufacturing challenges. Eclipse's SEC-filed director biography states he has served as a Partner at the firm since July 2016. Before venture capital he was Vice President, Production at Tesla, responsible for global manufacturing, supply chain and automation engineering, and earlier was Senior Vice President of Operations at SunPower Corporation. He serves on the boards of Enovix, where he has been a director since July 2021, and VulcanForms, and holds a B.S. in mechanical and industrial engineering from the University of Minnesota.
