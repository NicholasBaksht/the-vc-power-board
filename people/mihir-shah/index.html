Biography

Mihir Shah is a Venture Partner with the Eight Roads technology team. His profile states he brings over 30 years of experience in technology leadership and served as CIO and Head of Data Architecture and Engineering at Fidelity Investments before retiring in March 2024, where he was responsible for an enterprise-wide data strategy including a firm master data management platform and a firm-wide data and analytics platform. Earlier he was Head of the Technology Architecture Group at Fidelity and Chief Technology Officer for Fidelity's asset management business.
