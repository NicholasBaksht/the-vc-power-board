Biography

Brice Lapouble is listed as Partner on the Axeleo Capital team page. The firm's own announcement of 2 July 2024 states that he joined in 2021 and was promoted to Partner, and attributes to him "first checks in TRIVER, Zygon, Picsellia, some other unannounced golden nuggets, and recent exits like Husprey (acq. by Collibra) and Kadiska (acquired by Netskope)", within an enterprise software focus covering cybersecurity, infrastructure, devtools, AI, data and fintech. His bio on the firm's axc.vc site, which still carries his older title of Investment Director, gives his education as mathematics engineering at Bordeaux University and corporate finance at Audencia Business School, and his prior roles as venture capital investor at Hi inov and OneRagtime and founder's associate at the Spanish startup Deliberry. No current board seat could be verified.
