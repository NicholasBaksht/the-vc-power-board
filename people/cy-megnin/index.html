Biography

Cy Megnin is Vice President of Software at Elevate Ventures. His team-member page states he has been an entrepreneur, founder and advisor for over two decades and names past companies including PrepFlash, an EdTech company described as an Intel Capital portfolio company, and CloudCoreo, a configuration management company that took investment from Microsoft and was ultimately sold to VMware. It records that he was in the 2014 class of Forty Under 40 for Professional Remodeler Magazine while leading the real estate investment firm OCPS. He holds a degree in Economics from Indiana University and an MBA from Baylor University, and the page says he is based in Bloomington, Indiana after 15 years in Austin, Seattle and the Bay Area. No individual company has been tied to him by a first-party source as an Elevate investment, so notableInvestments is empty.
