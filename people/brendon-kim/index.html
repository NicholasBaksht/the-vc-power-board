Brendon Kim

Managing Director, Samsung NEXT Ventures at Samsung NEXT

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Brendon Kim led venture investing at Samsung NEXT, where Samsung NEXT's own blog identifies him as Samsung NEXT Ventures managing director and a 2018 conference interview gives his title as VP, MD, Global Head of Ventures. Before Samsung he was a co-founding general partner at Altos Ventures and worked as a strategy consultant at CSC Index and SMS Coopers & Lybrand, and he earlier ran early-stage venture investing at Samsung Electronics' Open Innovation Center as a Senior Director. He holds an MBA from Stanford Graduate School of Business and an A.B. from Princeton University. He is not listed on the current Samsung NEXT team page.
