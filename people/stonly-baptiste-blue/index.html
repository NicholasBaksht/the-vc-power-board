Biography

Stonly Baptiste Blue is a co-founder of Third Sphere. His firm bio describes him as Co-founder and Managing Partner, says he was born in Haiti and immigrated to the US as a child, launched an IT consultancy after high school and went on to found several software, hardware and service companies, the last of which was an enterprise cloud platform acquired and integrated into NetApp. His bio says that exit led to the firm's co-founding in 2013. He has taught at the University of Chicago Booth School of Business and lectured at Harvard Business School and UC Berkeley. Impact Capital Managers lists him as Board Chair of its ICM Institute board, described there as 'Managing Partner & Co-founder, Third Sphere'. He signed the Third Sphere Fund IV, LP Form D in May 2022, where he is listed as Partner and Executive Officer.
