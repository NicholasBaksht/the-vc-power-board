Biography

Gilles Dufour is a Partner at VIVES Partners and a member of its investment committee, covering life sciences and biotechnology. He holds a PhD in biomedical sciences from the University of Liège and carried out postdoctoral research at Harvard Medical School. He worked on biotech investment evaluation at RA Capital Management and held corporate development and clinical strategy roles at Synthorx, which Sanofi acquired for USD 2.5 billion. He also founded and ran Accretio Management, a boutique life sciences consulting firm. VIVES describes him as having more than ten years of experience across venture capital, clinical strategy, corporate development, company creation and biotech operations. VIVES does not publish his joining year or board seats, so those are left blank.
