Biography

Thomas Nivard is a Partner at 360 Capital, where he joined in 2021 and focuses on early-stage investments in France and Spain. Before 360 Capital he spent two years as Head of Investment for an NGO in Cuba, leading a team across more than 20 education and social investments and fundraising in over 15 countries, and three years as a consultant at Eurogroup Consulting in Paris. He holds an M.B.A from INSEAD and an engineering degree from Ecole Centrale Lyon, and is one of the partners running 360 LIFE II, the firm's climate tech fund that announced a EUR 140 million first close in December 2024.
