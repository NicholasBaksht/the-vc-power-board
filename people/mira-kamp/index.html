Biography

Mira Kamp is a Principal at Redalpine and Managing Director for Germany. She studied management at the University of Mannheim and gained practical experience at Siemens and Deloitte Garage. She was the first employee at Join Capital, where she focused on SaaS and deep tech investments, before joining Redalpine. At Redalpine she works on SaaS and deep tech investments and supports portfolio companies operationally. The firm does not publish her joining year, board seats or named investments.
