Biography

Arturo Ancira García is an Associate at Elaia. He studied mechanical engineering, materials science and aerospace engineering at INSA de Lyon and holds a master's in corporate finance from EM Lyon Business School. He worked as an analyst at Fisterra Energy, a Blackstone energy-focused private equity fund, and then as a venture capital analyst at OneRagtime before joining Elaia. Elaia lists his focus as developer tools, artificial intelligence and materials science. His joining year is not stated on the firm's site.
