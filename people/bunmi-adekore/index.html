Biography

Bunmi Adekore is a Venture Partner at Contrarian Ventures. His firm bio states he leads investing at DawnBox, a fund focused on innovative industrial solutions. He was previously a Partner at Breakthrough Energy Ventures, where he led investments in manufacturing, mobility, energy and emerging markets. Before investing he spent over a decade as a serial entrepreneur building hardware manufacturing businesses in clean technology, semiconductor engineering and biotechnology. He holds bachelor's and doctorate degrees in Materials Science and Engineering from North Carolina State University. Contrarian does not publish his join year or board seats.
