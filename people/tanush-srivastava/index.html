Biography

Tanush Srivastava is an Analyst at Arkam Ventures. His stated focus is AI, fintech, SaaS and high-growth Indian startups, with an interest in founder-led, product-centric businesses. On his firm profile he cites Zerodha as a company that "picked a deeply underserved customer, built something radically simple, and let the product do the talking". Arkam does not publish his education or previous employers.
