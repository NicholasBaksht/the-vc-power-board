Biography

Adam Nelson is a Partner at FirstMark, where his bio states he focuses on Fintech and Vertical Software, including companies like Kasa, Backflip, Workstep, and Authentic. Before joining FirstMark in 2019 he was a Venture Partner at South Park Commons and a Partner at Social Capital, and earlier an early employee at Twitter and at Dropbox, where he built and scaled the Partner Network and Revenue Partnerships teams. He began his career in investment banking and private equity at Merrill Lynch and Lindsay Goldberg. He holds a B.A. from Brown University and an M.B.A. from Stanford Graduate School of Business, and founded the Fintech Driven community in 2022.
