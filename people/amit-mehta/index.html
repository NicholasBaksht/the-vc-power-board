Biography

Dr Amit Mehta is a General Partner at Builders VC and leads its healthcare investment practice, which his firm bio says supports companies from seed through Series B. His bio states "Amit manages our investments into Carbon Health, Notable Labs and Fitbiomics", that he was founder and Chief Medical Officer of Intrinsic Imaging, which was acquired by WCG-Clinical, and that before Builders he deployed health-sector investments for Formation 8. He completed his residency and a fellowship in Minimally Invasive Image Guided Surgery at Harvard Medical School. He is named as a related person on the Form D filings for Builders VC Fund II, L.P. and Builders VC Fund III, L.P.
