Biography

Srini Ananth joined Intel Capital in 2015 and is a Managing Director based in the Bay Area. His Intel Capital bio states that he focuses on deep tech, cloud infrastructure and AI application companies, and that he serves as director or observer on the boards of Astera Labs, Ayar Labs, Common Sense Machines, Eliyan, Inworld AI, SiPhox and VeriSIM Life. The same bio names Astera Labs, Ayar Labs, BabbleLabs, Common Sense Machines, Eliyan, Inworld AI, Iris.TV, Joby Aviation and OnScale among his investments. He brings more than a decade of operating experience across engineering, business development and product management in IoT, mobile and semiconductors, and holds an MBA from UCLA and an MSEE from Arizona State University.
