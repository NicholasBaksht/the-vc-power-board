Biography

Vikas J. Parekh is a Managing Partner at SoftBank Investment Advisers, where his own bio states he focuses on investing in Automation, Enterprise software, and other Emerging Technologies. He joined SoftBank in 2016 after working in private equity at KKR and in consulting at Boston Consulting Group. He serves as a director on public and private company boards, including Symbotic, and was appointed to WeWork's board in November 2022. He holds an M.B.A. with highest honors from Harvard Business School and B.S. and M.S. degrees in electrical and computer engineering from the Georgia Institute of Technology.
