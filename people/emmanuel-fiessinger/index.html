Biography

Emmanuel Fiessinger is a Senior Partner in Seventure Partners' Life Sciences team. His Seventure bio says he is frequently involved in the firm's investments in France, particularly in medical devices and industrial biotechnologies. He began his career in international project engineering in Qatar and Portugal, joined Seventure Partners in January 2000, and has been a Venture Capital Investor in the Life Sciences team since 2005. He is an engineer from the ICAM Catholic Institute of Engineering in Lille and holds a Master's degree in Finance from EMlyon business school. His Seventure bio names Bioalliance, Biomatlante, Endocontrol, Impeto Medical, Metabolic Explorer, Middle Peak and CardioSecur among his portfolio companies.
