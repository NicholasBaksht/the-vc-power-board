Biography

Pilar Carvajo Lucena is listed as Investments & Fund Partnerships Principal on Third Derivative's team page and as Principal on the Third Derivative team in RMI's own staff directory. Her RMI bio states that she leads Third Derivative's investments and fund partnerships, overseeing "the investments of the D3 Accelerator Funds as well as capital strategy to support D3 startups as they come on board, grow, and scale", and it says she joined RMI, which identifies RMI as her employer. Her bio records a decade of investment experience across asset classes and geographies and financial risk management. Before RMI she worked at IDB Invest structuring climate funds and investing across asset classes in Latin America and the Caribbean, previously at Accion Global Investments on private equity transactions in financial services in Asia and Latin America, and earlier as a financial risk management consultant for infrastructure and renewable energy clients at Deloitte. She holds an MBA from MIT Sloan and a joint degree in law and business from Universidad Pablo de Olavide in Seville. No join year, board seat or individually attributed investment could be sourced for her.
