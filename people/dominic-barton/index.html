Biography

Dominic Barton is a Partner at Radical Ventures, where he advises portfolio companies on commercialisation and go-to-market strategy. He is chair of Rio Tinto and of LeapFrog Investments, recently completed a term as Canada's Ambassador to China, and was global managing partner of McKinsey & Company from 2009 to 2018. He studied as a Rhodes Scholar at Oxford.
