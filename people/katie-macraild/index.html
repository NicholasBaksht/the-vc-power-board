Biography

Katie MacRaild is a Partner at Maven Capital Partners, based in the Glasgow office, where she administers the Maven Investor Partner client base. Her Maven profile says she joined in November 2018 and that before Maven she spent 18 years in private wealth management roles focused on creating bespoke investment products and advising on portfolio construction for high net worth and ultra high net worth investors, and subsequently worked for a private investment office providing investment advice and product solutions with an emphasis on private equity. She holds a bachelor's degree in Accounting and Finance from the University of Aberdeen.
