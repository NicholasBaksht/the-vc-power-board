Biography

Alex Sacerdote founded Whale Rock Capital Management in 2006 and serves as its CEO and Chief Investment Officer. A Hamilton College graduate with an MBA from Harvard, he was previously a portfolio manager at Fidelity focused on technology and media. At Whale Rock he runs a concentrated growth strategy built around long-duration technology trends. He serves on the board of his alma mater, Hamilton College.
