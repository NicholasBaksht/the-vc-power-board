Biography

Leila Rastegar Zegna is a founding general partner of Kindred Capital. She graduated cum laude in Mathematics and Philosophy from Yale before an MBA at Harvard Business School. After starting her career at Bain & Company she became general manager of Plum District, then COO of the genomics startup GenapSys, before co-founding Kindred Capital in 2015 to build its equitable venture model.
