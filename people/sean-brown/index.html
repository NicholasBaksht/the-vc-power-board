Biography

Sean Brown is a Venture Partner at Hack VC. The firm's team page states he specializes in infrastructure and mechanism design, particularly cross-chain, verified compute and DeFi, led product at UMA Protocol, was part of the founding team at Across Protocol and was a senior blockchain consultant at IBM. He authored or co-authored Hack VC's published investment posts on Hyperlane and Initia.
