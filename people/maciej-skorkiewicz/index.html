View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Maciej Skórkiewicz is an Operating Partner at Balnord covering the legacy portfolio, where he supports portfolio management and works with founders on operational and strategic challenges. He has over 20 years of senior executive experience across venture capital, life sciences and ICT, including serving as CEO of the Inveno Venture Capital fund. He previously spent 11 years as Director of Investor Relations at Prokom Software, a Polish ICT company, and founded Sopot's Brokerage House. He has held CEO, finance vice-president and board roles at several early-stage life sciences and ICT companies. His professional focus is fundraising, capital markets, investments and M&A, with particular strength in IPOs and public securities trading, and he has been active in the Polish capital market since the early 1990s.
