Biography

Alice Newcombe-Ellis is Founding & General Partner of Ahren Innovation Capital, which she launched in September 2018 alongside nine Founding Science Partners drawn largely from Cambridge. Ahren's team page states she has over a decade of investing experience, including at TPG Capital and Lansdowne Partners, with a focus on technology and healthcare. She holds mathematics degrees from the University of Cambridge and an MBA from Harvard. In Ahren's 2019 announcement of its Graphcore Series D investment she was quoted on the company's chip architecture. Her exact join-year is taken as 2018, the year Ahren publicly launched.
