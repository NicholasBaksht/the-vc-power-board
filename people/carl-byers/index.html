Biography

Carl Byers is a Partner at F-Prime Capital, based in Cambridge, where he works on healthcare investments. He was a founding team member at athenahealth and served as its Chief Financial Officer from 1997 to 2010, with responsibility for the finance, legal and corporate technology teams, and he managed the company's 2007 IPO. Before athenahealth he was a Senior Consultant at Booz, Allen & Hamilton in New York, covering financial services and health insurance. He is an Adjunct Lecturer on Health Policy and Management at Harvard's Chan School of Public Health and was appointed by Governor Charlie Baker to the Massachusetts Digital Health Council.
