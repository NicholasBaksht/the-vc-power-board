Biography

Lee Shmulevitz is an Associate at Viola Growth. She joined the fund as an Analyst in 2021 and was promoted to Associate in 2023. Before Viola she was a Customer Success Manager at the cybersecurity company BlueVoyant, and earlier held strategy, policy and foreign relations roles across Israeli government ministries. She served four and a half years as an officer in the Iron Dome air defense unit, including as Deputy Commander of a battery. She holds a B.A. and an M.A. from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.
