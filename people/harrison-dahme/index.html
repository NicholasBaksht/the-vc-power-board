Biography

Harrison Dahme is Partner and Chief Technology Officer at Hack VC, a role he announced in the firm's blog post of 18 August 2025. Hack VC's team page describes him as focused on AI, privacy and cryptography, with over a decade in AI since 2010 and activity in crypto since 2017, and states he is a multiple-time founder and CTO with nine patents, was previously CTO at a crypto AI company, was a crypto advisor to Lightspeed, and founded a company that exited to Coinbase. In his own joining post he describes fifteen years across gaming, data, distributed systems, crypto and AI, and a prior role as Chief Scientist at a crypto AI protocol. He authored Hack VC's investment posts on Math Inc. and Zama.
