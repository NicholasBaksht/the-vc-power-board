Biography

Brett Queener is a Managing Director at Bonfire Ventures; the firm's team page lists his title as Managing Director and his individual page carries the title Partner. His account of his career on the firm's site is that he helped build the alliances and marketing organisations at Siebel Systems, spent 12 years at Salesforce across revenue operations and product and led several larger acquired business units there, and served as Chief Operating Officer of SmartRecruiters. He writes that he works at the intersection of product and go-to-market with founders. He is named as a Managing Director of the general partner in the Form D for Bonfire Ventures IV, LP, and as one of the managers of Bonfire Select Associates II, LLC in the Schedule 13G filed on MNTN stock in August 2025. He writes a Substack newsletter and authored the firm's investment posts on Katalyze and Vali Health.
