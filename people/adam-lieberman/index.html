Biography

Adam Lieberman is Chief Technology Officer at Fifth Wall. He was previously Chief AI Officer at Finastra, where he led global artificial intelligence strategy across the enterprise and supported the deployment of AI solutions for financial institutions in more than 130 countries, and before that led computer vision and machine learning initiatives within NCR's innovation lab. He began his career as co-founder of Cappio, an AI-powered investment research startup that was acquired by Simply Wall St. He graduated from Georgia Tech with an MS in Computer Science and a BS in Applied Mathematics.
