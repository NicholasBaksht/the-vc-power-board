Biography

Lenny Pruss is a General Partner at Amplify Partners. He came to the United States as a young child when his family left the former Soviet Union, studied EECS at UC Berkeley, and holds an MBA from Harvard Business School. He began his venture career in New York and was a principal at Redpoint Ventures focused on cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity and developer tools before joining Amplify in 2017, where he was promoted to General Partner in 2020. His Amplify bio lists Antithesis, Anyscale, bitdrift, Chainguard, Dagster, dbt, exe.dev, Hightouch, Prisma, Sourcegraph, Temporal and TigerBeetle among his select companies.
