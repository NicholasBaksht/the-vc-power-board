Biography

Jonah Midanik is Managing Partner at Forum Ventures, a firm that invests exclusively in early-stage B2B software through an AI studio, an accelerator and a pre-seed fund. He spent about twenty years building companies in Canada and the United States, including as founder and CEO of Limelight, a venture backed company where he raised eight figures of capital, and Forum's team page lists his previous life as founder of Limelight. He went through Forum's own accelerator as a founder before joining the firm. In 2024 he described his role as COO and General Partner and said he runs Forum's AI Studio, which launches eight AI native companies a year.
