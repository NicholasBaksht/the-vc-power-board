Biography

Paul Irving is a General Partner of GTMfund, the title on the firm's own team page as of 7 September 2026. Inovia, an institutional limited partner in GTMfund's second fund, publishes a profile of him under the title "Partner & Platform Director" which states he is a graduate of McGill, co-founded a laundry and grocery subscription delivery company after university, and went on to work at the Royal Bank of Canada helping invest a $900M discretionary wealth portfolio. The same profile says he joined GTMfund seven months into the fund's journey and leads the GTM platform, community, portfolio company support programs, investment and LP relations. His Startupfest speaker profile describes him as the fund's first full-time employee. He is the bylined author of GTMfund's own investment announcements for Eightball AI and Examen, and is quoted as General Partner in the Examen post. No join year, IPO count, exit count or current board seat could be sourced.
