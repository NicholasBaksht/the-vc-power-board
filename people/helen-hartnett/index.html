Biography

Helen Hartnett is listed as Managing Partner on the SVG Ventures team page and as Chief Operating Officer on THRIVE's team page and in the media contact blocks of the firm's press releases. Her bio describes her as a technology and operations executive with more than twenty years of international experience in the technology industry across business planning, supply chain, new products, program management and global operations, with prior roles at Intel, Intuit, Novell, Lucent Technologies, AST Computer and Digital Equipment. She is also stated to be Executive Director of the Irish Technology Leadership Group and to head its Women in Technology group in Silicon Valley. She holds an honours degree in Business Studies from the University of Limerick and is CPIM certified. No investment is attributed to her personally in the firm's announcements; she appears in them only as the media contact, which is not evidence of deal involvement.
