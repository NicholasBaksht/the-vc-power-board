Biography

Pietro Bezza is a co-founder and Managing Partner of Connect Ventures, the London seed firm, and has been a designated member of Connect Ventures LLP since its incorporation on 24 August 2011. His firm bio describes him as "a founder turned VC, focusing predominantly on B2B software". Before Connect Ventures he was co-founder and CEO of Neo Network, a mobile communication platform started in 2001 and acquired in 2008, and before that spent five years in product and marketing management at Kraft Foods. His firm bio states that he "led the seed round and serves on the board of category defining companies like Typeform, TrueLayer, Oyster, Purple Dot, Aikido and Plain". Companies House records show he resigned as a director of TrueLayer Limited on 4 March 2021, of TrueLayer Group Holdings Limited on 17 September 2021 and of Purple Dot Technologies Limited on 4 April 2024, so those board seats are former rather than current, and his only current UK directorships are Connect Ventures fund and general partner entities plus a residential management company. No education is published by the firm.
