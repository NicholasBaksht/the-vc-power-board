Biography

Jean-Francois Formela is a Partner at Atlas Venture, which he joined in 1993 to establish the firm's U.S. life sciences practice, and focuses on novel drug discovery technologies and therapeutics. He co-founded Intellia Therapeutics, serving on its board from May 2014 until June 2023, and co-founded Korro Bio and IFM Therapeutics. He currently serves on the boards of IFM Therapeutics, Korro Bio, Antares Therapeutics and Triveni Bio. He holds an M.D. from Paris University School of Medicine and an M.B.A. from Columbia University.
