Biography

Amy Schulman is a managing partner at Polaris Partners, which she joined in 2014, focusing on healthcare companies and early stage biotech start-ups. In 2017 she co-founded the Polaris Innovation Fund, which she continues to manage alongside her role as a managing partner of the Polaris main funds. Before Polaris she worked at Pfizer as general counsel and then as president of both Pfizer Nutrition and Pfizer Consumer Healthcare, practised law as a partner at DLA Piper and began her career in litigation at Cleary Gottlieb Steen & Hamilton in 1991. She chairs the board of Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, sits on the board of Elevance Health, and joined Harvard Business School's faculty as a Senior Lecturer in July 2014.
