Biography

Shuichiro Kawamura (河村 修一郎) founded Energy & Environment Investment, Inc. in 2006 and is its President. The firm's own members page states he joined the Industrial Bank of Japan in 1989 and from 1996 covered the power and energy sectors as an analyst in its Industry Research Division, serving on advisory bodies to the Ministry of International Trade and Industry including the Electric Utility Industry Council. In 2000 he moved to IBJ Securities, now Mizuho Securities, where he ran corporate finance for energy-sector companies, and in 2003 he co-founded Industrial Decisions Inc. with the US consulting firm Strategic Decisions Group, serving as CEO for three years before founding EEI. He holds a degree in law from the University of Tokyo and an MPA from the Woodrow Wilson School at Princeton University. He is named as 代表 or 代表取締役 of the firm in its own materials from 2018 through its July 2026 rebranding announcement. No source was found tying him individually to a named portfolio investment or to a current board seat.
