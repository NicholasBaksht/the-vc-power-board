Biography

Chun Xia is a Founding Partner of TSVC, which he co-founded with Eugene Zhang in 2010 as TEEC Angel Fund. The TSVC team page records him as a co-founder of brightinfo.com, Thinkmart and Mash5, and as an Architect at Sun Microsystems. He is named on the Form D filings of TSVC Fund V LP and TSVC Fund VI, LP as an executive officer, a manager of the investment manager TSVC Management LLC and a managing member of the fund general partner. Chinese-language coverage records him as 夏淳 and states that he and Eugene Zhang were classmates and dormitory roommates at Tsinghua University; no degree or graduation year could be sourced from a first-party page, so education is left empty.
