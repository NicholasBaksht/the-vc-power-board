Biography

Ian Hogarth is a co-founder of Plural. He co-founded Songkick in London at age 25, raised over 20 million dollars in venture funding, was part of the second British company backed by Y Combinator and grew the site to 12 million monthly visitors before Songkick's litigation against Ticketmaster settled two weeks before trial. He chairs the UK AI Security Institute, formerly the AI Safety Institute, having previously chaired the Frontier AI Taskforce, and the UK government records that he continues his duties to Plural while recusing himself from investment decisions on companies in scope of the Institute. His listed investments at Plural include Proxima Fusion, Oriole, Hypersonica, Phasecraft and Callosum.
