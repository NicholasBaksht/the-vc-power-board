Biography

John Onwualu is an Investment Partner at Flourish Ventures focused on financial infrastructure and the systems that underpin global finance. He was previously a Managing Director at the U.S. International Development Finance Corporation, where he built and led the agency's first direct equity investment platform deploying growth capital into emerging-markets companies. Before that he worked at SoFi, launching new businesses, executing acquisitions and helping prepare the company for its public listing. Earlier he traded emerging-markets sovereign and corporate debt at Morgan Stanley and advised on M&A and capital raises. Flourish lists his current investments as Borderless, CipherOwl, Clerkie, CurbWaste, Spade and Tesouro, with SeedFi as an exited investment. He holds a B.S. in Finance from Wharton with a minor in African Studies.
