Biography

Matthew Cowan is a General Partner at N47, working across the firm's Applications and Infrastructure categories from Silicon Valley and Israel. His N47 profile says he joined in 2017 and focuses on AI applied at the application layer to bring automation and efficiency to underserved industries, citing BuildOps as the clearest expression of that thesis. He co-founded Breezeworks, a technology automation company for service professionals, and earlier worked at Intel. He describes having watched three major product cycles over three decades. N47 does not publish his education or board seats.
