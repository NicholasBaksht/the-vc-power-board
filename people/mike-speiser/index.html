Biography

Mike Speiser is a managing director at Sutter Hill Ventures, where since 2008 he has driven the firm's incubation-style investing — personally serving as founding chief executive of both Pure Storage and Snowflake before handing each to permanent leadership. He previously held product, marketing and executive roles at Hewlett-Packard, F5 Networks and Symantec.

Previous Experience

  • Vice President and technical advisor to the CEO, Symantec
  • Vice President of Marketing, F5 Networks
  • Product management and marketing, Hewlett-Packard

Career Timeline
