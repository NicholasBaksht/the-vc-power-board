Sam Englebardt

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Sam Englebardt is listed on the Galaxy Interactive team page as Founding General Partner of Galaxy Interactive, the interactive-technology venture arm of Galaxy Digital. iHeartMedia's March 2022 announcement of his appointment to its board describes him as General Partner of Galaxy Interactive and states that prior to Galaxy Digital he was a Partner and Managing Director at Lambert Media Group, where he helped source and oversaw investments in Rave Cinemas (sold to Cinemark), Gold Class Cinemas (sold to iPic) and Demarest Films and managed a portfolio of early-stage media tech venture investments; that he previously served as Vice President and Financial Advisor at AllianceBernstein and is a licensed attorney in California; and that he earned his J.D. from Harvard Law School and studied philosophy, political science and economics at Oxford University and the University of Colorado at Boulder. Galaxy Interactive's team-page biographies are behind a "Read Bio" control that did not render through the fetcher, so no further first-party detail was retrievable.
