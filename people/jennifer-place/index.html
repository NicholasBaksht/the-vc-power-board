Biography

Jennifer Place was identified as a Principal at Munich Re Ventures in the firm's 21 May 2025 announcement of a new $125 million fund from HSB, which stated that she would oversee the HSB Funds and lead investments for the Built World sector. In June 2025 she co-authored Munich Re Ventures' post announcing the firm's participation in Runwise's Series B round for its smart operating system for buildings. Munich Re Ventures' team page did not list her when checked in August 2026, and Munich Re had said in late 2025 that it would wind the venture unit down by mid-2026.
