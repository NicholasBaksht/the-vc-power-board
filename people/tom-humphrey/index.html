Biography

Tom Humphrey is a Partner at Blackbird, based in Sydney. Blackbird's post of 1 December 2020 announced that he had joined the firm's investment team as a Principal. His Blackbird profile states that he spent eight years operating before investing: he joined OurDeal, a Sydney direct-to-consumer ecommerce company that exited to News Corp, then moved to the United States in 2011 to launch Kanopy, a B2B video streaming company, with his sister, where he was Chief Operating Officer and which exited to private equity; he also spent about two years as a venture capital analyst at Access Ventures. He is the named author or co-author of Blackbird's investment announcements for Marqo, Lorikeet, Springboards and Alloy. No board seat is recorded. Blackbird's investment announcement posts, twenty-four of which were read on 7 September 2026, name the authoring investor but contain no sentence appointing anyone to a portfolio company board, and no portfolio company board page naming this person was found.
