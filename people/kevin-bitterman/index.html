Biography

Kevin Bitterman is a Partner at Atlas Venture, which he joined in 2017 after thirteen years on the life science team at Polaris Partners, and focuses on creating and investing in companies that translate science into medicines. He was the founding CEO of Editas Medicine, Morphic Therapeutic and Visterra. He currently serves on the boards of Disc Medicine, Judo Bio, Kinaset Therapeutics, nChroma Bio, Remix Therapeutics and Renasant Bio. He holds a Ph.D. in genetics from Harvard Medical School and a B.A. in biology from Rutgers University.
