Biography

Ewan MacKinnon is Managing Partner of Maven Capital Partners, with responsibility for all aspects of the firm's business including strategy, new investment activity and client management. His Maven profile says he has over 25 years of experience managing, advising and investing in SMEs, that he joined Maven in 2009 after working in Johnston Carmichael's corporate finance team, and that his industry background includes serving as managing director of MacKinnons of Dyce Limited, a specialist retail business acquired by a FTSE 250 listed company in 2006. He is principal Fund Manager for the Maven VCTs with oversight of investment strategy for late seed and Series A investments, and leads the Maven Investment Committee. He is based in Glasgow and Edinburgh. No education is published on his Maven profile.
