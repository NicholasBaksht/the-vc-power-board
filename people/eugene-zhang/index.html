Eugene Zhang

Founding Partner at TSVC · Joined 2010

Biography

Eugene Zhang is a Founding Partner of TSVC, the Silicon Valley seed firm he co-founded with Chun Xia in 2010 as TEEC Angel Fund and which was renamed TSVC in 2018. The TSVC team page records him as Founder/CEO of InnoSpring and JEDA and a founding team member at Cisco and Juniper. A Forbes profile of 5 December 2022 headlined him as an early venture capital investor in Zoom and states “His firm, TSVC, was the first institutional investor in the initial round of funding for Zoom in 2011. Its check for $250,000 garnered a 200x return.” The same article describes the firm at that time as “a small team of investors composed of four partners Zhang, Chun Xia, James He and Michael Jin”. He is named on the Form D filings of TSVC Fund V LP and TSVC Fund VI, LP as an executive officer, a manager of the investment manager TSVC Management LLC and a managing member of the fund general partner. Chinese-language coverage records him as 张于庆. No degrees or institutions could be sourced, and no current board seat could be verified against any portfolio-company filing.
