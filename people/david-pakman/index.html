Biography

David Pakman is listed on the CoinFund team page as Managing Partner, Head of Venture Investing. CoinFund's September 2021 announcement of his appointment states that he spent thirteen years at Venrock, where he led the firm's crypto investing efforts, that he led the Series A and B rounds of Dollar Shave Club and served on its board until its $1 billion acquisition, that he led the Series A of Dapper Labs and serves on its board, that he led the Series A of Rarible, and that he was a personal pre-IPO investor in Coinbase. He is named as Managing Partner and Head of Venture Investing in CoinFund's August 2022 announcement of the $300M CoinFund Ventures I fund, and he authored CoinFund's investment announcement for Raad Labs and two of the firm's 2025 insights pieces. The Dapper Labs board seat is stated in a 2021 source and was not re-confirmed against a current first-party page, so no board seat is recorded.
