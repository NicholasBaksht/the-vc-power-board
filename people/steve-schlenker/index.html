Biography

Steve Schlenker co-founded DN Capital in June 2000 and is listed on the firm's site as Managing Partner, based in its Sand Hill Road office and running DN Capital's US operations. He began his career in venture capital and private equity in 1990, and before DN Capital was Chief Investment Officer for technology investments at SUN Capital Partners in London and an analyst at Interlaken Capital in Greenwich, Connecticut. DN Capital states that his investment experience has been in software and software related businesses, with sector focus on enterprise software and, to a lesser extent, business online marketplaces and consumer mobile services. He holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and a BSc in Economics from the Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania.
