Biography

Martin Casado is a general partner at Andreessen Horowitz, where he leads the firm's infrastructure practice, having joined in 2016. He holds a PhD and a master's degree in computer science from Stanford University, helped pioneer software-defined networking as a Stanford researcher, and previously worked at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory. He cofounded Illuminics Systems, acquired by Quova in 2006, and Nicira, where he was CTO until VMware acquired it for $1.26 billion in 2012, after which he served as a Fellow and senior vice president at VMware leading networking and security. He has received the ACM Grace Murray Hopper Award and the NEC C&C Award, and sits on boards including Convex, Cursor, Fivetran, Kong, Netlify and World Labs.
