Biography

Richard Rubino is an Operating Partner on Deerfield's Healthcare Services team and joined the firm in 2026. Before Deerfield he spent over two years as Chief Financial Officer at VillageMD, and before that nearly two years as EVP and Chief Financial Officer at Cedar Gate Technologies, Inc. He was Chief Financial Officer at Aerie Pharmaceuticals, Inc. for nearly nine years. Earlier he spent 19 years at Medco Health Solutions, Inc., most recently as Chief Financial Officer, and before Medco ten years at International Business Machines Corporation in accounting and finance management roles. He began his career at Price Waterhouse & Co. and holds a B.S. in Accounting from Manhattan College.
