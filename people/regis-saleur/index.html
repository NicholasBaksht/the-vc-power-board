Biography

Régis Saleur is Managing Partner of Supernova Invest. The firm's team page states: "Régis has been part of the team since 2008 as Managing Partner. He has more than 25 years of experience in private equity and operations." His stated 2008 start predates the 2017 creation of Supernova Invest and reflects tenure with the team that came from CEA Investissement. He was named as General Director of Supernova Invest in November 2021 coverage of the Ambition Industrie fund. No education, prior employers, portfolio board seats, IPO count or exit count could be sourced.
