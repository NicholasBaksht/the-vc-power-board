Biography

Justin Hall is a partner at Golden Gate Ventures, the Singapore-headquartered venture capital firm. His firm bio states that he joined Golden Gate Ventures as its first intern in 2012 and was promoted to partner in 2018. Before joining the firm he was a scholar at the National University of Singapore's Lee Kuan Yew School of Public Policy, where he took a Masters in Public Policy, and earlier read history and political science with honours at Trinity College Dublin. Golden Gate Ventures lists companies including Carousell, Homage and Locofy on his profile page.
