Harley Miller

Managing Partner & CEO at Left Lane Capital · Joined 2019

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Harley Miller founded Left Lane Capital in 2019 and serves as its Chief Executive Officer, a statement made in EquipmentShare.com Inc's Form 8-K exhibit of 10 June 2026. That filing also states that before founding Left Lane he spent nine years as a Principal at Insight Venture Management, LLP. He is named as the sole related person on the Forms D for Left Lane Capital Partners I LP (filed March 2020) and Left Lane Capital Partners III LP (filed July 2025). He was appointed to the board of directors of EquipmentShare.com Inc, which trades on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker EQPT, effective 8 June 2026. Left Lane's own team page lists his title as Managing Partner and CEO.
