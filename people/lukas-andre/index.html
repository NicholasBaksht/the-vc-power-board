Biography

Lukas Andre is a General Partner at Redstone and states on his Redstone profile page that he is responsible for the firm's activities in Switzerland, having joined in 2016. He began his career as a startup founder in the early internet era and then moved into investing at an investment boutique in Zurich and Geneva. His stated interests include quantum computing applications, logistics and supply chain optimisation and pharmaceutical discovery. Redstone's own September 2023 announcement of the Redstone Quantum Fund describes him as Managing Partner of Redstone Switzerland, which differs from the General Partner title the firm's current team page gives him. No education, board seats or attributed portfolio companies are published for him.
