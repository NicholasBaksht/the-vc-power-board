Biography

Will Nuelle is General Partner of Galaxy Ventures. The Galaxy Ventures team page states that he focuses on early-stage investments in protocol layer infrastructure, DeFi applications and software products, that he started at Galaxy in Research where he developed quantitative risk software for trading, that before Galaxy he built incentive simulations for the Ethereum protocol FOAM as an intern, and that he holds B.S. degrees from Stanford University in Mathematics and Architecture. He is the named author of Galaxy Ventures' announcements of both closes of Galaxy Ventures Fund I and of several of the firm's "Backing" investment posts. No joining year, board seat, IPO count or exit count was found in a first-party source.
