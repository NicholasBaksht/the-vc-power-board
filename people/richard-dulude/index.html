Biography

Richard Dulude co-founded the Boston venture capital firm Underscore VC in 2015 and is listed on its team page as Venture Partner. Before Underscore he was an associate at Founder Collective, started his career as a quant at Goldman Sachs and built the team that launched the direct-to-consumer eCommerce business at Procter & Gamble. He co-authored Underscore's investment announcements for Orion Security, Terra Security and Hypernatural in 2025. He holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and a Master of Engineering and a Bachelor of Science in Engineering from Cornell University.
