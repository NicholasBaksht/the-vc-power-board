Biography

Alex Fayette is a Partner at ACME Capital, based in San Francisco. He joined the firm in 2016 and was promoted to Partner in 2022, and has spent over a decade at ACME working with founders on technically demanding ventures. His expertise spans aerospace and defense, next-generation computing, tech bio, physical AI, energy and raw materials. He is recognised for communicating advanced technology to commercial audiences and for identifying risks and opportunities across emerging technology domains. He holds a B.A. in Economics from Yale University, where he was a Whiffenpoof, and previously worked in the investment banking group at J.P. Morgan. He sits on the boards of Akash, AviaGames, Laurel, Replika, Sphere Semi, True Anomaly and Muon Space.
