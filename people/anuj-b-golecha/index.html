Biography

Anuj B Golecha is listed as Co-Founder on the Venture Catalysts team page, where his name is rendered "CA Anuj - B Golecha". His profile on the Beams VC site describes him as a seasoned investor, a qualified Chartered Accountant and Co-Founder & Sponsor Partner at Beams Fintech Fund, with over 20 years of experience across entrepreneurship, investment and advisory, and states that he is the co-founder of Venture Catalysts. He was quoted as Co-founder and partner of Beams Fintech Fund in Business Standard's April 2022 report of that fund's Rs 270 crore first close, and as Cofounder of Venture Catalysts in Outlook Business's September 2025 report of the firm's Rs 150 crore fundraise. Forbes India names him as one of the four co-founders of 9Unicorns in August 2019. No board seat is stated by any source found.
