Biography

Jesse Morris is listed as a General Partner on Human Ventures' team page. He founded Very in 2012, a company that helped non-technical founders launch software businesses, and held an operating role there until 2018 before moving into investing. He joined Human Ventures in 2019. His investments include Spora Health, Kingdom Supercultures and Upstream, and he looks for founders with technical depth or another deeply differentiated skill. He is also an autism advocate who works with families and founders in that space. Human Ventures does not publish his education.
