Biography

Amit Somani is Managing Partner at Prime Venture Partners, an early stage firm in Bangalore. He was previously Chief Products Officer at MakeMyTrip, where Prime's bio says he took part in the company's 2010 NASDAQ IPO, directed teams working on search, mobile and advertising products at Google, and headed Enterprise Search and Discovery at IBM in San Jose. He holds a B.S. in computer science from the Indian Institute of Technology Varanasi and a master's in computer science from the University of Wisconsin, Madison. In September 2025 he co-authored Prime's announcement of its $5 million Series A investment in Klaar and was quoted on the round in trade press.
