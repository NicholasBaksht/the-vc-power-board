Michel Akkermans

Chairman at Volta Ventures

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Michel Akkermans is listed on Volta Ventures' site as Chairman, alongside managing partners Frank Maene and Sander Vonk. Reporting on the firm's second fund in 2019 described him as a serial entrepreneur and investor continuing in his role as chairman of the shareholder advisory board and venture partner, a governance role rather than a day to day investing seat. He founded FICS in 1989 and led it as Chairman and CEO until its 1999 merger into S1 Corporation, where he was Executive Chairman, and in 2001 co-founded Clear2Pay, which FIS agreed to acquire in 2014 for 375 million euros. Volta Ventures' site publishes no biography for him and attributes no investments to him.

Education

  • Postgraduate degree in Economics & Finance, University of Leuven

  • Investor & Executive Chairman, Data4S (acquired by Norkom in 2004)
  • Executive advisor, FIS

Career Timeline

1989
Founded FICS, an online banking and regulatory financial reporting software provider, serving as Chairman and CEO.
1999
FICS merged with Edify and Vertical One to create S1 Corporation, where he became Executive Chairman.
2001
Co-founded Clear2Pay, a Brussels-headquartered payments technology company, as Chairman and CEO.
2014
FIS agreed to acquire Clear2Pay for 375 million euros, when he was co-founder, chief executive and chairman.
2019
Continued as chairman of Volta Ventures' shareholder advisory board and venture partner as the firm launched its second fund.
