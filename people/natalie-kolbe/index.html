Biography

Natalie Kolbe is the Managing Partner of Norrsken22, responsible for overall management of the fund, based in Johannesburg. She spent 18 years at Actis, most recently as global head of private equity overseeing eight funds across Africa, Latin America, India, China and Southeast Asia with roughly $2.5 billion in assets under management. She is a CFA charterholder.

Education

  • BComm, University of Witwatersrand
  • MBA, Wits Business School

Previous Experience
