Biography

Kentaro Matsui is a Managing Partner at SoftBank Investment Advisers, based in the firm's Tokyo office, where his bio states he focuses on investment opportunities in Asia. Before his current role he worked at Mizuho Securities, where he advised on SoftBank's investments in Vodafone Japan, Sprint and Arm. Reporting in 2020 described him as concentrating on Asia-based financial, healthcare and logistics technology deals, with Didi Chuxing, Manbang Group and Ping An Healthcare Management among his portfolio. He has since been quoted for the Vision Fund on investments including AI Medical Service and QuEra Computing.
