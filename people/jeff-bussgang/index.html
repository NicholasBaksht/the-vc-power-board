Biography

Jeff Bussgang co-founded Flybridge and is a General Partner focused on agentic business applications, vertical SaaS and fintech. He is a Senior Lecturer of Business Administration at Harvard Business School, where he teaches Launching Technology Ventures. Before venture capital he co-founded Upromise, serving as President and COO until its acquisition by Sallie Mae, and was an executive at Open Market, which went public in 1996. He began his career at The Boston Consulting Group. He leads Flybridge's Graduate Syndicate pre-seed fund for Harvard Business School graduates.
