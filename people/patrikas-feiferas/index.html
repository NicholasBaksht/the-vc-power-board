Biography

Patrikas Feiferas is a Principal at Contrarian Ventures, based in London. His firm bio states he joined in 2020 and leads the firm's UK expansion while working closely with portfolio companies. His specialism is distributed energy resources, covering financing, deployment, connectivity and optimisation. He actively supports Beebop, Starflow, Jumptech and Metafuels, and has contributed to firm platform initiatives including Climate50 and the Energy Tech Summit. He holds a BSc in Finance from ISM University of Management and Economics and studied at Singapore Management University and Shantou University. He previously worked at SEB Bank and Telia.
