Biography

Cheryl Cheng is Managing Partner at M12. She has spent 16 years investing in early- to growth-stage software companies ranging from healthcare to enterprise applications. Before M12, she founded and led Vive Collective and was a General Partner at BlueRun Ventures. She also worked in brand management at Clorox and Sharper Image and in technology M&A at Broadview International.
