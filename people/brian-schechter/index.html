Biography

Brian Schechter is a Partner (Infrastructure) at Primary Venture Partners. He joined Primary in 2020 after co-founding and serving as CEO of consumer brand startup SelfMade (2015–2020). He is a two-time founder turned investor who has led investments in AI infrastructure companies, including co-leading the Seed round for AI hardware firm Etched.
