Biography

Osman Kent is a Venture Partner at Atlantic Bridge. His Atlantic Bridge bio states that he is a serial technology and media entrepreneur best known as the founder and CEO of 3Dlabs, described on the page as a fabless chip company whose products were used in the making of films including Titanic and The Matrix. The bio states he is a Fellow of the Royal Society of Arts in the UK, holds a First Class degree in Computer Science and Electronics Engineering from the University of Birmingham, and is the inventor of numerous patents in computing and graphics. No Atlantic Bridge investment is attributed to him by any source read, and no board seat could be confirmed.
