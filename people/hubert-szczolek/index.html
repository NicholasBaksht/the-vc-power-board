Biography

Hubert Szczołek is Partner and CFO at Balnord, responsible for LP reporting, treasury, accounting, portfolio valuation and financial due diligence. He was previously CFO at Black Pearls VC from 2015, where he structured the fund and led financial planning and analysis, reporting and due diligence, and before that held pre-seed investment and consulting roles involving capital raising and strategic planning. He has guided more than 20 companies through financial planning and supported more than 10 through Series A-B rounds and exits, and he serves on portfolio company boards. Five of his Black Pearls investments have exited: Nanoxo, Prosoma, VoiceLab, Xopero and Inpharmica. He holds a degree in Finance and Accounting from the Warsaw School of Economics.
