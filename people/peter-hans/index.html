Biography

Peter Hans is Partner and Global Head of Business Development at Hack VC. The firm's team page states he was previously head of digital assets product and strategy for Fidelity Asset Management and chief strategy officer for Arca Investment Management, and that he has more than 25 years in investment management, fintech and capital markets. It records a B.A. from Colby College and an MBA from Vanderbilt. He co-authored Hack VC's Berachain Series A post and writes the firm's 'Cycle Tested' research series.
