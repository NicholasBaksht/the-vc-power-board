Biography

Anamitra Banerji is listed on Afore Capital's own site as co-founder and managing partner of the firm, which invests at pre-seed. He and Gaurav Jain were named as managing members of the general partner of Afore Capital, L.P. in that fund's Form D filed with the SEC in August 2016. A 2015 Form D filed by Kik Interactive Inc. names him as a director of that company.
