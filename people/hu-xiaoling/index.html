Hu Xiaoling (胡晓玲)

Managing Partner, Private Equity / Head of PE (创始合伙人、管理合伙人，私募股权投资) at CDH Investments · Joined 2002

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Hu Xiaoling is a founding partner of CDH Investments and Managing Partner and head of its Private Equity business, which invests in consumer, healthcare, hard technology and business services and new infrastructure. CDH's team pages say she joined CICC's private equity group as a Senior Associate in 1999 and remained there until co-founding CDH in 2002, and that from 1995 to 1999 she was a Senior Auditor at Arthur Andersen providing audit and business advisory services to listed and foreign-invested companies. She holds an M.S. in Economics and a B.S. from Northern Jiaotong University and is a member of the Association of Chartered Certified Accountants of the United Kingdom and a qualified Securities Certified CPA.
