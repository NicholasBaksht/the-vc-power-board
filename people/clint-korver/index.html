Clint Korver

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Clint Korver is a cofounder and Managing Director of Ulu Ventures, a seed-stage venture capital firm he co-founded with Miriam Rivera, and his Ulu bio also gives his title as CIO. Before venture capital he co-founded four companies that trained executives in decision analysis and built software for data-driven decision making, and he co-authored Ethics for the Real World with Stanford professor Ron Howard. He has taught entrepreneurship at Stanford's School of Engineering, mentored at StartX and co-founded Stanford Angels and Entrepreneurs. In 2023 he was quoted on Ulu's lead investment in Pontoro's Seed II round, and in 2025 he was named among Ulu's leaders when the firm announced a 208 million dollar fourth fund.
