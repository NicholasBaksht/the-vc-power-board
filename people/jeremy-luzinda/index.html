Biography

Jeremy Luzinda is a Partner at Haatch, where his Haatch biography says he leads the investment and portfolio teams and drives the firm's investments and M&A, and that since joining in 2021 he designed the investment strategy behind the firm's pre-seed and seed funds. Before Haatch he was Chief Operating Officer of a VC-backed startup funded by Forward Partners, Founders Factory and angels including Sir John Hegarty, then Managing Director of a university spinout co-founded by Peter Saunders OBE built on patented cold-storage technology, then worked at Playfair Capital, where his biography says he worked the pre-seed rounds of Omnipresent, Nory and Protex AI. His biography states that AeroCloud brought him to Haatch, which backed its pre-seed alongside Playfair, and that he led Haatch's investment into Data & AI Literacy Academy. He was quoted as Principal at Haatch in the firm's CertChain announcement and Haatch separately published a note on his promotion to Principal, so his Partner title is more recent than both. His biography also states he is an active angel investor. No education, board seat, IPO count or exit count could be sourced.
