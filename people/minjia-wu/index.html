Biography

Minjia Wu is a Partner at Orbit Ventures, which her profile describes as a venture capital fund focused on high-growth opportunities in emerging markets. She was previously a Partner at SOSV, where her profile states she oversaw a portfolio of over 30 companies, deploying US$16.5M in funding, and that those companies raised more than US$102M in follow-on funding. Her stated focus covers the United States, Southeast Asia and India, with core expertise in media and logistics. Her Orbit profile states verbatim: "She is a Board Member at mobility platform Pandabus and entertainment firm Two Four Seven Group, and a Board Observer at insurtech company The CareVoice." The CareVoice is recorded as an investment rather than a board seat because a board observer role is not a board seat.
