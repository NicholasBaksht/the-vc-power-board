Biography

Khaled Helioui is a co-founder of Plural. He joined the games company Bigpoint as Chief Games Officer in 2012, taking over a 450 strong department, and stepped up as Chief Executive Officer within months to rebuild the company's vision, leadership and board as social and mobile gaming rose. His listed investments at Plural include Helsing, The Exploration Company, Augur and Isometric, and he wrote Plural's public investment notes on The Exploration Company, Augur and Isometric.
