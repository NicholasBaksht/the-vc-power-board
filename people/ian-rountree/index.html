Ian Rountree

General Partner at Cantos · Joined 2016

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Ian Rountree founded Cantos in 2016 to invest in technology outside the areas Silicon Valley venture firms were then funding, on the view that hardware and biology were underserved. Before Cantos he was on the early teams at Invisible Technologies and SoFi. He has told the press he is not technical by background and holds a bachelor's degree in international relations. He raised the firm's $50M third fund in 2022 and a $70M Fund IV that closed in 2026, investing at pre-seed and seed in physical-world companies across defense, aerospace, energy, materials and biotech.
