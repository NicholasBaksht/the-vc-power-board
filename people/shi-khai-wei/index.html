Biography

Shi Khai Wei is Co-Founder and Managing Partner of LongHash Ventures, the title printed on the firm's About page. Forbes reported in October 2022 that the firm was founded by Emma Cui and “Shi Khai, her ex-McKinsey colleague”, which is the only prior employer sourced for him from an acceptable publication.
