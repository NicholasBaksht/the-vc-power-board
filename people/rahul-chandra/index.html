Biography

Rahul Chandra is a co-founder and Managing Director of Arkam Ventures. He has more than twenty years in venture capital, having co-founded Helion Venture Partners, one of India's homegrown VC firms, and having started out at Walden in Silicon Valley in 1997. He left Helion and launched the successor vehicle in 2017 under the name Unitary Helion, which was rebranded Arkam Ventures at the debut fund's first close in June 2020. His prior and current investments referenced by the firm and press include Gupshup, MoEngage, IntrCity, Equitas, KreditBee, Jar and Skyroot. He studied in BITS Pilani's Master of Management Studies programme.
