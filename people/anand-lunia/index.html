Biography

Anand Lunia is a founding partner of India Quotient. An MBA from IIM Lucknow, he began his career in banking at ICICI and finance at Asian Paints before co-founding the ed-tech venture Brainvisa Technologies in 2000, which he sold in 2005. He was then a partner at Seedfund, known for exits including CarWale and Redbus, before co-founding India Quotient with Madhukar Sinha.
