Biography

Brent Hoberman CBE is co-founder and managing partner of Firstminute Capital. He co-founded and led lastminute.com from 1998, selling it to Sabre in 2005 for $1.1B, and later co-founded Made.com, leaving before its 2021 listing. He also co-founded Founders Factory and Founders Forum.

Previous Experience

  • Co-Founder & CEO, lastminute.com (1998–2005) — sold to Sabre for $1.1B
  • Co-Founder, Made.com (2010) — departed before its 2021 IPO
  • Co-Founder, Founders Factory
  • Co-Founder, Founders Forum
