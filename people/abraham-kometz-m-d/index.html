Biography

Abraham Kometz, M.D., who Deerfield's profile refers to as Avi Kometz, is a Partner on the Medical Technologies team and joined the firm in 2014. Before Deerfield he was a Partner at Ascent Biomedical Ventures, where he led investment efforts in biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, medical devices and healthcare information technology. Before Ascent he was head of corporate development at a specialty pharmaceutical company focused on pediatrics, and was a member of the Healthcare Practice at McKinsey & Company in New York. He received his medical doctorate from the University of the Witwatersrand Medical School in South Africa, began his career as a clinician in South Africa and founded and built a group of clinics. Adela, Inc.'s Form D filed 23 April 2026 names him as a Director of the company.
