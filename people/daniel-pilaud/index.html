Biography

Daniel Pilaud is a Venture Partner at Elaia. He started as an assistant professor at the Grenoble Institut National Polytechnique and co-managed the Verimag laboratory for nine years during the 1990s. From 1999 to 2007 he was CEO of Polyspace Technologies, which was sold to MathWorks, and he later co-managed IT-Translation between 2011 and 2019. He holds a PhD in computer science from INPG. Elaia lists his expertise as embedded systems and software.
