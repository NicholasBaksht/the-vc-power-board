Biography

Kunal Bahl is a co-founder of Titan Capital, the early stage investing brand he runs with Rohit Bansal, and is listed on the Titan Capital team page with the title CO-FOUNDER. His own Titan Capital biography states that he is co-founder of AceVector Limited, the holding company for Snapdeal, Unicommerce and Stellaro Brands, and that through Titan Capital he "has invested in more than 250 start-ups across various sectors in consumer tech, health-tech, fin-tech, SaaS, consumer brands, and B2B services in companies like Ola Cabs, Urban Company, Razorpay, Ofbusiness, Mamaearth, among others." He co-founded Snapdeal in 2010. He graduated from the Jerome Fisher Program in Management and Technology at the University of Pennsylvania and holds degrees in engineering and business from the Wharton School. He is listed as an Independent Director on the board of Piramal Enterprises Limited on that company's own board of directors page. His Titan Capital biography also lists him as a member of the National Startup Advisory Council, a member of the Board of Governors of the Indian Council for Research on International Economic Relations, and Chairman of the Confederation of Indian Industry Startup Committee; these are councils and committees rather than company boards and are not recorded as board seats.
