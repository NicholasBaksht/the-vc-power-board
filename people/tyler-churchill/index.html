Biography

Tyler Churchill is a Partner at Bonfire Ventures. The firm announced his promotion to Partner in a post dated 30 July 2025 that describes his path from summer intern to Partner. Before venture he worked across the Motion Pictures and Business Development teams at Creative Artists Agency, and had operating roles shaping sales strategy at EverString, a predictive analytics company later acquired by ZoomInfo, and TechValidate, a customer intelligence company later acquired by SurveyMonkey. He attended USC for his undergraduate degree and earned an MBA from The University of Chicago Booth School of Business. He writes that he spends most of his time on industrial and blue-collar technology, including hardware-centric and hardware-enabled companies, and on fintech and the future of financial services and capital markets. He is based in North Bend, Washington.
