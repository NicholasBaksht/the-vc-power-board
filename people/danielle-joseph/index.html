Biography

Danielle Joseph is Managing Director and Head of Closed Loop Ventures at Closed Loop Partners, overseeing the platform's venture capital and growth equity strategies, a portfolio of more than 40 early-stage circular economy companies, and an investment team spanning origination, diligence and portfolio management. She led both the investment in and exit of composter Atlas Organics, and serves on the boards of SOLARCYCLE, Capra Biosciences, HomeBiogas and the nonprofit Billion Oyster Project. She previously worked in growth equity and project finance at NewWorld Capital Group, evaluated clean energy opportunities at Invex Infraestructura in Mexico, and assessed energy infrastructure at Queensland Investment Corporation. She holds an MBA from Stanford Graduate School of Business and a BA from Pomona College.
