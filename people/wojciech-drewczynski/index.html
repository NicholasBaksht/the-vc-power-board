Biography

Wojciech Drewczyński is Head of Research at Balnord, responsible for sourcing, investments and building the firm's brand recognition across the European market, with a focus on identifying companies through accelerators and his founder network in Poland and the Baltics. In 2015 he co-founded Space3ac, an international accelerator that connected 160 startups from 14 countries with 20 major corporations. He was previously a Principal at Black Pearls VC, where he led six investment deals with a majority concentration in DeepTech and SpaceTech. His portfolio there includes Ondato (Lithuania), Wordware (USA, Y Combinator S24), Surveilly, Deep-image.ai, Traxlo, Fluentbe and infoShare Academy. He is the author of a bestselling book on corporate-startup collaboration and is recognised as a LinkedIn Top Venture Capital Voice.
