Biography

Christian Schütz is a Partner at b2venture. The firm's team page states he is responsible for managing its Industrial Technologies Fund and its biotech and health-related investments, and that he is a member of b2venture's Board of Directors. It says he joined in 2003 while completing a PhD focused on venture capital and angel investing, helped develop term sheets and structure the firm's first two dedicated funds, led fundraising in healthcare, renewable energy and biotechnology, and has actively managed more than 35 investments. His PhD in business administration and finance is from EBS Universität für Wirtschaft und Recht. The firm's imprint lists him, as Dr. Christian Schütz, as one of two managing directors of b2venture AG alongside Florian Schweitzer.
