Biography

Santosh Sankar is a co-founder and Managing Partner of Dynamo Ventures, the Chattanooga based pre-seed and seed fund for supply chain and the physical economy. Before Dynamo he spent five years on Wall Street in banking and equity research covering the insurance and technology sectors. An entrepreneur from an early age, he founded Razor Studios, an IT support and web development firm, at 13, and co-founded the financial media publication Bullish Bankers while at Penn State, where he earned a BS in Business and managed technology investments for the Nittany Lion Fund. He was named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 for venture capital in 2017, became a Kauffman Fellow in 2021, and serves as a Commissioner of the Chattanooga Metropolitan Airport Authority.
