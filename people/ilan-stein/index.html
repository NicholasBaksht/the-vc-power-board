Biography

Ilan Stein is a Principal at Viola Growth, having joined the fund as an Associate in November 2024. He brings over six years of investment banking experience, most recently as an Investment Banking Associate in Goldman Sachs' Israel office advising on M&A, equity and debt transactions. Before that he managed an investment banking team at KPMG focused on M&A advisory for Israeli companies, and worked as a financial planning and analysis analyst at PeerSpot. He holds a BA from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, an MBA from Tel Aviv University, and is a Certified Public Accountant.
