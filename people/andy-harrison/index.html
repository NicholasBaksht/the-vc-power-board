Biography

Andy Harrison is CEO and General Partner of S32 with a background in healthcare and deep tech. He co-founded Verily (Alphabet’s healthcare company) and held executive roles at GoogleX and Verily Ventures. He joined S32 in 2021 and was named CEO in 2023. He holds degrees from McGill University and Cornell University.
