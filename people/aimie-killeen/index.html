Biography

Aimie Killeen was appointed Chief Legal Officer of Digital Currency Group in an announcement dated 25 April 2024, with a start date of 1 May 2024. She joined from Billtrust, where she was Chief Legal Officer, and was previously Executive Vice President and General Counsel at Cardtronics. Earlier in her career she worked at Ashurst Australia. No investment is attributed to her individually by any source located, and no education could be sourced.
