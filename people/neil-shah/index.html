Biography

Neil Shah is a partner at Greenoaks. Greenoaks publishes no team page or partner biographies, so the record for him rests on financing announcements. A May 2025 press release announcing a 75 million dollar Series A round in the nutrition brand David, led by Greenoaks with participation from Valor Equity Partners, identifies him as a Greenoaks partner and quotes him on the investment. He is a different person from Greenoaks co-founder Neil Mehta.
