Biography

Heather Hartnett is General Partner and CEO of Human Ventures, the New York startup studio and early-stage venture firm she co-founded with Joe Marchese in 2015. She holds a BA in Economics and Business Management from Maharishi University of Management and a certificate in fundraising and development from New York University. Fast Company reported in February 2019 that the firm closed a $50 million debut fund under her leadership. Her investments and portfolio associations include Reserve, Current, theSkimm, Tiny Organics, The Muse and Girlboss. She is a Kauffman Fellow and a founding board member of the David Lynch Foundation.
