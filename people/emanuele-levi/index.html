Biography

Emanuele Levi has been Amministratore Delegato e Direttore Generale (Chief Executive Officer and General Manager) of CDP Venture Capital Sgr S.p.A. since 30 May 2025, per the firm's own appointment release. He holds a degree in Business Administration from Turin and completed a corporate finance programme at London Business School. His career began in 1993 with the Credito Italiano Group in London, followed by Bain & Company and Lazard investment banking in Milan. He has worked in venture capital since 2000, first at Pino Venture and then at 360 Capital, where he was General Partner until 2025.
