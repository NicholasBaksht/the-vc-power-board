Biography

Fergal Mullen is a Partner at Highland Europe. His Highland Europe profile page states that he "Birthed Highland Europe in 2012" and describes him as a Dublin man. The page records that he first heard the expression venture capital at Brown University, departed in 1993 for Harvard Business School, worked at Cambridge Technology Partners, and that in 2001 RSA Security wanted to launch a corporate venture fund. Highland Europe's own about page states the firm spun off from Highland Capital Partners in 2012. Irena Goldenberg's profile page records that he recruited her to launch Highland Capital Partners in Europe in 2007.
