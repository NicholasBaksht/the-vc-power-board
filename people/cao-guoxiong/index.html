Biography

曹国熊 (Cao Guoxiong) is named by Tencent News as 普华集团董事长 (Chairman of Puhua Group) and is reported to have started the business on 8 April 2004 in Hangzhou together with founding managing partner 沈琴华. Tencent News states that consumer and culture investing is run by him together with managing partner 姚臻. NetEase reports only that he studied finance (曹国熊学的是金融); no university, degree or prior employer could be sourced. No board seats, IPO count or exit count could be sourced.

Career Timeline

2004
Starts the business that becomes Puhua on 8 April 2004 in Hangzhou with 沈琴华, under the name 普华投资.
2015
Sets up 头头是道 with financial author 吴晓波 to cover culture and new consumption, per Tencent News.
