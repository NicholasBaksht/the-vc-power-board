David Sainteff

Investment Partner at Global Founders Capital

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

David Sainteff is one of three Investment Partners listed on Global Founders Capital's own website, alongside Cedric Asselman and Fabricio Pettena. In April 2024 he told TechCrunch that the firm would not raise a third fund and would instead deploy 300 million euros from Rocket Internet's balance sheet, saying it was not the best moment to raise funds with limited partners. He described the restructured firm as focused on early stage investments with capacity to follow on into later rounds.
