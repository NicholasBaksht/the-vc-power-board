Biography

Cassie Young is a Partner (GTM Tech) at Primary Venture Partners. She has 15 years of go-to-market leadership experience, including serving as Chief Revenue Officer at Sailthru. She also led a major marketing technology roll-up at private equity firm Marigold. She holds degrees from Duke University and Dartmouth College (Tuck).
