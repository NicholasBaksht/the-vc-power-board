Biography

Eliza Cushman is a Partner at Congruent Ventures managing investments and portfolio support. She was previously an Associate at KKR (origination in private credit) and began her career in leveraged finance at Bank of America. She holds an MBA from Columbia and a B.S. from Wake Forest University. She serves on the board of Ezra Climate.
