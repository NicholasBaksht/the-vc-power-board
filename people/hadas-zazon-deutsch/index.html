Biography

Hadas Zazon Deutsch is an Analyst at Viola Growth. Before joining the fund she was an economic researcher at the OECD, studying how countries support their industries through financial incentives. Earlier she worked in the Macro-Economics and Policy Research Department at the Bank of Israel, covering the macroeconomic effects of COVID-19, multinational company taxation and financial planning for the Israeli metro system. She co-founded First, a mentorship initiative supporting first-generation college students. Her year of joining Viola Growth is not disclosed on the fund's site.
