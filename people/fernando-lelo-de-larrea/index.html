Biography

Fernando Lelo de Larrea co-founded ALLVP in 2012 and served as its Managing Partner until 2021. Across that period his investing spanned foodtech through Robinfood, proptech via Flat, fintech with Flink, climatetech through Enlight and e3, and healthcare through MSC Health. Before ALLVP he held the CFO, COO, CEO and Executive Chairman roles at UmbralCapital and worked in investment banking at Protego Asesores, now Evercore. He holds a bachelor's in economics from ITAM, a master's in economics from the London School of Economics and Political Science and an MBA from Stanford Graduate School of Business, and he is now founder and Managing Partner of Rumbo Ventures.
