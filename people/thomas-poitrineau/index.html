Biography

Thomas Poitrineau is an Investor at Contrarian Ventures, based in Paris. His firm bio describes roughly a decade of operational and investment experience in European technology. He spent five years in operational roles at Jumia and Onefinestay, then four years investing at Entrepreneurs First and three years at EQT Ventures with a deep tech and climate focus. His stated interests span closed-loop recycling and critical mineral extraction, chip manufacturing, energy sovereignty and fusion energy. Contrarian does not publish his education, join year or board seats.
