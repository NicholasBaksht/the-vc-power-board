Biography

Louis Coppey is a Partner at Point Nine, the European early-stage venture capital firm. He joined the firm as an Associate and was promoted to equal Partner in September 2020, announced alongside Ricardo Sequerra Amram. Point Nine's own announcement credits him with leading the firm's investment in Qwilr six weeks into the job and with key roles in PlayPlay and cargo.one, as well as the firm's first investment in Armenia. He previously worked as a summer analyst at Alven Capital in Paris and holds degrees from HEC, MIT and Telecom ParisTech.
