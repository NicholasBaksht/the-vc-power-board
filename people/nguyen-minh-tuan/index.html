Biography

Nguyen Minh Tuan is the representative of CyberAgent Capital's Vietnam company. The firm's own profile states that after completing graduate study he joined a major Japanese mail order retail company as a systems engineer, where he took part in launching new businesses, and that he joined CyberAgent Capital in August 2014. His profile says he considers Vietnam a market with potential given its educated and young population, and that he aims to support startups answering that market's needs using the strengths and accumulated expertise of the CyberAgent Group. His profile lists the portfolio companies Vexere.com, JupViec.vn, Kyna.vn and Tiki.vn, of which Vexere.com, JupViec.vn and Tiki.vn are marked as trade sales. He is a graduate of the University of Tsukuba and its graduate school. No board seats are stated on his profile page. His title uses 法人代表, representative of the corporation, which indicates a separate Vietnamese legal entity.
