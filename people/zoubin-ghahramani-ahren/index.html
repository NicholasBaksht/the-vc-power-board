Biography

Professor Zoubin Ghahramani is a Founding Partner of Ahren Innovation Capital, named among the Founding Science Partners at the firm's September 2018 launch. Ahren's team page describes him as a world leader in machine learning and co-founder of Geometric Intelligence, which was sold to Uber. The page notes he is with Google Brain and is a Professor at the University of Cambridge, and a Fellow of the Royal Society. He was quoted in Ahren's March 2019 announcement of its Graphcore Series D investment, commenting on bottlenecks in machine learning hardware.
