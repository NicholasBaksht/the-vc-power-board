Biography

Tim Guleri is a managing director of Sierra Ventures, which he joined in 2001 after a career as a serial enterprise-software entrepreneur. He holds a BS in electrical engineering from Punjab Engineering College and an MS in robotics and industrial engineering from Virginia Tech. He built and sold Scopus Technology, which listed in 1995, and founded Octane Software, which merged into Epiphany. At Sierra he has taken two companies public: Sourcefire and MakeMyTrip.

Education

  • BS Electrical Engineering, Punjab Engineering College, Chandigarh
  • MS Robotics and Industrial Engineering, Virginia Tech (1988)

Previous Experience

  • Executive Vice President, Epiphany, Inc. (2000–2001) - after leading the merger of Octane into Epiphany
  • Founder & Chief Executive, Octane Software (1996–2000)
  • VP of Field Operations, Scopus Technology (1992–1996) - IPO 1995, later acquired by Siebel Systems for $750M
  • Information technology, LSI Logic Corporation (from 1989)
