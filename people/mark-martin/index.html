Biography

Mark Martin is a General Partner and co-founder of Cybernetix Ventures, a Boston venture firm backing early stage robotics, automation, and industrial AI companies. He spent much of his career at Analog Devices, where as vice president of industrial automation, sensors, and IoT he led an organization of more than 600 people generating about 1 billion dollars in annual revenue, and he oversaw more than 450 million dollars in business sales and over 100 million dollars in acquisitions. He was based in Hong Kong for three years and has invested in or advised more than 20 startups. At Cybernetix he applies that operating and M&A experience to help hardware founders reach commercial deployment. He holds a BS in electrical engineering from Cornell University and an MS in engineering management from the Gordon Institute at Tufts University.
