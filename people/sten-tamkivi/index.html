Biography

Sten Tamkivi is an Estonian entrepreneur and investor and a partner at Plural. He spent more than eight years as an early Skype executive, growing its Tallinn R&D office ahead of Microsoft's $8.5B acquisition, later co-founded Teleport, and made roughly 40 to 50 angel investments before co-founding Plural in 2022.

Previous Experience

  • Early executive, Skype (8+ years) — grew the Tallinn R&D office to 450 people ahead of Microsoft's $8.5B acquisition
  • Co-Founder, Teleport — acquired by Topia in 2017
  • Entrepreneur-in-Residence, Andreessen Horowitz
  • Co-Founder, Taavet+Sten angel partnership — roughly 40–50 angel investments
