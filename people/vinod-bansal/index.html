Biography

Vinod Bansal is Co-Founder and CFO of Inflection Point Ventures, and his IPV profile page also gives him the title President, CXO Genie. The page describes him as a Chartered Accountant with over four decades of experience across banking, foreign exchange and credit, investment, fund management and business development, and lists prior roles as Relationship Manager at PNB, Executive Vice President at PNB Mutual Fund, GM Finance and Director at the Nahar Group of Companies, CFO of GPI Textiles Limited and Senior Partner at Garg Bansal & Associates. No individual investments are attributed to him by any source read, and he is not listed among the Physis Capital partners.
