Biography

Arielle Ring is President of SKY VC, the travel and transportation venture firm founded in 2016 as JetBlue's corporate venture arm and acquired by aviation asset manager SKY Leasing in May 2025. She was announced as President of JetBlue Ventures on June 5, 2025, and the firm states she oversees company strategy, capital solutions planning and monetization support for portfolio companies. She previously served as Chief Financial Officer of Northvolt N.A. and of Ohmium International, and as Senior Vice President and Head of Capital Markets and Strategic Finance at BBAM, having begun her career in Citi Investment Banking's Global Industrials Group covering travel and transportation. She holds an A.B. in Government from Dartmouth College, awarded magna cum laude.
