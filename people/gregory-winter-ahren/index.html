Biography

Sir Gregory Winter is a Founding Partner of Ahren Innovation Capital, named among the Founding Science Partners in the firm's September 2018 launch announcement. Ahren's team page describes him as a Nobel Laureate and the inventor of the technology leading to Humira, the first fully human antibody blockbuster. He received the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 2018. Ahren does not publish his degrees, prior employers or board seats on the team page.
