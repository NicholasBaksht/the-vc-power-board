Biography

Jackson Vaughan is a co-founder and managing partner of Konvoy, a thesis driven venture capital firm in Denver, Colorado that invests in the technologies and platforms of interactive entertainment, deep tech and critical industries. Before Konvoy he was a software developer in IBM's Watson AI and machine learning group, and he graduated from NYU Stern School of Business in 2015. He founded the firm in 2018 with Jason Chapman and Josh Chapman, and Konvoy closed its first fund at 10.8 million dollars in 2019.
