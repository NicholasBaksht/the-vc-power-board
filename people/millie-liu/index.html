Biography

Millie Liu is a Founding Partner of First Star Ventures. The firm's About page states she focuses on helping deep technology entrepreneurs build global businesses, that she previously worked at APT, acquired by Mastercard for $600m, and that she co-founded an MIT startup on event detection that became Infervision, an AI healthcare platform backed by Sequoia China. It states she is an advisory board member of MIT CSAIL and holds a Master of Finance from MIT and a B.S. in Mathematics from the University of Toronto. Her legal name appears as Ruiyi Liu in the SEC Form D filings for First Star Venture Fund II LP and First Star Venture Fund III LP, where she is named Manager of the General Partner; she signed the Fund II amendment filed 20 August 2019. The MIT CSAIL role is an advisory board seat and is not recorded as a directorship. No corporate board seat is stated by any source read.
