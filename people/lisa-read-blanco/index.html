Biography

Lisa Read Blanco is Partner and Legal Counsel at Piva Capital in San Francisco. She has more than 20 years of experience executing venture capital transactions, with a focus on energy technology and impact investing. She was previously General Counsel and Chief Compliance Officer of Energy Technology Ventures, a joint venture of General Electric, NRG Energy, and ConocoPhillips, a corporate partner at the international law firm King & Spalding, and General Counsel of the impact investment firm Gray Ghost Ventures. She holds a Juris Doctor from the University of Georgia School of Law and a BBA in International Business from the University of Georgia, where she served nine years on the Terry College of Business board, including as chair.
