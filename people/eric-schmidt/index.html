Eric Schmidt

Co-Founder & Non-Managing Member at Innovation Endeavors · Joined 2010

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Eric Schmidt started Innovation Endeavors in 2010 with Dror Berman, Scott Brady and Rick Scanlon, and the firm's own fund announcement describes him as continuing there as a co-founder, non-managing member and anchor investor rather than as an operating partner. He was chief executive officer and chairman of Google from 2001 to 2011, remained chairman of Google until 2015, was executive chairman of Alphabet from 2015 to 2018, and served as technical advisor to Alphabet from 2018 to 2020. Before Google he was chairman and CEO of Novell and spent 14 years at Sun Microsystems, rising to chief technology officer, with earlier technical positions at Xerox PARC, Bell Laboratories and Zilog. He chaired the U.S. Department of Defense Innovation Board from 2016 for four years and chaired the U.S. National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence, which issued its final report in 2021; he has been Chair and CEO of Relativity Space since 2025.
