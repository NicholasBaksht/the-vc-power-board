Biography

Brian McLoughlin is Partner and Co-Founder of MTech Capital, the insurtech focused venture firm he started with his brother Kevin, investing from Santa Monica across North America and Europe with backing from some of the world's largest insurers, brokers and asset managers. Before MTech he spent 14 years as a Partner at Los Angeles venture firm Upfront Ventures, where he focused on fintech investing, and earlier served as chief financial officer of a venture backed fintech company. He sits on the boards of MTech portfolio companies including Embroker, Inshur, Matic, Openly and Polly. He holds an MBA from the Wharton School and a BA from Rutgers College.
