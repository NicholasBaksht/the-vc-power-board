Biography

Miela Mayer is Investor and Operating Partner at Third Sphere. Her firm bio says she joined in 2022 and focuses on hardware investing and non-dilutive capital sources for climate tech, and that she is based in San Francisco. Before Third Sphere she spent over two years at Bain & Company consulting for utilities and sustainability clients, and worked in climate finance at the New York Green Bank and Bloomberg New Energy Finance. She holds a dual BS from Yale in Environmental Engineering and Environmental Studies plus a scholar certificate from the Yale Climate and Energy Studies Institute. She is the named author or co-author of Third Sphere's published investment write-ups for Sention, SwellCycle, AccessGrid, Focal and Return To Vendor.
