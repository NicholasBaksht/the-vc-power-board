Biography

Matt Gorin is a Managing Partner and co-founder of Contour Venture Partners. His firm bio states "twenty six years of experience as an operator, entrepreneur and seed stage venture capitalist". Before Contour he worked in merchant banking at Promontory Financial Group and was a launch team member at Promontory Interfinancial Network, later IntraFI; he had earlier roles in Red Hat's strategic planning and corporate development group, at a Morgan Stanley venture capital fund and as a turnaround consultant at PricewaterhouseCoopers. He founded the nonprofit StreetWise Partners. He holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and a BA in Economics and American Studies from Brandeis University. The firm's team page ties him to portfolio companies including Datadog, Pendo, OnDeck, Movable Ink, ShopKeep, LeagueApps, SmartAsset, Octane, Cutover, Compyl, Felix Pago, OnRamp, Routehappy, SwapDrive, Ticketfly, Wunderkind, YellowJacket and Yhat. The page does not state board seats, so none are recorded.
