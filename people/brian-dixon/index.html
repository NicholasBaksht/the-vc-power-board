Biography

Brian Dixon is Managing Partner at Kapor Capital, the Oakland-based early stage fund. He joined the firm in 2011 as an MBA intern, was named a Partner in November 2015 after serving as Principal leading deals from seed to Series A, and with Ulili Onovakpuri raised the firm's $126 million Fund III, announced in September 2022. His stated investment interests are FinTech and EdTech. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer and Information Science from Northeastern University and an MBA from the F.W. Olin Graduate School of Business at Babson College, and is a Kauffman Fellow and a Management Leadership for Tomorrow Fellow.
