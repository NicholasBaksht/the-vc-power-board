Gagan Goyal

Partner at India Quotient · Joined 2017

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Gagan Goyal served as a general partner at IndiaQuotient from 2017. He began his career in 2004 as an engineer at Hindustan Petroleum Corporation and in 2006 co-founded ThinkLABS Technosolutions with Gaurav Chaturvedi, where he stayed for over a decade. In June 2026 Inc42 reported that he had told the firm he was leaving, and he does not appear on IndiaQuotient's team page. No public source attributes specific investments to him individually.
