Biography

Neil Tolaney is a General Partner at TCV, which he first joined in 2011 and rejoined in 2020. His TCV bio describes investment and operating experience in the consumer internet space, spanning digital entertainment, digital health, online fitness, prosumer empowerment, e-commerce, fintech, edtech and online travel. Before returning to TCV he was a partner at Francisco Partners, a Managing Director at PersonalizationMall.com and Director of Strategic Development at LegalZoom. His TCV bio lists current investments including Adevinta, ByteDance, GoodRx, Grow Therapy, Hotmart, LegalZoom, Mercury, onX, Strava and Zillow.
