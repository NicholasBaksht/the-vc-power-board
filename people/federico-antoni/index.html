Biography

Federico Antoni is a managing partner of ALLVP, the Mexico City based Latin America venture firm that has operated under the name Hi since January 2024. The firm's team page lists him as managing partner since 2012 and states that he resigned as CEO of a retail public company to start the firm, and that he joined the board of Carrot, the first company the firm backed. Form D filings with the SEC name him as a director of Cornershop, Super Seguros Inc, Fintual, Inc. and Enbalg Group Corp, and as a managing partner of Hi Ventures IV LP.
