Biography

Jai Das is a partner, president and co-founder at Sapphire Ventures, which he joined in 2006 and where he invests in AI and machine learning, data analytics, security, open source, DevOps and crypto companies. His firm bio states that more than fifteen of his investments have gone public and twenty-four have been acquired, naming Netskope, MuleSoft, JFrog, Five9, Nutanix, Alteryx, Square, ExactTarget, Apigee, Box and Sumo Logic among the IPOs. He was promoted to president of the firm in January 2019, having previously been a managing director. He holds an electrical engineering degree from Brown University and an MBA from the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, and worked at Oracle, Intel Capital, Agilent Ventures and MVC Capital before Sapphire.
