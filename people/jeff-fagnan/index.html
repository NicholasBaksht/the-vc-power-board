Biography

Jeff Fagnan is a Founding Partner of Accomplice and describes himself as a founding investor who has worked with most of his portfolio since inception. Before Accomplice he was a partner at Atlas Venture, and earlier held roles at SoftBank's venture practice, Booz Allen Hamilton and Nortel. Alongside Accomplice he founded Spearhead, Maiden Lane, BOSS, TUGG, TINT and REV Boston. His portfolio includes AngelList, Carbon Black, CoinList, DataCamp, FreshBooks, Hopper, Integral Ad Science, Patreon, PillPack, Recorded Future, SecurityScorecard, Veracode and WHOOP.
