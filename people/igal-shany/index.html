Biography

Igal Shany joined Viola Growth as a General Partner in November 2023 after roughly 25 years as a senior technology executive. He was CEO of Eitan Medical, which he scaled to about $100 million in sales, orchestrated a three-company merger and divested its wearable unit Sorrel in 2023. Earlier he spent six years as CFO of Kenshoo and was CFO of Saifun Semiconductors, where he led the IPO and the merger with Spansion, later serving as VP Business Development at Spansion. At Viola Growth he focuses the fourth fund on climate technology, digital health and large undisrupted industries.
