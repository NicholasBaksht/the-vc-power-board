Biography

Mark Murphy is the President of Digital Currency Group. His Tech:NYC leader profile states that he leads DCG's legal, communications, marketing, public policy, security, cybersecurity, human resources and facilities teams, and that he is a member of the Board of Directors of the Blockchain Association. Before joining DCG he led communications teams at Bloomberg, First Data and SecondMarket, and earlier in his career worked as a commercial litigation attorney. He is a graduate of Miami University (B.A.) and St. John's University School of Law (J.D.). He was promoted to President in November 2022. No investment is attributed to him individually by any source located.
