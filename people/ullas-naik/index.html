Biography

Ullas Naik is the Founder and General Partner of Streamlined Ventures, the seed-stage firm he founded in Palo Alto, California. He is the sole related person named on the firm's fund Form D filings, described there as Executive Officer and Managing Member of the General Partner. His firm bio states he has over 30 years of entrepreneurial and early-stage investment experience across more than 600 companies, and that at Streamlined he has led seed investments in more than 20 companies valued at over $1Bn. Before founding Streamlined he was a senior member of the investment team at Globespan Capital for 12 years, and he also helped co-found the investment firm Cota Capital. Earlier he was a research analyst at FAC Equities/First Albany, and before his investment career he started a chemical trading business in India. His firm bio states he received an MBA and holds a bachelor's degree in organic chemistry, but names no institution for either. Portfolio-company Form D filings from 2024 list him as a director of Humans, Inc., Zero Cognitive Systems, Inc. and LaylaTex Holdings Inc.
