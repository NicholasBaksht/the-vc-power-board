Biography

Joel Cutler co-founded General Catalyst with David Fialkow and has served as a Co-Founder and Managing Director there since January 2000. His firm bio lists a focus on consumer, enterprise and fintech companies and credits him with backing KAYAK, ITA Software, Airbnb, Lemonade, Oscar Health, Venmo and OLX, alongside current positions including Warby Parker, Multiverse and Blank Street Coffee. He co-founded KAYAK with Paul English and Steve Hafner before it became a General Catalyst portfolio company. He has served on the board of directors of Warby Parker since 2012.
