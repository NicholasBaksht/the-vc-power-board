Biography

Anthony Goldbloom is a co-founder and Investment Partner at AIX Ventures. He founded Kaggle and built it into the world's largest community of data scientists before it was acquired by Google in 2017; he continued to lead it as CEO under Google ownership. He is a prolific angel investor with stakes in more than 50 AI startups, including Weights & Biases and Roboflow. He was named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 list. At AIX he focuses on early-stage AI companies across data infrastructure and machine learning tooling.
