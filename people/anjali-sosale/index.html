Biography

Anjali Sosale is a Partner at WaterBridge Ventures, the Delhi-based early-stage Indian venture firm. WaterBridge's own account of its second fund states that she joined the firm as a partner in 2020, when that fund was being launched. She wrote the firm's published rationale for its investment in the rural gold-loan platform Yellow Metal in June 2022, and she has worked on the firm's Fast Forward seed programme aimed at speeding up institutional seed funding for first-time founders.
