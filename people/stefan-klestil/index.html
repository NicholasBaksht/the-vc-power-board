Biography

Stefan Klestil is General Partner of the Speedinvest Fintech fund, Si F, and part of the firm's Fintech and DeFi and Middle East, Africa and Beyond investment teams. He has more than 20 years of experience in international payments and banking technology across advisory, operational and investment roles. Before Speedinvest he was President of Central and Southern Europe at First Data, a Partner at Roland Berger and a Principal at A.T. Kearney in New York and Vienna. His firm bio names him as an early investor in N26, Wefox, Curve, Billie, Bitpanda, Open, Wayflyer and Fairmoney, and credits him with the exits of Payworks to Visa, iyzico to PayU and Holvi to BBVA.
