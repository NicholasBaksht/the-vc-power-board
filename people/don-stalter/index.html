Biography

Don Stalter was a Partner at Global Founders Capital, where he focused primarily on the US early stage venture market. Before investing he co-founded CityDeal, which was acquired by Groupon, built Groupon's international offices in Europe and Asia, and then led business development globally at Airbnb. At Global Founders Capital he was an early investor in Brex, Checkr, Deel and Slope, and he joined Hyperscience as a board observer after the firm led the company's 100 million dollar Series E announced in December 2021. He is now listed as Founder of Sunshine Lake Capital, a global pre-seed and seed fund.
