Biography

Vivek Subramanian is Partner and Chief Product Officer at Goodwater Capital. Before Goodwater he worked at Sonder, was Head of Product at Coupang, and co-founded CalmSea. He holds an MBA from UC Berkeley, a Masters in Computer Science from the University of Texas at Austin, and a BS in Computer Science and a Masters in Chemistry from BITS Pilani in India. He was named as one of Goodwater's investing partners in the firm's 25 July 2023 fundraising announcement.
