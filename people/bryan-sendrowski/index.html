Biography

Bryan Sendrowski is a Partner and Chief Financial Officer of Deerfield Management and joined the firm in 2012. He is responsible for the overall finance, accounting and tax functions of the firm, oversees the financial management and related operating activities of the firm and its affiliates, and oversees the operational support provided by the External Operations team. Before Deerfield he was Chief Financial Officer of New American Therapeutics, a specialty pharmaceutical company, and previously served in senior finance and accounting positions at early-stage specialty pharmaceutical companies. He was a Senior Manager in the financial statement audit practice of KPMG. He received his B.A. from Pace University and is licensed, inactive, as a Certified Public Accountant in New Jersey.
