Biography

Dr Annegret de Baey-Diepolder, MD, is a Venture Partner at Seventure Partners based in Munich, Germany, focusing on life sciences opportunities in the DACH countries. Her Seventure bio says she joined Seventure as a Venture Partner in April 2017. From October 2011 she worked for six years as an independent consultant and interim manager in the biotech industry, including co-founding and serving as CEO of Rigontec GmbH and as CEO of ImmunOligo. Earlier she spent ten years in venture capital, primarily at TVM Capital in Munich and at GIMV NV in Munich and Antwerp, and began her professional work in 1998 as a senior research group leader developing therapeutic vaccines at Micromet AG, now Amgen. She holds an MD in human genetics from Ludwig-Maximilians-University Munich, studied medicine at Georg-August University in Goettingen and the Technical University in Munich, completed two years of specialisation training in dermatology and allergology at the University Hospital of Munich, and received postdoctoral training as a Fellow of the German Research Foundation at the Basel Institute for Immunology.
