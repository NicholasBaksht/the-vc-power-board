Biography

Eric Hippeau is a co-founder and Managing Partner of Lerer Hippeau, which lists him on its team page as having been with the firm since 2010. Before that he was chairman and chief executive officer of Ziff-Davis, then a managing partner at SoftBank Capital, where regulatory filings say he co-led the firm's investing activities for nearly a decade in companies including Buddy Media, GeoCities and Yahoo!. He invested in The Huffington Post while at SoftBank Capital and later became its chief executive officer. He served on the board of Yahoo! from 1996 to 2011, on the board of BuzzFeed, and on the board of Marriott International until May 2024.
