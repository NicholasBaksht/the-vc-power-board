Biography

Christophe Maire is Founding Partner of FoodLabs, the Berlin-based early-stage venture capital firm and venture studio for food, sustainability and health. He founded the Berlin venture firm Atlantic Labs, out of which FoodLabs originated as Atlantic Food Labs in 2016 before being spun out as a standalone firm in 2021. He is described in coverage of the FoodLabs launch as a long-time tech investor. FoodLabs' own announcement of its €100M fund states the firm was founded by Christophe F. Maire.
