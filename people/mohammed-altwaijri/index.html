Biography

Mohammed AlTwaijri is a Senior Investment Professional at Shorooq Partners, based in the firm's Riyadh office. He has an entrepreneurial background, having built a portfolio of seven food and beverage and retail brands with successful exits, before moving into a strategy role at a high-growth startup. He holds a bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering and Electronics from King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals.
