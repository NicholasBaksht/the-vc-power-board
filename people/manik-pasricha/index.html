Biography

Manik Pasricha is listed on the Titan Capital team page as VICE PRESIDENT within the Titan Capital Seed team. His Titan Capital biography describes him as an ex-founder with over a decade of experience across four continents and states that at Titan Capital "Manik leads technology investments, evaluates deals, and develops theses based on macro and micro trends." It states that he founded a digital lending marketplace focused on mortgage loans in India, mentored founders through the Polsky Center for Entrepreneurship at the University of Chicago, and held roles at Boston Consulting Group in Chicago, at Fractal AI and with the Government of India. It lists an MBA from the University of Chicago Booth School of Business, a B.Tech from IIT Delhi, CFA Level 2, and a LAMP Fellowship. No year of joining Titan Capital, and no individually attributed investment or board seat, could be sourced.
