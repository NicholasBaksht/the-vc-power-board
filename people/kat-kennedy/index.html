Biography

Kat Kennedy is a General Partner at Kickstart. Her Kickstart biography page states she holds a BS from Utah State University and brings fifteen years of operating experience, and that before joining Kickstart she was President and CXO of Degreed. Deseret News reported on 30 March 2023 that she had been hired in August 2022. Her Kickstart biography page lists eight companies under a "Board Director" heading and one under "Board Observer". No education beyond the Utah State University degree, and no prior employer other than Degreed, could be sourced.
