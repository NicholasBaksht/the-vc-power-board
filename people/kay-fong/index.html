Biography

Kay Fong is a Venture Partner at NGC Ventures. The firm's team page states that he holds a bachelor's degree in software engineering, first bought Bitcoin in 2013 while still in high school and mined CPU altcoins, and returned to the blockchain industry in 2019 as an intern at Binance. The same page states, in a single sentence quoted verbatim, that 'Kay has led investments in EthSign, Scroll, Dora Factory, Nubit and more for NGC.' That sentence is the firm's own first-party attribution of those deals to him and is the basis for the notableInvestments entries below. It does not state a board seat, so boardSeats is empty.
