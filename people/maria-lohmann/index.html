Biography

Maria Lohmann is listed by Novo Holdings as a Partner in Capital Investments. Her profile says she joined Novo Holdings in November 2023 as a Principal in Capital Investments, responsible for analysing fund and co-investment opportunities within infrastructure and the green transition. Before Novo Holdings she gained experience at a specialist fund-of-funds manager, Danske Bank, A.P. Moller-Maersk and Ørsted. She holds an MSc in Economics from the University of Copenhagen.
