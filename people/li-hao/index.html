Biography

李浩 is a founding partner and co-CEO of 中科创星 (CAS Star). The firm's own news page for its sixth hard-technology entrepreneurship camp prints his title verbatim as 中科创星创始合伙人、联席CEO 李浩 and records him attending in person. 西科控股, which co-founded the firm, also names him as a 创始合伙人 in its 2024 awards round-up. No education, prior employers, board seats or individually attributed investments could be sourced for him; the firm's team page could not be retrieved.
