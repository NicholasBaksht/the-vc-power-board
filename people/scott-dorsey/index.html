Biography

Scott Dorsey is a co-founder of High Alpha and its Managing Partner, per the firm's team page. He is one of four people named as Executive Officers and members of the Board of Managers of High Alpha Studio, LLC in that entity's Form D filed on 21 May 2015, and one of four individuals listed as related persons of High Alpha Capital Fund IV, L.P. in its Form D/A filed on 5 June 2024. He was quoted as Managing Partner in the firm's 11 June 2024 announcement of the $125M High Alpha IV fund. He is listed on Zylo's own leadership page as Executive Chairman, where he is also described as Managing Partner of High Alpha and as a co-founder of ExactTarget, which Salesforce acquired. High Alpha's May 2024 article on partner commencement addresses names Indiana University Bloomington as his alma mater, class of '89.
