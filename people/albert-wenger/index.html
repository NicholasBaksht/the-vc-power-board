Biography

Albert Wenger is a partner at Union Square Ventures according to the firm's own site, where the batch title of Managing Partner is not used. Before joining USV he was president of del.icio.us through the company's sale to Yahoo and an angel investor in Etsy and Tumblr. He is the author of the book The World After Capital and writes about technology, science and philosophy on his blog Continuations. He graduated from Harvard College in economics and computer science and holds a Ph.D. in Information Technology from MIT.
