Biography

Henry Tantum is an Associate at N47 in Silicon Valley, focused on cybersecurity and data infrastructure within the firm's Infrastructure and Systems categories. Before joining he was a strategy consultant at Accenture in Washington, D.C., where he advised Department of Defense officials on combat readiness. He holds a BA in Economics from Middlebury College and describes earlier work as a data analyst on market trends. A Bay Area native, he says he has always gravitated toward creatively solving problems using data analysis. N47 lists no portfolio companies, board seats or joining year for him.
