Biography

Daniel Marlo is a Managing Partner at 4DX Ventures, where he works with portfolio companies on revenue growth, team building, corporate development, product and strategic partnerships. He joined the firm's management team as a general partner when 4DX closed its $60 million second fund in December 2021, after serving as a senior advisor to the firm from its beginning. He previously founded and ran global businesses across fintech, telecom, sports and technology in emerging markets, including as Founder and CEO of Unidos, and served as a Senior Advisor to Silver Lake. He earned a B.A. from Georgetown University and a J.D. from Northwestern University School of Law and completed advanced coursework at the Kellogg School of Management.
