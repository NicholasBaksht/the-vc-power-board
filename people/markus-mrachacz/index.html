Biography

Markus Mrachacz is Managing Director of Bayern Kapital, the venture and growth investor of the Free State of Bavaria, where he has worked since 2000. He joined as a Beteiligungsmanager, rose through several leadership positions, and was most recently Prokurist and Teamleiter responsible for co-designing and running the investments of the ScaleUp-Fonds Bayern. He was appointed Co-Geschaeftsfuehrer alongside Monika Steger in July 2023. His own firm bio states that he finances start-ups in the life science and software sectors.
