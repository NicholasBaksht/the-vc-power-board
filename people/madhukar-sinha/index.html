Biography

Madhukar Sinha is a Founding Partner of IndiaQuotient, the Bengaluru based sector agnostic early stage firm he co-founded with Anand Lunia in 2012. Before IndiaQuotient he was an investment manager at Aavishkaar Microfinance and Impact Fund and worked in India's microfinance sector. The firm's page describes his focus on legal and regulatory aspects of investment opportunities and on networking within entrepreneur circles. He holds a degree in agriculture engineering and a postgraduate degree from IIM Lucknow.
