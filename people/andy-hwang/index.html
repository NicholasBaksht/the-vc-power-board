Biography

Andy Hwang is a General Partner at Wavemaker and leads Wavemaker Ventures, the firm's early-stage fund, working with the investment team and founders from fundraising through scaling. He also oversees the firm's corporate innovation work connecting large enterprises with startups. Before Wavemaker he spent ten years at Facebook, where he helped launch operations in Asia and served as Managing Director for SMB and mid-market advertising, growing that business to over two million customers and US$6B in annual revenue, and he also held global and regional leadership roles at Google and Stripe. He is an angel investor and startup coach. He holds a bachelor's degree in Business Administration from UC Berkeley and an MBA from the Wharton School.
