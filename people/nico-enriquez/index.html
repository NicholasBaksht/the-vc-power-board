Biography

Nico Enriquez is a Principal at Future Ventures, where he focuses on energy, robotics, climate, and biotech companies. Before joining the firm he served as a Commercialization Fellow at ARPA-E, the Advanced Research Projects Agency-Energy within the U.S. Department of Energy, working on bringing energy innovations to market. Earlier he co-founded and ran a consumer startup for eight years, reaching distribution in 14 states, and was named to a Forbes 30 Under 30 list. He holds a B.S. in Cognitive Neuroscience with Sigma Xi honors from Brown University, an M.B.A. from Stanford Graduate School of Business, and a professional certification in sustainability finance and analytics from Columbia University.
