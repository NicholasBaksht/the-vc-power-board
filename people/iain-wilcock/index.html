Biography

Iain Wilcock is a Venture Partner at Seventure Partners based in London, United Kingdom. His Seventure bio says he joined the firm's Life Sciences team in 2008 as a Venture Partner responsible for investments in the UK and Ireland, having spent over 10 years creating and managing biotech and health venture capital activities at Quester Capital, and that in 2007 he founded Vela Venture Partners to advise venture capitalists in the UK. The bio describes him as a former Director who has served on the boards of biotechnology, medtech and cleantech companies including Vivacta, Avidex, Oxxon, Lectus and Xention; those are described as former roles and are therefore not recorded as current board seats. It also states he is a member of the British Venture Capital Association, the BioIndustry Association, the Industrial Biotech Leadership Forum and UNICO, and that he was appointed in 2008 to the Strategic Intellectual Property Policy Advisory Board advising the UK Intellectual Property Office. He holds a Biochemistry degree from the University of Oxford, a UK tax expert qualification, and is a graduate of London Business School.
