Ryusuke Hayashi

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Ryusuke Hayashi (林 隆介) joined Energy & Environment Investment in 2020 as Head of Mobility Business Innovation, the firm's English members page states, to explore opportunities in the mobility area. It says he worked in the automotive and airline industries before joining Nissan in 2004, where he led EV new business incubation covering V2H, V2G, EV carsharing and EV battery business as well as new EV product planning, and was responsible for EV business synergy creation within the Renault Nissan Mitsubishi Alliance. A Response.jp interview of 18 March 2021 prints his Japanese name and title as 環境エネルギー投資 モビリティ事業創造室長 林隆介氏, which is how the Japanese-language surname for the first-name-only English profile was established. That interview describes his portfolio as spread across several businesses and fields but names no company, so no notableInvestments and no board seats are recorded.
