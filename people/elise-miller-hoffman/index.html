Biography

Elise Miller Hoffman is a General Partner at Cultivation Capital. Her firm profile states that she invests in digital health and life sciences startups from seed to Series B. She is Chief Operating Officer of ImageMover, a Director of the Holekamp Seed Fund and Investor in Residence at the Skandalaris Center for Interdisciplinary Innovation and Entrepreneurship at Washington University, and was previously a board observer at NarrativeDx, which was acquired in 2020. She holds a BA in Spanish Literature and Latin American Studies and an MBA, both from Washington University in St. Louis. Her firm profile does not list a current portfolio board seat, so boardSeats is empty; her ImageMover role is an operating role rather than a board seat.
