Biography

Tea Elezi is a General Partner at Peak, where she covers the Benelux and focuses on software founders. She started in tech mergers and acquisitions advising large companies on deals before moving into venture capital at Newion, where she backed B2B SaaS companies including Parloa, Salonkee and Dexter. She then helped build the venture arm of Mediahuis and shape its thesis in EdTech and the Future of Work. Peak describes her as combining data analysis with product and behavioural research. She speaks seven languages and has lived in five countries.
