Biography

Jason Pressman has worked at Shasta Ventures since 2005 and is a Managing Director of the firm. Before Shasta he was Vice President of Strategy and Operations at Walmart.com from 2000 to 2004. His Shasta bio states that he serves on the boards of data.world, Lattice, Nextdoor and Zuora, and that he led the firm's investments in Adometry, Demdex, Glint, Scalyr, Steelbrick and StrongLoop, each of which was acquired. He holds a B.S. in Finance from the University of Maryland, College Park and an M.B.A. from Stanford University.
