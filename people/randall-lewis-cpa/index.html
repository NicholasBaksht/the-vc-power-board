Biography

Randall Lewis, CPA is Managing Partner, Portfolio at Cleveland Avenue, which he joined in 2020. His firm bio says he is responsible for leading investment and management of the firm's ag tech portfolio investments and supporting the CAST US Fund, and that he has over 35 years of finance, risk management and operations experience across GE, Wells Fargo and Elevance Health, Inc. (formerly Anthem), where his roles included Executive Vice President and Chief Compliance Officer, Executive Vice President and Chief Auditor, Managing Director of Corporate Development, and chief executive of a startup logistics firm that was sold. His firm bio states he is a Director for Simon Property Group (NYSE: SPG); SEC records for the Section 16 filer LEWIS RANDALL J (CIK 0001123485) show Forms 4 for Simon Property Group continuing through 1 July 2026, and Simon Property Group's proxy statement filed 1 April 2026 contains both "Cleveland Avenue" and "Lewis". He obtained a Bachelor of Science in General Management/Accounting and an MBA in Finance from Purdue University.
