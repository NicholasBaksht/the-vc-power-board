Biography

Mark Rostick joined Intel Capital in 1999 and is Vice President and Senior Managing Director, based in Raleigh, North Carolina, and a voting member of the firm's investment committee. He co-manages Intel Capital's Cloud domain investment activities and his bio cites investment experience in cloud applications, infrastructure hardware and software, and AI and machine learning. Intel Capital lists him as a director or observer on the boards of Beep, Cherre, Hypersonix, Immuta, Lilt, MinIO, Opaque Systems, Tetrate and Verta, and names Amplidata, Cherre, Enpirion, Kazan Networks, Nyansa, Velostrata, Verta and Virtustream among his exits. Before Intel Capital he worked as a practicing attorney and in banking.
