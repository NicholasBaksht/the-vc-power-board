Biography

Ben Yu is a Managing Partner at Sierra Ventures and has been with the firm since 2000. Before Sierra he received his PhD in electrical engineering from Princeton and worked as an engineer and researcher in networking infrastructures. His firm bio lists his areas as AI and ML, semiconductors and component technologies, deep tech, industrial automation and smart machines.
