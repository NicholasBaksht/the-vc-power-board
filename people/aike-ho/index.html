Biography

Aike Ho is a Partner at ACME Capital, where she focuses on frontier health investments. She invests in the physical layer of biology, where robotics, synthetic biology and engineering intersect. Her career in healthcare was motivated by personal experience after she battled cancer in her mid-20s and set out to change the healthcare ecosystem. She also studies consumer behaviour and occasionally backs consumer companies such as Partiful. She joined ACME in 2017, was promoted to Partner in 2022, graduated from Yale University, and sits on the boards of Conceivable Life Sciences, Arya Health, Camber and Brightside. She lives in New York, and her daughter was the first American baby born using Conceivable's robotic IVF technology.
