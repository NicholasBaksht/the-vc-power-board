Biography

Robert Trezona is listed on Kembara's site as a Senior Strategic Advisor with more than 25 years of experience, though the fund's February 2026 launch materials describe him as a General Partner. He is the founder of Kiko Ventures, the climate tech investment platform, and was previously Head of Cleantech at IP Group plc. He began his cleantech career around 2000 as a hydrogen and fuel cell scientist and later worked at the Carbon Trust. He is a materials scientist by training with a PhD from the University of Cambridge, and founded a business based on a materials characterisation technique developed during his doctorate. Kembara lists Ceres Power, Hinge Health, Hysata and OxCCU among his investments.
