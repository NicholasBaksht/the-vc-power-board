Biography

Alexander Tamas was described by Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz as 'Yuri Milner's human supercomputer' for the landmark early bets he sourced at DST Global - Facebook, Airbnb, Spotify, and Twitter among them - before founding Vy Capital, which later contributed to Elon Musk's Twitter acquisition.
