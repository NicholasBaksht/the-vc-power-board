Biography

Yi Jean Chow is an Investment Partner at Clean Energy Ventures, based in the firm's European operations. She brings more than a decade of experience across consulting and the energy sector, spanning business development, product management, partnerships and commercial contracting with international energy utilities. She was previously Investment Principal at Future Energy Ventures, the corporate venture arm of E.ON, where she led development of the fund's Impact and ESG methodology and held board director and observer roles at energy and mobility startups. Earlier in her career she worked at energy startups in Boston, New York and London after starting in strategy and operations consulting. She holds a BA in Chemistry and Physics from Harvard, an MSc from University College London, and is a CFA Charterholder.
