Biography

Dan Gwak brings a genuinely unusual path into venture capital — from combat service in Afghanistan as a Marine, through Wall Street and private equity, to strategic investing for the U.S. intelligence community at In-Q-Tel, before joining Point72 to lead its Defense Tech and Growth investing.

Previous Experience

  • Fireteam Leader, United States Marine Corps (Operation Enduring Freedom, Helmand Province)
  • Investment Banking Analyst, M&A Group, Credit Suisse
  • Private Equity Associate, The Carlyle Group
  • Partner, In-Q-Tel
