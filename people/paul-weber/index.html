Biography

Paul Weber is a General Partner at Cultivation Capital with over 25 years in enterprise software. He was Chief Business Officer and Senior Vice President of Sales at Interactive Intelligence, Inc., which was acquired by Genesys for $1.4B; he joined in 1997, helped establish the go-to-market strategy and built the salesforce to $400m in revenue. Earlier he spent five years at Software Artistry Inc. in the early HelpDesk and CRM sector, from its startup phase through its IPO and sale to IBM. His firm profile says he is a board member or advisor to several early-stage private cloud companies but does not name any of them, so boardSeats and notableInvestments are both empty. No education is published on his firm profile. The Form D for Cultivation Capital Tech Fund IV, LP names him as a Managing Director of the fund's general partner.
