Biography

Florian Denis is a Partner at Elaia, focused on biotech, medtech and cleantech investments. He joined the firm as an Investment Manager in 2019 and was appointed Partner in January 2025. Before Elaia he was an associate at Auriga Partners and an equity research analyst in the healthcare division of Société de bourse Gilbert Dupont, part of Société Générale. He holds a master's degree in technology and innovation management from Université Paris Dauphine, Mines ParisTech and ENS, and an MSc from Sup'Biotech Paris.
