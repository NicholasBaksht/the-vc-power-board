Biography

David Wu joined Maveron in 2012 as a venture partner in the firm's San Francisco office, having previously been an executive at Homestead.com, an active angel investor and an Executive-in-Residence at Redpoint Ventures. Eargo's investor relations site describes him as a partner at Maveron since 2012 whose primary focus is emerging consumer internet companies, and states he joined Eargo's board in July 2014. He led Maveron's Series A investment in the on-demand fuel delivery company Booster in 2016 and joined its board. He holds engineering and economics degrees from Stanford University.
