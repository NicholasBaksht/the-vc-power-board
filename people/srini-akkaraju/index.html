Biography

Srinivas Akkaraju, M.D., Ph.D. is the Founder and Managing Partner of Samsara BioCapital, described on the firm's team page as Founder and Managing General Partner. He was in Business and Corporate Development at Genentech from October 1998 to April 2001, joined J.P. Morgan Partners in 2001 and became a Partner there in 2005, co-founded and was a Managing Director of the private equity firm Panorama Capital, LLC, served as Managing Director of New Leaf Venture Partners from January 2009 to April 2013, and was a General Partner of Sofinnova Ventures from April 2013 to February 2016. He is named in the 21 March 2017 Form D for Samsara BioCapital, L.P. as a Managing Member of the fund's general partner. He received his M.D. and a Ph.D. in Immunology from Stanford University and undergraduate degrees in Biochemistry and Computer Science from Rice University. Samsara's team page states that he serves as a director of Scholar Rock, Mineralys Therapeutics, vTv Therapeutics, Alumis, Inventiva, Kalaris and several private biotechs, and that he previously served on the boards of Seattle Genetics, Principia Biopharma, Chinook Therapeutics, Intercept Pharmaceuticals, Eyetech Pharmaceuticals, ZS Pharma, Synageva Biopharma Corp., Amarin Corporation and Barrier Therapeutics.
