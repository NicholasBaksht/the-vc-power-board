Biography

Christian Nagel co-founded Earlybird in 1997 and is a Co-Founder and General Partner based in the firm's Berlin office. Earlybird lists his focus areas as Software Infra & Models and DeepTech & Hardware, and states he specializes in the FinTech, InsureTech and FoodTech verticals with over 27 years of entrepreneurial and investment experience. Before Earlybird he was Chairman of the Board at Tipp24 AG and Interhyp AG, both of which led to IPOs, and an Engagement Manager at McKinsey. He holds a Diplom-Wirtschaftsingenieur from the Technical University of Hamburg and a PhD in Management from the University of St. Gallen.
