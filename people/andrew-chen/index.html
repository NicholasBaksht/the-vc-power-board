Biography

Andrew Chen is a general partner at Andreessen Horowitz, which he joined in 2018, and he focuses on consumer technology, entertainment and AI through the firm's a16z speedrun initiative. He led the Rider Growth teams at Uber before joining the firm and began his career as an analyst at Mohr Davidow Ventures. He holds a B.S. in applied mathematics from the University of Washington and wrote The Cold Start Problem, a book about how startups launch and scale using network effects. He led the firm's investments in Substack and Clubhouse and joined both boards, and announced its investments in Sandbox VR and Sleeper.
