Biography

Nikhil Basu Trivedi is co-founder and a general partner of Footwork, the early-stage venture firm he started with Mike Smith in April 2021. He was previously a Managing Director at Shasta Ventures, where he invested in Athelas, Brigit, Canva, ClassDojo, Color Health, Frame.io, Imperfect Foods, Lattice and The Farmer's Dog. Before Shasta he worked at Insight Partners and was on the founding team of Artsy. He describes himself as having been a venture capitalist since 2010, focused on consumer technology and enterprise companies building consumer-like products. He writes the Next Big Thing newsletter on Substack.
