Biography

Toph Day is CEO of Elevate Ventures, the Indiana-based nonprofit venture development organization that operates under a professional services contract with the State of Indiana and the Indiana Economic Development Corporation. He appears in first-party material under both Toph Day and Christopher Day; a SEC Form D filed on 22 January 2025 names him as Christopher L. Day, executive officer, director and promoter of Elevate Ventures Growth Fund I GP, LLC. His Elevate Ventures biography states he co-founded eight businesses in seven sectors over roughly 30 years and has led companies in artificial intelligence, SaaS, hardtech, broadband, entertainment, investment banking and real estate, and that he is also Chief Visionary for Rally. He is a 1992 graduate of Purdue University. He was quoted as CEO of Elevate Ventures in the IEDC's 23 May 2024 announcement of a $100 million Indiana growth-stage fund and in Elevate Ventures' 25 November 2024 announcement of the Amplified Sciences seed round.
