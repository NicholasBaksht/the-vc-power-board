Biography

Paul Veradittakit is Managing Partner at Pantera Capital, a firm that invests exclusively in blockchain assets. He joined Pantera in April 2014 and focuses on early-stage investments, publishing the weekly VeradiVerdict newsletter on the blockchain industry. In October 2025 he set out Pantera's investment rationale for TransCrypts, Meanwhile and Coinflow, and stated that Pantera led Coinflow's Series A.
