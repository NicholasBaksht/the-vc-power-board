Biography

Landon Young is Vice President of Pre-Seed at Elevate Ventures. His team-member page describes him as a trans-disciplinary entrepreneur, educator and investor focused on high-impact technology innovation, and records that he was named one of thirteen Kauffman Foundation Global Scholars in 2013, awarded a National Science Foundation Fellowship in 2011, is the Founding Curator of the World Economic Forum Global Shaper Hub in Kansas City and was a 2018 MP1 Fellow. He holds a Ph.D. from Purdue University and completed executive education at Harvard University, and was an advisor to Stanford University's University Innovation Fellows Program. His page states he is an active advisor and board member for several venture-backed startup companies but names none of them, so no board seat has been recorded here and notableInvestments is empty.
