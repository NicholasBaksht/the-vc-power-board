Biography

Alexis Ohanian co-founded Initialized Capital with Garry Tan and left the firm in June 2020. He co-founded Reddit and attended the University of Virginia, graduating in 2005. Axios reported that he stepped back from Initialized in 2014 to return to Reddit, rejoined full time in 2018, and backed companies including GOAT, Ro and Patreon before departing. He founded Seven Seven Six in 2020.
