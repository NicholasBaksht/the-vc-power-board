Biography

Wendy Xiao is a Partner at Northzone based in the firm's New York office, where she joined in 2015. Northzone states she invests broadly in the healthcare, consumer and developer tools sectors and has invested in over 19 companies, naming Spring Health and Magic Labs among them. Before Northzone she founded CoHatchery, a co-working and childcare company, and worked as a consultant at A.T. Kearney and Accenture. The firm notes she has been recognized on the Midas Brink List.
