Biography

Celia Hart is General Partner at Supernova Invest, where the team page places her in the Healthcare sector team. The firm's team page states: "Celia has more than 20 years' experience in private equity. She joined the team in 2005 and is responsible for the early team, the healthcare sector and the Supernova 2 fund." Her stated 2005 start predates the 2017 creation of Supernova Invest and reflects tenure with the team that came from CEA Investissement. She provided the firm's commentary on the closing of the Supernova Innovation 3 seed fund at 100 million euros. No education, prior employers, portfolio board seats, IPO count or exit count could be sourced.
