Biography

Mike Kennedy is a Venture Partner at Ananda Impact Ventures. He began his career in retail and commerce at Unilever, Marks and Spencer and Harrods before moving into venture capital in 2000, holding roles at 3i plc, YFM Group, Electra Private Equity plc and Acuity Capital. He co-founded Inogesis, a technology innovation consultancy, and later joined Restoration Partners. At Ananda he supports Closed Loop Medicine and Open Bionics and is exploring investments around grit and resilience. Outside work he runs a suckler beef herd in southwest Ireland using agroecological methods. Ananda's profile names his employers but not his titles at most of them, and publishes no education details or joining year.
