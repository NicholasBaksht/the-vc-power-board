Biography

Howard Horowitz is a Venture Partner at Blu Venture Investors, named as one of five new Venture Partners in the firm's own announcement of 26 April 2024. His firm profile states he was Head of Global Real Estate at Equinix, a Principal at Trammell Crow Company and President of Telecom Realty Group, and is Managing Principal of Kestrel Venture Partners and President of Kestrel Advisors, LLC. The profile names him a trustee of the Baldwin Center for Preservation Development.
