Biography

Mark Shifke has been Chief Financial Officer of Digital Currency Group since 1 August 2023, succeeding Michael Kraines, DCG's first CFO, who left in April 2023. He signed an agreement filed as an exhibit to Grayscale Investments, Inc.'s Form S-1, dated 16 May 2024, as Chief Financial Officer of Digital Currency Group, Inc., which is the most recent first-party confirmation of his title located. Before DCG he was Chief Financial Officer of Billtrust and of Green Dot Corporation, and earlier held roles at J.P. Morgan and, as a Vice President, at Goldman Sachs. No investment is attributed to him individually by any source located.
