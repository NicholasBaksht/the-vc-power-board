Biography

Daniel Graf is a General Partner at Redalpine. Swiss by origin, he has been based in Silicon Valley since the early 2000s and the firm credits him with over 25 years building and scaling technology products. He founded the digital media platform Kyte, which was acquired in 2011, led product on Google Maps and local search at Google, was VP of Product and Management at Uber where he scaled a team of more than 1,000 people, and served as Chief Product and Technology Officer at DispatchHealth. As an angel he has backed more than 60 startups and 20 venture capital funds globally. He holds an M.S. in Computer and Systems Engineering from Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from NTB in Switzerland. Redalpine does not publish his joining year, board seats or named fund investments.
