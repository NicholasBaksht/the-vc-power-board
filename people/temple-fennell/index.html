Biography

Temple Fennell is a co-founder and Managing Partner of Clean Energy Ventures with more than 25 years of experience as an investor and founder. He began his career as a management consultant at KPMG in New York and as an analyst at leveraged buyout firm Clinton Capital Corporation, later serving as a strategic advisor to Flagship Ventures and co-founding three privately funded companies. He is a member of the Keller Family Office and co-founded and co-directs the University of Zurich/Harvard/MIT "Impact Investing for the Next Generation" program, and co-leads MIT's "Visionary Investing" workshops. He holds a B.S. in Systems Engineering from the University of Virginia and an MBA from MIT Sloan, where he was a Sloan Fellow, and is a Senior Fellow at the University of Zurich. He serves on the boards of Rebound Technologies, Aqua Membranes, SunDensity and Leading Edge Equipment Technologies.
