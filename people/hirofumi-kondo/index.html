Biography

Hirofumi Kondo is Representative Director and President of 株式会社サイバーエージェント・キャピタル. He holds this role concurrently with being an 執行役員 (Executive Officer) of the listed parent CyberAgent, Inc., where the firm's own page states he is the officer responsible for the investment and incubation business (投資育成事業管掌役員). His CyberAgent Capital profile lists him currently as a 取締役 of 株式会社サイゲームス・キャピタル and a 社外取締役 of 株式会社トランジットホールディングス, and as 常務理事 and head of the large enterprise collaboration committee (大企業連携部会長) of the Japan Venture Capital Association. His profile states that CyberAgent Capital moved early into Asia on the principle of contributing to the creation of innovative internet companies representative of Asia, and that it runs venture capital operations in 7 countries and 9 bases, investing actively in startups from seed stage. He was already Representative Director and President when the company changed its name from CyberAgent Ventures to CyberAgent Capital with effect from 1 January 2019. He graduated from Meiji University's School of Political Science and Economics.
