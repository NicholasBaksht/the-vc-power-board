Biography

Hany Nada is Co-Founder and Partner of ACME Capital, which he co-founded in 2018. He co-founded GGV Capital in 2000 and was its Managing Partner until October 2016, remaining a Venture Partner until 2018. Before venture capital he spent ten years at Piper Jaffray & Co. as a Managing Director and Senior Research Analyst specialising in internet software and infrastructure. He has served as a long-term partner to more than 150 companies over two decades, advising CEOs and management teams on global markets, business development, strategy and M&A and IPO guidance. He currently sits on the boards of AviaGames, Canopy, Forma.ai and Regology, and his investments include DraftKings, IonQ, Athenahealth, Endeca, Heptagon, Kintana, Tudou and Vocera Communications.
