Biography

Mike Vernal is a general partner at Conviction, Sarah Guo's venture firm backing AI-native companies. He was a product manager and engineer at Microsoft before spending eight years at Facebook leading engineering, product and design teams. He was a partner at Sequoia Capital from April 2016 until he left in 2023, where his work included Notion, Rippling and Verkada. He joined Conviction in early 2025, announced alongside the firm's $230 million second fund.
