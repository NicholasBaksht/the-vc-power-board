Biography

Nicolas Berman is a partner at Kaszek, which he helped start in 2011 with MercadoLibre founders Hernán Kazah and Nicolás Szekasy. He joined MercadoLibre as its eighth employee after a commercial role at LG, and he graduated in business administration from the Universidad de Buenos Aires. He has represented Kaszek on rounds including Warren's Series B in 2020, Winnin's Series A in 2024, where he joined the board, and TravelX's Series A in 2026, the firm's first aviation technology investment. He is also a director of Endeavor Chile.
