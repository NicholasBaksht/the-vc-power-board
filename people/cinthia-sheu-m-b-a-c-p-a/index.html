Biography

Cinthia Sheu is the Partner and Chief Operating Officer of Vivo Capital, overseeing the firm's administration, finance, information technology and tax, and working with the legal team on fundraising, operations and regulatory compliance. Her Vivo biography states she has over 25 years of professional experience in financial services and has worked in the private equity industry since 1999. Before Vivo she was controller at Infinity Capital, a technology venture capital firm, and she began her career in public accounting as a tax manager with PricewaterhouseCoopers, serving private and public clients in the financial and technology sectors. She holds a B.A. in Economics from National Taiwan University and an M.B.A. from the University of Texas at Dallas, and is a Certified Public Accountant in California.
