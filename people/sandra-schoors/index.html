Biography

Sandra Schoors is listed as a Partner at VIVES Partners and sits on its investment committee, covering life sciences. She holds a PhD from KU Leuven, where her research on the role of metabolism in angiogenesis was published in Nature and Cell. She joined Droia Ventures in 2017 and became a partner there, focusing on private investments in oncology and genetic disease therapeutics, leading early-stage venture investing and company creation, holding board positions and operational C-level roles in start-ups, and establishing Droia Labs to support new venture development. She joined VIVES in 2024. Her own VIVES profile text describes her as Senior Investment Manager while the team page lists her under Partner, and that inconsistency is recorded rather than resolved.
