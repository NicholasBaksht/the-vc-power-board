Biography

Jenny Graetzel is Partner, Seed Funds at N47, based in Silicon Valley, where she leads the firm's seed fund strategy and acts as an adviser to early-stage fund managers investing in enterprise technology. Her N47 profile says she invested in venture and growth funds for 17 years across the US and Europe before joining, but does not name the firms she worked at. She describes her role as building trusted partnerships with early-stage fund managers who are on the ground floor with the next generation of enterprise founders. She authored the N47 perspective The case for seed funds. N47 does not publish her education, board seats or the year she joined.
