Biography

Hoang Thi Kim Dung is Country Director of Vietnam at Genesia Ventures, Inc. and works from the firm's Ho Chi Minh City office. Her Genesia Ventures profile page states she studied in the International Economic Relations department of Foreign Trade University in Vietnam, received a Japanese government MEXT scholarship, and obtained her degree from Osaka University's Faculty of Economics in 2012. It states she then worked as a sales representative at IBM Japan on projects with major Japanese financial institutions and took part in the IBM Blue Hub programme supporting startups. She joined Genesia Ventures in April 2019 and returned to Vietnam in September 2019 to establish the firm's Ho Chi Minh City office. She was named as the responsible capitalist (担当キャピタリスト), with the title Associate at the time, in the firm's announcements of its investments in BuyMed and Vietcetera. No current board seat could be sourced.
