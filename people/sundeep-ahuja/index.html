Biography

Sundeep Ahuja is the Founder and General Partner of Climate Capital, an early-stage climate investor. Climate Capital's own newsletter identifies him in that role in a post dated 28 May 2026, and his personal site records '2019: founded Climate Capital, an early stage fund for climate impact startups', hyperlinked to climatecapital.co. The firm's own 'Climate Capital 5.0' post dates the start of the effort to 2015, when he bought the climatecapital.co domain and launched the first climate SPV. His personal site lists him as a co-founder of RichRelevance and Sparked, founder of blissmo in 2010 (acquired by Garten), co-founder of the Silicon Climate accelerator in 2014, and records three years in business development at Amazon Web Services and one and a half years as a Partner at Republic, plus earlier product roles at MySpace and eBay, marketing at Kiva.org and banking at JPMorgan. He holds a BS in Computer Science from Stanford. The Invested In Climate podcast page for episode 29 says he has 'invested in over 100 companies through syndicates that he's led'. No source found in this research ties him individually to a specific named portfolio company, so notableInvestments is empty; no current board seat could be verified from any portfolio-company filing or board page.
