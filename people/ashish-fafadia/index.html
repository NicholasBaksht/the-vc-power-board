Biography

Ashish Fafadia is a Partner at Blume Ventures, where his bio states he leads the Fintech and Growth portfolios and supports portfolio companies including Slice, Purplle, Turtlemint, Jai Kisan and Stellapps. He joined Blume in 2012 as an early member of its leadership and helped establish Constellation Blu, a transaction and financial advisory firm. Before Blume he worked at Times Group, Standard Chartered Bank and State Bank of India. He is a Chartered Accountant and Company Secretary by qualification and is based in Mumbai.
