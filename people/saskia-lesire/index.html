Biography

Saskia Lesire is an Associate at VIVES Partners, where she supports sourcing and evaluation of investment opportunities in therapeutics and medtech. She holds a PhD in biomedical sciences from KU Leuven, with doctoral research in molecular medicine, target validation and assay development, carried out in collaboration with KU Leuven's Centre for Drug Design and Discovery. She completed technology and knowledge transfer training through Leuven Research & Development and holds the Guberna Director Effectiveness certification. She joined VIVES in 2025. She is the most junior investing professional listed on the firm's team page and is included here as an investing professional rather than administrative staff.
