Biography

Ilonka Jankovich is a Venture Partner at Rubio Impact Ventures, where she leads the People Power team and focuses on growth-stage work-technology companies. Rubio describes her as a serial entrepreneur who has built and successfully exited two companies in the PeopleTech field; the firm does not name them, so they are not recorded here. She founded the Randstad Innovation Fund, the corporate venture arm of the Dutch staffing group, and has served as a non-executive board member of technology companies including Exact. She sat on Rubio's Advisory Board from 2019 before joining as Venture Partner in January 2022. On appointment she said her contribution would be making investments in the future of work.
