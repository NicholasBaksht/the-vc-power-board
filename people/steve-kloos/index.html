Biography

Steve Kloos is a Partner at Burnt Island Ventures. A PhD technologist by training, he spent his corporate career in the water industry, including serving as Membrane Technology Leader at GE Water, one of the sector's largest treatment businesses. He moved into venture investing as a Partner at True North Venture Partners, where he backed early stage water and energy technologies, and he served as founding board chair of the water innovation organization Current. At Burnt Island he applies technical and commercial depth to diligence on treatment, filtration, and water infrastructure companies.
