Biography

Shilpi Kumar is a Partner at Third Sphere. Her firm bio describes the firm as 'Third Sphere (fka Urban Us), an investment platform focused on urbantech and climate solutions', and says she focuses on customer development and product strategy for early-stage companies. It states she previously led go-to-market and sales at Filament, a wireless networks startup, and that her investment background spans hardware, mobility, energy, IoT and robotics, with work alongside VTF, First Round Capital and Village Global on decarbonisation, urban infrastructure and climate mitigation. She holds a degree in Neuroscience and Economics from Duke University. She is listed as Partner and Executive Officer on the Third Sphere Fund IV, LP Form D filed in May 2022. No investment announcement or filing found in this research attributes a specific portfolio company to her individually, so notableInvestments is empty.
