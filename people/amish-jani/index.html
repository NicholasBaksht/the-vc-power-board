Biography

Amish Jani is a Founder and Partner at FirstMark, where his bio describes investing across the cloud landscape including Enterprise Applications, Commerce Infrastructure and Vertical Software. He was previously a Partner with Pequot Ventures, and was one of four Pequot Ventures managing directors who spun the group out in 2008 to form FirstMark Capital. He holds a B.S. and an M.B.A. from the Wharton School of Business at the University of Pennsylvania and serves on Wharton's Undergraduate Executive Board. He is a co-founder of the AI NY summit and leads the FirstMark Founders Guild.
