Biography

Sean Park is the Founder, Group CIO and General Partner at Anthemis, the fintech investment firm whose about page names him and Amy Nauiokas as its founders. Before Anthemis he sat on the Global Markets Management Committee at Dresdner Kleinwort, where he ran several businesses including its digital markets initiative and authored the AmazonBay video on the future of finance in 2005. Anthemis lists him as a founding investor in Betfair, The Climate Corporation and Markit and an early investor in Zoopla, and as a board director at Cowbell Cyber, Kaiko, Maxwell, TAINA Tech, Tide, TreasurySpring and Silvur. He holds a bachelor's degree in materials science from Rice University and is based in Geneva.
