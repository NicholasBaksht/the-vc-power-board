Biography

Luke Harris is General Counsel and Chief Operating Officer at Fifth Wall. Before joining the firm he served as General Counsel at a credit-focused asset manager with $5B in assets under management, and earlier held in-house legal positions at Morgan Stanley Investment Management and with the Alternative Investments group at UBS Financial Services. He began his legal career in the Private Funds Group at Proskauer and was later an associate in the Private Funds practice at Weil, Gotshal and Manges. He graduated from Excelsior College with a degree in Liberal Studies and received his JD from Brooklyn Law School.
