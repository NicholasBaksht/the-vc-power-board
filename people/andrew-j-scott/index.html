Biography

Andrew J Scott is Founding Partner of 7percent Ventures. His page on the firm's site says he is a "Serial entrepreneur turned Founding Partner 7percent Ventures" who "has been investing in deeptech for >10 years", and that as a founder he built "the world's first online digital news archive (BritishPathe.com), world's first location based mobile social network (playtxt) and an early personalisation AI startup (Rummble)". The same page says he "previously sat on the board of UnLtd World (sold to Guardian Media Group), the Silicon Valley Advisory group to the EU Commission and SVB's Early Stage Advisory Board", that in 2009 he co-founded ICE, a not-for-profit founder community, and that he is Co-Founder and Chair of DragonChasers, a GP and LP community. It states that he currently sits on the boards of Universal Quantum, Aerovolt and Greenjets. No education is given on the firm's site.
