Alice Bentinck

Cofounder and CEO at Entrepreneur First · Joined 2011

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Alice Bentinck co-founded Entrepreneur First with Matt Clifford in 2011 and is listed on the firm's own site as Cofounder and CEO. She had previously been the firm's Chief Product Officer, and before founding it she worked as a management consultant at McKinsey & Company from 2009 to 2011. She also co-founded Code First: Girls with Clifford and co-authored the book How to be a Founder with him. She was appointed MBE for services to business in 2016 and served on the UK AI Council from 2019 to 2022.
