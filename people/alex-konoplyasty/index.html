Biography

Alex Konoplyasty is Co-founder and Managing Partner of Flashpoint. His firm profile records a Master's degree in International Economic Relations and prior roles as Director and Associate in Investment Banking at UBS covering media, telecom and financial services in the CIS region with over 20 transactions completed, Investment Banking Analyst at Morgan Stanley in European TMT and leveraged finance, and Consultant in Transaction Support at PricewaterhouseCoopers. His profile ties him to Guesty, Chess.com, MarketMan, ChiliPiper, OfficeRnD and Mize. In the firm's August 2022 VC III announcement he is styled Co-founder and General Partner and Managing Partner for VC III. He is named in that release as the source of the firm's claim about expat founders from Eastern Europe and Israel being more cost-efficient than the average US startup.
