Biography

Miguel Nigorra is a Partner at Fifth Wall and Head of Europe, leading the firm's investment activities and strategic initiatives across the region. He spent over nine years at McKinsey in its digital practice, working out of the Madrid, New York and London offices and leading teams across Europe on real estate engagements. He was also Business Development & Strategy Manager at Fon Wireless. He holds a BA from Universidad Pontificia de Comillas in Madrid and an MBA from Columbia Business School. He is based in London.
