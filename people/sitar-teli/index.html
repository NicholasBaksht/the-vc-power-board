Biography

Sitar Teli is a Managing Partner of Connect Ventures in London and has been a designated member of Connect Ventures LLP since 1 April 2013. Her firm bio states that she "has been a VC since 2008, focusing on early-stage investments in consumer and B2B2C enablement in marketplaces, market networks and networked SaaS", and that before Connect she was with Doughty Hanson Technology Ventures, where she led their Series A round in SoundCloud. The bio lists her investments as ai-coustics, Hopkins and Deepstash. She holds a dual degree in Mechanical Engineering and Economics from Duke University. Companies House records show she previously held directorships at Fiit Limited, Second Nature Healthy Habits Ltd, CharlieHR Ltd, Charlie OS Limited and DeepHealth UK Limited, all now resigned; her only current UK directorships are Connect Ventures fund and general partner entities.
