Biography

Wynston Reed is a Principal at Clean Energy Ventures, promoted to that title in February 2026. His expertise is in product financialization and federal energy policy, which he applies to help climate tech startups structure financing and navigate government programs. He was previously a management consultant at Grant Thornton working with multinational energy companies and federal credit programs, including research for the U.S. Department of Energy's Loan Programs Office. During his graduate studies he was Business Strategy Lead at Arkea Bio and a summer associate investor at Energy Impact Partners, where he built a monitoring database of climate tech startups with diverse founding teams. He holds a Master of Finance from MIT Sloan, where he was a Dean's Fellow, and dual undergraduate degrees in Finance and International Business from the University of Maryland.
