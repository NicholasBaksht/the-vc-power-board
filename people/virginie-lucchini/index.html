Biography

Virginie Lucchini is listed on the Axeleo Capital team page as Partner, Head of Middle Office. Her bio on the firm's axc.vc site gives her title as Partner, Head of Middle Office, Compliance & ESG Officer, states that she joined Axeleo Capital in February 2020, that she is an ESSEC alumna, and that between 2015 and 2018 at Younited Credit she created the compliance department and ultimately served as General Counsel. Her role is an operations, compliance and ESG role rather than an investing role, and no investment or board seat is attributed to her by any source found.
