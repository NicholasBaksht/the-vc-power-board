Biography

Peter Diamandis is a partner at BOLD Capital Partners, the venture firm he co-founded in 2015 to invest in exponential technologies and longevity companies. Before and alongside BOLD he founded the XPRIZE Foundation, where he is Executive Chairman, and was the Executive Founder of Singularity University, which he started with Ray Kurzweil in 2008. He is also a co-founder and Vice-Chairman of Celularity and Vaxxinity and co-founder and Chairman of Fountain Life, and by XPRIZE's own account has started over 25 companies across health-tech, space, venture capital and education. He holds degrees in molecular genetics and aerospace engineering from MIT and an M.D. from Harvard Medical School.
