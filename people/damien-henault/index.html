Biography

Damien Henault is Managing Director and Partner at Forgepoint Capital International, leading the firm's cybersecurity, AI and infrastructure software investments across Europe, Israel and Latin America. He has over 30 years of experience as a technology investor, M&A advisor and corporate development executive. Before Forgepoint he was a partner at TempoCap and at Andurance Ventures, a senior director of strategy and corporate development at Hewlett-Packard, and a TMT M&A investment banker at J.P. Morgan.
