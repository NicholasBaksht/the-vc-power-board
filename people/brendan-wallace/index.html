Biography

Brendan Wallace is Chief Executive Officer and Chief Investment Officer of Fifth Wall, which he co-founded with Brad Greiwe in 2016. Before Fifth Wall he co-founded Identified, a workforce optimization data and analytics company that raised $33M and was acquired by Workday in 2014, and co-founded Cabify. He began his career at Goldman Sachs in the real estate, hospitality and gaming group and then worked in The Blackstone Group's real estate private equity practice. He has led more than 60 angel investments.
