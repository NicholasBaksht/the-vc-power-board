Biography

Andre Jabban is a Partner and Head of Capital Formation at Prime Movers Lab, where he leads fundraising across the firm's funds, special purpose vehicles, and portfolio companies. He was previously Partner and Head of Fund Investor Relations at Flagship Pioneering, where he managed capital formation efforts including the firm's 3.6 billion dollar Fund VIII. Before Flagship he spent a decade as Head of Alternative Investments for the Polk Wealth Management Group at Morgan Stanley, sourcing and managing alternative investments and co-investments, and earlier held roles at Merrill Lynch, York Capital Management, and P. Schoenfeld Asset Management. He holds a BBA in international business and marketing from Loyola University Maryland and earned the CAIA designation in 2016.
