Biography

Denis Mosolov is Managing Partner at Flashpoint, having joined in 2021 as Managing Partner of the Growth Debt Fund, where his profile says he oversees strategy, deal sourcing, execution and portfolio oversight across the CEE, Baltic, Finnish and Israeli markets. Before Flashpoint he spent thirteen years at Macquarie Group as Managing Director and Head of South East Europe for its Infrastructure and Real Assets division. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Commerce and Actuarial Studies. His profile names no board seats or individual portfolio companies.
