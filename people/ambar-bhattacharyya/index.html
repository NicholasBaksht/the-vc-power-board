Biography

Ambar Bhattacharyya is one of the six people listed on the Maverick Ventures investment team page. Aligned Marketplace's 27 January 2025 funding announcement quotes him as "Ambar Bhattacharyya, Managing Director, Maverick Ventures", which is the most recent sourced statement of his title; the Maverick Ventures page itself prints no titles. He filed Section 16 forms as an insider of Hims & Hers Health, Inc. from 2021 and is named in that company's proxy statements from 2021 through 2024, but he is not among the directors listed in the company's 2025 proxy statement, so no current board seat is recorded. No education or prior employment was sourced from an acceptable first-party page.
