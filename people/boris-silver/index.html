Biography

Boris Silver is co-founder and President of FundersClub. His FundersClub profile gives his title as "Co-Founder and President, FundersClub" and states that before FundersClub he worked at the global agribusiness Bunge in its Commodity Risk Management Training Program. The profile states that he graduated Summa Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Science in Economics from The Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, that he is a Y Combinator alumnus, and that he has been named to the Forbes 30 Under 30 in Tech list and the Silicon Valley Business Journal's 40 Under 40. His role is independently confirmed by the SEC Form D filed by FCVC Fund III L.P. in June 2022, which names him as President of FundersClub Inc., the manager of the fund's general partner. No source read for this record ties him personally to a specific portfolio investment or names him as a director of a portfolio company, so notableInvestments and boardSeats are empty.
