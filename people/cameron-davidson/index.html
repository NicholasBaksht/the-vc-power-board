Biography

Cameron Davidson is Operating Partner at The Production Board, where she leads all non-investment activities including internal operations, finance, and investor relations, and has been with the firm since its founding. She previously managed the CEO's office at The Climate Corporation, handling executive communications and spearheading integration work after Monsanto acquired the company in 2013. Before TPB she directed water management projects across Israel, the West Bank, Jordan, Australia, Indonesia, and China. She holds a bachelor's degree in Conservation and Resource Studies from the University of California, Berkeley, and a master's degree in Integrated Water Management from the University of Queensland in Australia.
