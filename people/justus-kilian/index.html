Biography

Justus Kilian is a Partner at Space Capital, a venture capital firm investing in the space economy. He was a Principal at the firm as of 2020 and held the Partner title by late 2022. He serves on the boards of portfolio companies ClearRoad and Lunar Outpost and leads Space Talent, a career platform at the intersection of space and technology. He previously worked at Acumen, an early stage venture firm investing in emerging markets, and before that at Merrill Lynch, where he specialized in private market investments. He holds an MBA from the University of Chicago Booth School of Business and is a CFA charterholder.
