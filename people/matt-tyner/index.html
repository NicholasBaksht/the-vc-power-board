Biography

Matt Tyner is Managing Partner at Elevate Ventures, where his team-member page states he manages investment activities for Elevate's early and growth-stage investment funds. Before Elevate he was Vice President of Finance & Operations at SmarterHQ through its acquisition by Wunderkind in November 2020, and after leading the post-merger integration served as Wunderkind's Vice President of People Operations and General Manager of its Indianapolis presence. His Elevate biography describes him as an operator and financial professional who has led businesses through funding rounds from angel through Series C. No education is published on his Elevate Ventures page and none has been recorded here. No individual company has been tied to him by a first-party source, so notableInvestments is empty.
