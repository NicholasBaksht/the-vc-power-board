Biography

Brad Greiwe is Co-Founder and Chairman at Fifth Wall, where he contributes to strategic vision and leads discussions with strategic limited partners. He also serves as Chief Executive Officer of Quarterra. Before Fifth Wall he co-founded Invitation Homes, where as CTO he built the technology stack supporting valuation, acquisition, rehabilitation, leasing and management of over 80,000 homes across 17 markets. He started his career in investment banking at UBS, where his work included the $26B sale of Hilton Hotels to Blackstone, and later worked in real estate private equity at Tishman Speyer and Starwood Capital.
