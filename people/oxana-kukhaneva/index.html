Biography

Oxana Kukhaneva is a Senior Venture Partner at Seventure Partners, based in Geneva, Switzerland. Her Seventure bio says she joined the firm in 2016 as a Venture Partner covering Benelux, Scandinavia, Northern Switzerland and the United States, and specialises in nutrition, foodtech, digital food and nutrition, and microbiome applications in those fields. It states she has more than 12 years of experience in innovation and development capital within life sciences, particularly health, nutrition and consumer goods, that she previously held an investment role at Inventages, a health-focused fund whose primary investor was Nestle, and that she began her career at a Russian digital start-up working on strategy and business development. She holds a BA in Economics and an MA and MBA from IMD in Switzerland. Her Seventure bio names Cambrooke, MycoTechnology, Machtfit, Foodsmart, One Bio (formerly BCD Biosciences), Allozymes and La Vie among her investments.
