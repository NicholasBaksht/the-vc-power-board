Biography

Max Altschuler is Founder and General Partner of GTMfund, the title his firm's own team page gives him. He is the only natural person named on the related persons lists of the Form D filings for GTMfund II, LP and GTMfund III, LP, where he is described as Executive Officer and Manager of the General Partner of the Issuer. Before GTMfund he was the eighth employee at Udemy, a fact stated in GTMfund's own February 2026 Eightball AI announcement, and he founded Sales Hacker, which TechCrunch reports he sold to Outreach in 2018 and has since bought back; Sales Hacker, Inc. filed a Form D with the SEC in April 2015. TechCrunch's 4 February 2025 article reports that GTMfund's first fund launched in 2021, raised $22 million and had about 250 operator limited partners, and that the second fund closed at $54 million. He appears on Backblaze's published GTM Advisory Committee, which the company's own filed presentation distinguishes from its board. No education, join year, IPO count or exit count could be sourced.

Previous Experience
