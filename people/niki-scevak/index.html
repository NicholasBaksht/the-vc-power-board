Biography

Niki Scevak is a Partner at Blackbird and, per Wikipedia, co-founded the firm in 2012 with Rick Baker. His Blackbird profile states that he founded Startmate on returning to Sydney in 2010 and that he started two software companies before investing, having previously lived in New York. He is based in Sydney and states an interest in biology use cases outside healthcare, autonomous vehicles and robots. He is the named author or co-author of Blackbird's investment announcements for Aquila, Nomad Atomics, Clutch, Index and Superstat. No board seat is recorded. Blackbird's investment announcement posts, twenty-four of which were read on 7 September 2026, name the authoring investor but contain no sentence appointing anyone to a portfolio company board, and no portfolio company board page naming this person was found.
