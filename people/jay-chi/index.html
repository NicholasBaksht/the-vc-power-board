Biography

Jay Chi is a co-founder of Makers Fund, a venture capital firm specialising in interactive entertainment that was founded in 2016 and closed a 250 million dollar Fund IV in August 2026. Before Makers Fund he spent eleven and a half years at McKinsey and Company, where he led its global video games practice. He has been named as the Makers Fund partner on the firm's investments in Alta, whose board he joined, and Human Computer.
