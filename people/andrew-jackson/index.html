Biography

Andrew Jackson is General Partner and Chief AI Officer at B Capital, leading the firm's global artificial intelligence strategy across investment, portfolio management and operations. He brings more than 20 years of experience in AI, machine learning and data. Prior to B Capital he was chief AI officer at G42 and founded Inception, an AI lab that built a family of open-source AI models in the MENA region. Earlier he played a key role in expanding Palantir's Middle East presence.
