Biography

Thanachat Tangsriwong is the representative of CyberAgent Capital's Thailand office. The firm's own profile states he worked at the ChangeFusion Institute, a Thai social innovation and social enterprise consulting company, and at the boutique financial advisory firm PrimeStreet Advisory Group, and that he joined CyberAgent Capital in October 2013, since when he has been engaged in investment activity and management support for venture companies in Thailand. He holds a bachelor's degree in economics from Thammasat University in Thailand and a master's degree in corporate finance from Cass Business School, now Bayes Business School, in the United Kingdom. No portfolio companies or board seats are listed on his profile page.
