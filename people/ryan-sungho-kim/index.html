Biography

Ryan Sungho Kim is a Co-founder and Partner at Hashed. His profile on the firm's site says he focuses on discovering and supporting Asian founders in the Infrastructure, DeFi, Gaming and Entertainment sectors and helping them connect with global resources. Before Hashed he exited LOOKET, a mobile solution for small and medium enterprises, through a sale to the big data company Recobell; at LOOKET he led the development and planning of recommendation systems. He holds a bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering from KAIST. He wrote Hashed's own announcement of its investment in the Ethereum based rollup project Taiko in May 2024, where the byline reads Founding Partner at Hashed.
