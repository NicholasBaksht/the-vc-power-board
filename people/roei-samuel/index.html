Biography

Roei Samuel is an Operating Partner at Haatch. His Haatch biography states he founded Connectd in 2022, a B2C subscription platform for the fractional and portfolio career economy, and scaled it from zero to £12m ARR in under four years through a fully remote go-to-market motion. Previously he founded RealSport, a creator and content platform that reached 7m monthly active users and secured enterprise partnerships with major sports rights holders including the NBA and NFL (the full list in his Haatch biography also names English football's top division), before being acquired by Gfinity in 2018. As a Haatch Operating Partner he works with founders on brand, distribution and network effects, positioning and demand generation, and B2C subscription mechanics. Haatch's Operating Partners are portfolio-support roles rather than investment decision-makers. No education, join year, board seat, IPO count or exit count could be sourced, and no investment is attributed to him.
