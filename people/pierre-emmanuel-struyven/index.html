Biography

Pierre-Emmanuel Struyven is Managing Partner of Supernova Invest. The firm's team page states: "Pierre-Emmanuel has been part of the team since 2019 as Managing Partner. He has more than 25 years of experience in innovation and digital industries." He is identified as President and Managing Partner of Supernova Invest in the firm's 21 March 2024 announcement of the Orano Venture Fund, and as Chairman of Supernova Invest in November 2023 coverage of the agri and agrotech fund launched with Credit Agricole. No education, prior employers, portfolio board seats, IPO count or exit count could be sourced.
