View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Evan Cohen is listed as an Operating Partner at Human Ventures. Human Ventures announced his arrival in January 2020 as a Venture Partner, citing more than 20 years working with technology and media companies on business and product strategy, team building, operations and fundraising. TechCrunch reported that he joined Foursquare as general manager in January 2010 after serving as a vice president at Bebo, later becoming Foursquare's chief operating officer, and that he joined Lyft in December 2015 as director of East Coast operations. At Human Ventures he launched the Humans in the Wild program for experienced entrepreneurs. His education and individual investments are not published by the firm.
