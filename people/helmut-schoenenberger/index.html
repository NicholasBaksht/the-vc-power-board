View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Helmut Schönenberger is a Managing Partner of UVC Partners and is registered as one of three managing directors of Unternehmertum Venture Capital Partners GmbH. He is simultaneously co-founder and CEO of UnternehmerTUM, the centre for innovation and business creation at the Technical University of Munich, and serves as TUM's Vice President Entrepreneurship and as a professor at the TUM School of Management. UVC describes him as a founder, aerospace engineer and venture capitalist who works with sustainable ventures on strategy, recruiting, business development and financing. He sits on the supervisory board of the Tech for Net Zero Alliance of the German Energy Agency (dena), the activity for which UVC is entered in the German Bundestag lobby register. The firm attributes KONUX and Isar Aerospace to him. No education details are published on the UVC team page.
