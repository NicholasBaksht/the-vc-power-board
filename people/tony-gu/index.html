Biography

Tony Gu is a General Partner of NGC Ventures. The firm's team page states that prior to founding NGC Ventures he was General Partner at Rhodium Capital, a cross-border buyout advisory firm, where he focused on large buyout deals in North Asian countries and completed multiple deals in the technology, financial services and consumer sectors with a total transaction value exceeding $1B. He holds a dual EMBA from INSEAD and Tsinghua, a Master's degree in Computer Engineering from Nanyang Technological University and a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Zhejiang University. A 20 September 2018 PR Newswire release issued for NGC's investment in Muzika names him as Founder of NEO Global Capital. No first-party source read in this research ties him to a specific named portfolio investment as an individual, or to a current board seat.
