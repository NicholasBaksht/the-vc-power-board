Biography

Simon Sharp is a Senior Partner at Global Ventures, overseeing the firm's investment practice and operations. He joined Global Ventures as a Principal in early 2020 and brings over a decade of investment experience across venture capital and corporate finance. He was previously part of the investment team at Committed Capital, a London-based venture capital fund specialising in early-stage technology investments. He is a Chartered Accountant with the ICAEW and a member of the Chartered Institute for Securities and Investment, and holds a BSc in Mathematics from the University of Warwick.
