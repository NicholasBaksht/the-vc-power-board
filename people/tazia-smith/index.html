Biography

Tazia Smith is Managing Partner at Closed Loop Partners and Chief Executive Officer of Closed Loop Capital Management, where she oversees the firm's investment group, stakeholder management, capital formation and investor relations, and sits on the firm's Management Committee. She brings nearly 20 years of investment banking experience with institutional family offices across the capital structure, including as a Managing Director at Deutsche Bank, where she helped establish the Key Client Partners business and worked with the CEO strategy team on sustainable finance, and seven years at Goldman Sachs in institutional cross-asset sales. In 2019 she co-founded the IMP+ACT Alliance, now part of the GIIN. She holds a BA in Environmental Science and Policy from Duke University, magna cum laude, and is a CFA charterholder.
