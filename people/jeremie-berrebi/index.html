Jeremie Berrebi

Co-Founder at Kima Ventures · Joined 2010

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Jeremie Berrebi co-founded Kima Ventures with Xavier Niel in 2010 and left the firm in 2015. He was an editor for ZDNet France before co-founding Net2one in 1997, a news search and clipping tool that was sold to the TNS Group in the UK in 2004, and he co-founded the online shop platform Zlio in 2006. Kima Ventures states that he was already a shareholder in more than 20 startups around the world before Kima was created. He is based in Bnei Brak, Israel.
