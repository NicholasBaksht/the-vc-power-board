Biography

Gordon Daugherty is listed on the Capital Factory team page as Co-Founder & Chairman. His own site, Shockwave Innovations, states that he holds a computer science degree from Baylor University, began his corporate career at IBM and Compaq, spent eight years in the videoconferencing industry including four years as president of an Israel-based videoconferencing company that he says grew from $5 million to $36 million in revenue and listed on Euronext, and was a senior executive at the Austin network management company NetQoS, which he says reached more than $55 million in revenue before being acquired by CA Technologies. The same biography states he was a founding advisor to the digital advertising company MediaMind, which had a Nasdaq IPO in 2010, that he has been a board director for several Austin technology companies, and that he is a senior contributor to Texas CEO Magazine. He is the author of the book Startup Success: Funding the Early Stages of Your Venture.
