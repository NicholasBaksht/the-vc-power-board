Biography

Deborah Quazzo is Managing Partner of GSV Ventures, an education and workforce technology venture fund she co-founded in 2016 that manages more than $500 million across three funds. She co-founded the ASU+GSV Summit, an annual education innovation gathering that has grown to more than 15,000 attendees. Her investments include Coursera, Nearpod, Handshake, QuillBot, PhysicsWallah, ClassDojo, Quizizz, and MasterClass, and she serves on the boards of Ascend Learning, Degreed, Uplimit, Toddle, Ellucian, and Turnitin. Earlier she co-founded ThinkEquity Partners, a technology investment bank acquired in 2007, and worked in investment banking at Merrill Lynch. She holds a BA in History from Princeton University and an MBA from Harvard University.
