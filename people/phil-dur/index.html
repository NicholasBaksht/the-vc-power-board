Biography

Phil Dur is Co-Founder and Managing Partner of PeakSpan Capital. His firm bio states he has been a software investor for over 25 years and has served on the boards of over 40 growth stage companies, and that his sector expertise covers Customer Experience Management and Human Capital Management. Before co-founding PeakSpan he spent ten years at Investor Growth Capital leading the software investment team, and earlier worked at Morgan Stanley Venture Partners and began his career at Morgan Stanley Capital Partners. He is a graduate of Princeton University and the Stanford University Graduate School of Business and served as an officer in the U.S. Army Reserve for seven years.
