Biography

Nora Frey is a Venture Partner at Seventure Partners based in Basel, Switzerland, concentrating on nutrition, foodtech and microbiome-related opportunities. Her Seventure bio says she joined Seventure as a Venture Partner in June 2021 following two years as an Advisor focused on Food and Nutrition. It states she has over 20 years of experience in healthcare research, investment, management, strategy and business development, has co-founded several companies and served as board member and chairman across biotech, nutraceuticals and food production and finance. Her career began in 1998 at Bellevue Group in Switzerland as part of the BB Biotech team; she subsequently established Adamant Biomedical Investments AG, a healthcare asset management firm, as Chief Investment Officer, and in 2008 co-founded Cederberg GmbH, a healthcare marketing and distribution business. She holds Swiss and German citizenship, a PhD in Biochemistry/Immunology and a Master's degree in Microbiology. Her bio refers to board and chairman roles in general terms without naming current companies, so no current board seats are recorded.
