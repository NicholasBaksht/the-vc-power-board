Judith Li

Partner at Lilly Asia Ventures · Joined 2013

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Judith Li is described on the site of portfolio company Khartis Therapeutics as "a Partner at Lilly Asia Ventures (LAV), based in Menlo Park and focused on early and growth stage investments across biopharmaceuticals, medical devices, and diagnostics both domestically and cross-border", and that biography states she joined LAV in 2013. It records a B.A. in Neurobiology from Harvard and an M.B.A. from Harvard Business School, and prior experience at McKinsey's New York office, hospital administration at Partners Healthcare, and co-founding an interventional nephrology medical device venture. The same biography lists past board appointments at Inhibrx, Structure, TMunity and Just Biotherapeutics; those are written in the past tense and none was confirmed as current against a company filing, so none is recorded. The one board seat recorded here is Avenzo Therapeutics, Inc., where she is named as a Director in the related persons list of the company's Form D filed 5 September 2025. LAV publishes no team page, so no title statement from the firm itself is available for her.
