Biography

Andrew Murray is Managing Director, Venture Investing at Citi Ventures in London, where his bio on Citi's site says he leads the firm's investing coverage for the Commercial, Corporate and Investment Banking businesses and cross-business Client organization activities. His stated expertise is market infrastructure, institutional lending, capital markets and digital platforms, built over more than 15 years across Oliver Wyman's Finance & Risk practice, Barclays Capital's Strategy and Planning function and Deutsche Bank's Global Markets Strategic Investments function, where he led over 40 transactions and held governance positions at more than a dozen portfolio companies. He previously served as European investing lead for Citigroup's Markets Strategic Investing team. He holds a Masters in Engineering from Emmanuel College, Cambridge and has completed all three levels of the CFA programme.
