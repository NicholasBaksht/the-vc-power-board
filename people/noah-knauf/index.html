Biography

Noah Knauf is a general partner at Bond, which he co-founded in 2019 with Mary Meeker, Mood Rowghani and Juliet de Baubigny after the Kleiner Perkins digital growth team spun out as an independent firm. He joined Kleiner Perkins in 2016 as a general partner in its digital growth practice, arriving after about a decade at Warburg Pincus that began in 2006 and focused on healthcare and healthcare services. His Bond bio states that he focuses on investments in high growth technology companies, with a particular focus on healthcare, fintech, gaming and frontier technology, and lists active investments including Eucalyptus, Locus, Maven Clinic, SWORD and Tomorrow Health. Bond's site lists his current board seats as AKASA, Saildrone, Sidecar Health and Sunday.
