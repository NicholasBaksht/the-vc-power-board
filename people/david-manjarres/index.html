David Manjarres

Senior Partner - head of Digital Technologies team at Seventure Partners · Joined 2012

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

David Manjarres is Senior Partner and head of Seventure Partners' Digital Technologies team. His Seventure bio says he joined the firm in July 2012 as part of the acquisition of Masseran Gestion and has managed the Digital Technologies team since that time. His career in private equity dates from 2000; he spent six years at CDC-Entreprises as a fund-of-funds investor focusing on venture capital and seed funds, then worked at Tikehau Capital Advisors, and in early 2008 participated in establishing Masseran Gestion to launch its venture capital operations. Before private equity he spent seven years at the Ministry of Defence leading research and development projects in electronics and then providing digital intelligence on economic and strategic matters. He is a graduate of ENSTA Bretagne and holds a Master's degree in Management of Technology and Innovation from the University of Paris IX Dauphine. Seventure's institutional funds page states he manages the Digital Opportunities Fund. His Seventure bio names Arlettie, EBlink, Ipdia, Minutebuzz, Nexess, Presto, Scaleo Chip, Pitchy and Reelevant among his portfolio companies.
