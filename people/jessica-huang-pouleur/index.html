Biography

Jessica Huang Pouleur is a Partner at Openspace and leads Openspace Growth, the firm's growth strategy, from Singapore. She sits on the Openspace Growth Investment Committee and the Executive Committee and chairs the firm's ESG committee. Before Openspace she headed Corporate Strategy and Business Development for Asia-Pacific and the Middle East at The Walt Disney Company, led Providence Equity Partners' investment team in Southeast Asia, and worked in investment banking at Citigroup. She is a member of the Global Tech Council of the Global Private Capital Association, co-chairs the Singapore chapter of PEWIN and was a member of the Milken Institute's Young Leaders Circle. She holds a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science from Barnard College, Columbia University.
