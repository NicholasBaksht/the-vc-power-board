Biography

Lukas Weder is a Founding Partner of Founderful, which he co-founded in 2019 as Wingman Ventures with Pascal Mathis and Alex Stöckl. The firm's own releases identify him as a co-founder of the Swiss food delivery company EAT.ch. He is recorded in the Swiss commercial register as a member of the board of directors of Founderful AG (CHE-492.933.597). No education, portfolio board seats, IPO count or exit count could be sourced.
