Biography

Ben Ling is the founder and General Partner of Bling Capital, the seed-stage fund he started in 2018. Before founding Bling he was a General Partner at Khosla Ventures, where he led deals in Wattpad, Tapingo, GitLab, True Accord and Webflow. Earlier he was Senior Director of Search and Local Business Products at Google, Senior Director of Partnerships and Platform at YouTube, and the first director of the Facebook platform. As an angel he backed Airtable, Lyft, Square, Palantir, Quora, Gusto and Udemy. His firm bio states he has backed 19 companies that reached billion-dollar valuations.
