Biography

Assaf Jacobi is a Venture Partner at N47 based in Israel, covering the firm's Applications and Infrastructure categories. His N47 profile says he joined in 2019, having previously invested in mature companies at TPG Israel, and that he co-founded Jibe Ventures, a seed-stage fund based in Israel. His listed past investments include Avanan, which was acquired by Check Point, and Guardicore, which was acquired by Akamai, as well as BigPanda and VAST Data. He describes a Product First approach and measures an investment by how much genuine resonance a product sparks with users.
