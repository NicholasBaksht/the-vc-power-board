Biography

Ash Panchang is a Senior Associate at Bling Capital, where his bio states he focuses on opportunities in consumer, SaaS and fintech. Before joining the firm he was Director of Operations at Burrow, ran supply chain analytics at Peloton ahead of its IPO, and scaled customer experience operations at Blue Apron through its IPO. His bio also records building proprietary sourcing tools for private equity teams, working with alternative datasets to support public investing at Viking Global, and leading the MBA Fund.
