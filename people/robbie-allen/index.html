Robbie Allen

Biography

Robbie Allen is a General Partner of the NC Tweener Fund. The fund's about page states that he holds a Computer Science degree from East Carolina University and two Master's degrees from MIT and describes him as a serial entrepreneur. His own speaker biography at automated.co gives his current role as Managing Director of Automated Consulting Group, states that he founded Automated Insights in 2007 (acquired by Vista Equity Partners), Infinia ML (acquired by Aspirion) and Bionic Health (acquired by Thrive), that he spent thirteen years at Cisco where he became its youngest Distinguished Engineer, that he wrote ten technology books for O'Reilly Media, that he was NC Tech Executive of the Year in 2017, and that he is a General Partner of the NC Tweener Fund, which it says has invested in 170+ startups across North Carolina. He is quoted as General Partner of the fund in NC IDEA's press release of 20 February 2026, in which he says an NC IDEA grant helped fund the first year of Automated Insights. No join year for the fund is published. The fund invests as an index and publishes no per-partner deal attribution, so no notableInvestments are recorded for him, and no current board seat could be sourced.
