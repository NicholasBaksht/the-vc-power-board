Biography

Morgan Schwanke is General Partner and Founder of Mana Ventures. The firm's About Us page states "Morgan is former founder and angel investor in 300+ startups" and names Restream, Unity Technologies and OnMyBlock, a venture-backed real estate technology company he founded. He is the firm's published point of contact. SEC filings corroborate both halves of that account: a Form D filed by OnMyBlock, Inc. of Woodside, CA on 12 February 2014 contains his name, and he is named as Executive Officer and "Manager of the general partner of the Issuer" and is the signatory on Form D filings for the Mana single-company vehicles MV Funds Attentive, LP, Rubrik Mana Ventures, LP, Mana Anduril 2, LP and Mana Ventures Flock Safety, LP. The adviser entity behind the Mana Ventures brand is MV Funds, LLC, an exempt reporting adviser registered at a San Francisco address. No education, no board seat, no IPO count and no exit count could be sourced for him.
