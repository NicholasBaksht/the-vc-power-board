Biography

Scott Weavers-Wright OBE is a Co-Founder and General Partner of Haatch. His Haatch biography states he founded Kiddicare.com, which was acquired in cash for £70m by Morrisons, and that he reached sixth on the Retail Week Power List. After Kiddicare he founded Elevaate in 2014, a business enabling supplier monetisation programmes across retailer websites; Haatch's own April 2026 article describes Elevaate as a 276x multiple exit. He was awarded an OBE in 2022 for services to Technology and Retail E-commerce Entrepreneurship. At Haatch he has invested in and developed businesses in ad tech, B2B SaaS and retail tech within the FMCG market. He provided Haatch's commentary on its January 2026 lead investment in AAZZUR. No education, join year, IPO count or exit count could be sourced, and no current board seat is stated by any source found.
