Biography

Ashley Stinton is Managing Partner on the NAR REACH US Residential team, one of the two lists Second Century Ventures publishes on its own team page. Second Century Ventures announced her appointment as executive director of the NAR REACH programme on 6 December 2022; by the 14 May 2025 cohort announcement her title had become managing partner, NAR REACH, and the nar-reach.com US Residential page lists her as Managing Partner. She has provided the programme's comment on the 2025 and 2026 REACH scale-up cohorts. No education or prior employer is published by SCV or NAR REACH and none has been recorded. Selection into a REACH cohort is not stated by the firm to be an investment, so no cohort company has been recorded as a notable investment for her.
