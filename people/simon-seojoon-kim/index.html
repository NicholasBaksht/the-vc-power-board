Biography

Simon Seojoon Kim is CEO & Managing Partner at Hashed. His profile on the firm's site states that he works with Korean IT companies including Kakao, Line, Watcha and Spoqa on their blockchain initiatives. Before Hashed he co-founded the ed-tech startup Knowre in the US and Korea and served as its Chief Product Officer; the firm's profile says Knowre was named one of Fast Company's World's Top 10 Most Innovative Companies in Education and was later acquired by Daekyo. The same profile states that he serves as a member of the 4th Revolution Committee of the South Korean Parliament, sits as a Director of the Korea Blockchain Association and is a member of the Busan Blockchain Free Zone committee. He studied Computer Science and Engineering at Pohang University of Science and Technology. Korean press reports of December 2021 and February 2026 identify him as the representative of Hashed and of Hashed Ventures. No source found in this research attributes a specific portfolio investment to him individually.
