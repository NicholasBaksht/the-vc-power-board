Biography

Michael Letta is General Partner and Chief Operating Officer of Human Ventures, where he oversees the firm's financial and operational functions. He holds a BS/BA from The Ohio State University and is a Certified Public Accountant. He was previously chief financial officer at charity: water, where his Forbes Business Council profile says he helped scale the organization from an NYC startup to a global operation. Earlier in his career he held roles at Thomson Media and Deloitte. In 2020 he co-launched Human Ventures' Humans in the Wild entrepreneur-in-residence program. His joining year and personal investment list are not published on Human Ventures' own site.
