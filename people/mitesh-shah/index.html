Biography

Mitesh Shah is a Co-Founder of Inflection Point Ventures. His IPV profile page states his career began at Mandhana Industries Limited in 2001, where it credits him with work on the company's 2010 IPO and its 2011 licensing deal with the Being Human brand; that he became CFO of Ola in December 2013; and that he joined BookMyShow in 2016 to lead its finance function and strategic business management. The page states he co-founded IPV in 2018. The Physis Capital site adds that he was a member of the Sequoia scout programme and names him a Partner of Physis Capital, the separate $50 million fund run by the IPV founders. No education record is published for him.
