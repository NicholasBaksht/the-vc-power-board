Biography

Emmanuel Perez-Duarte is a Partner, CFO and General Secretary of Elaia; his role is finance and administration rather than investing, and he is included here because he carries an explicit Partner title. He began his career as an economist at Exane BNP Paribas, then joined Renault in audit and asset-liability management. He contributed to the launch of Unilend, a French crowdlending pioneer, and led the financial and IT department of the Vranken-Pommery Monopole group before joining Elaia. He holds an MSc in management and economics from HEC Paris and the D.S.C.G. accounting diploma.
