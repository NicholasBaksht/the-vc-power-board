Biography

TC Meenakshisundaram, known as TCM, is the Founder and Vice Chairman of Chiratae Ventures, the Indian technology venture firm that rebranded from IDG Ventures India in October 2018. His firm bio credits him with over 38 years of industry experience including more than 18 years in venture, and states that he serves on the boards of Lenskart, AgroStar, Vayana, Kristal.ai, Metadome, Bounce, HUVIAiR and Pando. Earlier, as part of Walden International's team, he was involved with investments in Mindtree, Venture Infotek and Jobstreet, and he served as Chief Financial Officer and President of Corporate Services at Venture Infotek. He spent 14 years at Wipro, where he last served as General Manager of the eCommerce Division.
