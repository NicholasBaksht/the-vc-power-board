Biography

Michiel Kotting is a Partner at Northzone based in the firm's Amsterdam office, focused on early-stage investments in SaaS, healthcare and AI. Northzone states he joined in 2016 and names Personio, Stonly, Idoven, Forto and Katana among the companies he leads investments in. Before Northzone he was a Partner at Accel in London and founded Digital Jones, an AI company in the Bay Area that he sold to Shopping.com, having begun his career at BCG. He holds a graduate degree in physics from Delft University of Technology and an MBA from Harvard Business School, and was named on The Midas List Europe in 2023.
