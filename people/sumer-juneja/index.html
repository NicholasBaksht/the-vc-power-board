Biography

Sumer Juneja is a Managing Partner at SoftBank Investment Advisers and head of the firm's EMEA and India investing. Per SoftBank's own bio, he joined SoftBank in 2018 to set up its local investing presence in India before expanding his remit to also oversee the firm's investment activities in EMEA, and he was elevated to Managing Partner in 2021. Before SoftBank he was a Partner at Norwest Venture Partners and a founding member of Goldman Sachs' Asian Special Situations Group in India. He holds a degree in politics and economics from the London School of Economics.
