Biography

Peter Niederhauser is a Founding Partner of Redalpine and sits on its investment advisory team. According to the firm, he founded an enterprise software company while still studying; it became a market leader, grew to several hundred employees and went public in 1999. He moved from that IPO into angel investing, and co-founded Redalpine in 2006 with like-minded partners after seeing how hard founders found it to raise venture capital in Switzerland. Redalpine does not publish his education, board seats or individual portfolio companies. Outside work the firm notes he skis, sails, dives, kitesurfs and golfs.
