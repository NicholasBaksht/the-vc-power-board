Biography

David Milstein is Managing Partner and Head of Japan at Eight Roads. His profile states he re-joined Eight Roads Ventures in 2012 to build the venture capital business in Japan, and that from 2005 to 2012 he was at The Walt Disney Company as Vice President of the Interactive Media Group in Japan, where he wrote the business plan for and led the team that created the Disney Mobile on Softbank MVNO, later being promoted to General Manager of the Interactive Media Group and overseeing the expansion of Disney Mobile to include Docomo. Earlier he was a founding partner at Japan Transaction Partners and worked in finance and business development at Asahi Glass.
