Biography

Aidan Madigan-Curtis is a Partner at Eclipse Ventures who joined in 2021. She spent five years at IoT company Samsara as VP & GM of Connected Sites, and earlier worked in operations at Apple. She also worked as an investment analyst at Bridgewater Associates. Aidan’s experience spans scaling hardware products for consumer tech and industrial IoT, though her stated investment focus was not explicitly published.
