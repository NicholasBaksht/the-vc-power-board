Biography

Elise Szwajkowski is Chief Marketing Officer at Fifth Wall, where she leads the firm's global marketing, communications and investor relations strategy and supports the firm's engagement with its strategic limited partners and portfolio companies. Fifth Wall announced her promotion to the role in a release dated January 29, 2026. She was previously Vice President at Marino, where she launched and led the agency's PropTech practice, and earlier held senior roles at Akrete, Brandstyle Communications and Steinreich Communications. Her bio states that she represents Fifth Wall on the board of Runwise and serves as a Senior Advisor to Roam, and that she graduated summa cum laude from George Washington University with a degree in Communications.
