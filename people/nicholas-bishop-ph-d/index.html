Biography

Nicholas Bishop, Ph.D., is a Partner on Deerfield's Therapeutics team and joined the firm in 2014, providing research and analysis on healthcare companies. He spent four years as a research analyst covering the biotechnology sector at Cowen and Company. Before entering finance he was a biomedical research scientist at Harvard University and the Massachusetts Institute of Technology and authored scientific journal articles on the genetic regulation of aging and lifespan, one of which was a Nature cover story. He holds a B.A. with honors from Rice University and a Ph.D. from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology.
