Biography

Anna Palmer is a Venture Partner at Flybridge, where her stated focus is agentic business applications and marketplaces and where the firm credits her with leading investments in Chief, Zubale, MixLab and Venus Aerospace. She co-founded XFactor Ventures with Flybridge Capital in 2016 and was announced in December 2020 as the first woman general partner in the firm's history. Before investing she co-founded and led Fashion Project, acquired by U5 in 2016, and Dough Collective, acquired by IMC in 2021. She holds a B.A. in Communications from Eureka College and a J.D. from Harvard Law School, and is founder and Managing Partner of Boston's National Women's Soccer League team.
