Biography

Charlie Pinto is an Operating Partner at Bling Capital, where he onboards portfolio companies and runs the firm's Series A program. His stated focus areas are SaaS, enterprise, marketplace, consumer health, defense tech and frontier tech. He worked on Google Domains and was Director of Product Management at Rocket Lawyer. He co-founded and led the Y Combinator-backed payments company SlidePay, which Rocket Lawyer acquired, and was the twelfth employee at WePay, acquired by JPMorgan Chase. He holds a B.S. from the University of Maryland.
