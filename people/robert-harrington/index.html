Biography

Robert Harrington is the only person named on SBXi's website. SBXi's About page states that he joined SBXi in 2023, that he is the Director of Operations, and that he is based in Cambridge, Massachusetts. The same page states that before SBXi he co-founded a start-up in the real estate space, after beginning his career on the Interest Rate Derivatives Desk at GFI Group in New York City, and that he holds a B.A. in Economics from Boston College, where he was a group leader for the Big Brothers Big Sisters program of Massachusetts Bay. SBXi states that he is regularly on the MIT campus and hosts office hours virtually and in person every Tuesday for MIT alumni or students interested in the fund. SBXi publishes no investment decision-making title for him and describes investment decisions as being made by the voting partners of its eight sponsoring funds, so no investment is attributed to him by any source found.
