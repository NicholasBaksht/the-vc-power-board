Biography

Elizabeth Bailey is a Managing Partner of Foreground Capital and a co-founder of RH Capital, the impact first women's health venture fund created under the nonprofit Rhia Ventures in 2019, whose Funds I and II Foreground continues to manage following the team's 2024 spinout. She brings more than 20 years of venture capital and early stage healthcare investing experience, including as a Partner at Commons Capital, a double bottom line fund. At RH Capital she helped raise a $5 million Fund I and an oversubscribed $38.5 million Fund II focused on maternal health, contraception, fertility and other underinvested areas. She serves on the boards of Raydiant Oximetry and Testmate Health and holds an MPA from Harvard Kennedy School and a BA from Brown University.
