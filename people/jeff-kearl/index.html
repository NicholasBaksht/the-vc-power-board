Biography

Jeff Kearl is listed as a Partner on Pelion's official team page. Domo, Inc.'s proxy statement filed 1 June 2026 names him as a director nominee and states that he has been managing director at Pelion Venture Partners since October 2019. GrainChain's February 2023 funding announcement states that the company would add him to its board of directors. He is named as a Manager of the General Partner in the Form D filings for Pelion Ventures VIII, L.P. and Pelion Ventures VIII-C, L.P. No education could be sourced for him from an acceptable source.
