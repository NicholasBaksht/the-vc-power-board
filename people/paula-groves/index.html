Biography

Paula Groves is a co-founder of Impact X Capital, the London-based venture firm launched by Eric Collins in 2018 to back underrepresented founders in Europe. She graduated from Stanford University in 1986 with an undergraduate degree in economics, worked at Credit Suisse First Boston in New York, took a graduate degree at Harvard Business School in 1991, worked at Leo Burnett in Chicago, helped launch the Boston private equity fund Triumph Capital, and in 2000 co-founded Axxon Capital, which raised over $50 million to back Black and women technology founders. In 2024 she was quoted as Impact X's Chief Investment Officer on the firm's seed investment in the Swedish maritime greentech company Cetasol.
