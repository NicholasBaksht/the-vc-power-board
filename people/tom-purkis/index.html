Biography

Tom Purkis is a Partner at Maven Capital Partners who leads the London office and is a co-lead on Maven's Institutional Buyout Fund. His Maven profile says he is responsible for deal origination and execution of management buyout investments across London and the South of England, typically investing between GBP 5 million and GBP 20 million of equity. He is also listed under the Bristol office. No joining year is published on his Maven profile. Before Maven he was an Associate Director in the M&A team at Grant Thornton in London, advising on private and public company transactions for private equity and corporate clients, having started his career in Business Restructuring before moving into Transaction Services. He graduated from the University of Durham with a BA (Honours) in Business and is a member of the ICAEW. Maven's own Envitia case study names and quotes him as the Partner on that management buyout.
