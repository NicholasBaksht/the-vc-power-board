Biography

Josep Maria Echarri, whose name appears in Spanish corporate filings as José María Echarri Torres, is listed as Managing Partner on Inveready's team page. The governance page of the listed Spanish energy company Audax Renovables, where he is a director, gives a fuller account: he holds degrees in Economics and in Actuarial and Financial Sciences, both from the Universidad de Barcelona, and a master's in Financial Management from ESADE. He was Director Financiero of Oryzon from 2003 to 2007 and, before that, ran what the page describes as the first integrated technology company creation programme developed by a Spanish public administration. The same page states that he is Consejero Delegado of Inveready Asset Management, S.G.E.C.R. and Presidente of Grupo Financiero Inveready, and that he is a founding partner and the largest shareholder of both. It states that from his position at Inveready he took an active part in dozens of corporate transactions, specifically naming the sale of PasswordBank Technologies, S.L. to Symantec, the sale of Indisys, S.L. to Intel, and Masmóvil's purchase of Pepephone. He is a member of the Instituto de Consejeros-Administradores and holds its diploma in good corporate governance for professional directors. CURRENCY NOTE: the Audax biography also names him as a board member of Masmóvil Ibercom, Agile Contents and Oryzon Genomics. Only the Audax and Atrys seats are recorded here as current, because Oryzon's own board page as at 7 September 2026 does not list him, Masmóvil Ibercom was taken private in 2020, and Agile Content is reported to have left BME Growth in August 2026; those three are treated as historic or unverified and are excluded from boardSeats.
