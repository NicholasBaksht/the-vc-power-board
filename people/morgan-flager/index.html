Biography

Morgan Flager is Managing Partner of Silverton Partners in Austin, Texas. His firm biography states he joined Silverton in 2006, has over 27 years of experience as a venture investor and technology executive, and has sponsored 26 investments and realized 13 acquisitions and two IPOs. Before Silverton he was a venture investor at FTV Capital in San Francisco, working on growth investments in software and financial technology, and earlier worked at Ingrian Networks (acquired by SafeNet) and Kintana (acquired by Mercury Interactive). He received his BS from Stanford University. He is named as a manager of the upper-tier general partner on the Form D filings for Silverton Partners VII (2022) and Silverton Partners VIII (2026). SEC Form D filings list him as a Director of Virdee, Scorability, Helper Bees and CenterSeat.
