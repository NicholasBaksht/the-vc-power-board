Biography

Rami Kalish is a Managing Partner and Co-Founder of Pitango Venture Capital, which he founded in 1993 under the name Polaris and where Chemi Peres joined him three years later. Pitango's site states that he specializes in cyber technology and B2B software companies and has mentored entrepreneurs and supported companies on their growth journeys to IPOs. His board experience includes ForeScout, Radware, Retalix and CardGuard, companies his firm bio states became publicly traded on NASDAQ. A 2016 ForeScout corporate document lists him on that company's board of directors.
