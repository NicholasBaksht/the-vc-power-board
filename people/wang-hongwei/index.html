Wang Hongwei (王红卫)

大湾区投资部副总经理、董事总经理 (Deputy General Manager of the Greater Bay Area Investment Department; Managing Director) at Shenzhen Capital Group · Joined 2016

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Wang Hongwei is deputy general manager of Shenzhen Capital Group's Greater Bay Area Investment Department with the title of Managing Director. The group's team page states he holds a bachelor's degree in optical instrument engineering, a doctorate in management and the rank of researcher, joined the group in 2016, has sixteen years of experience in technology entrepreneurship services and venture capital management, that the investment team he leads has led, participated in or made fund commitments to more than seventy projects of which twenty five have listed, and that he has overseen the incubation of more than three thousand technology companies. An interview published on the group's site on 27 April 2021 identifies him as head of the management team of Shenzhen Talent Fund No. 1 and general manager of the group's Talent Fund Investment Department, gives his bachelor's degree as from Zhejiang University, records his earlier roles as director of the Shenzhen Nanshan District Science and Technology Service Centre, director of the Nanshan District Dasha River Innovation Corridor Construction Office and standing director of the Shenzhen Venture Capital Association, and states that he then managed Shenzhen Talent Fund No. 1 with a first phase size of RMB 2 billion (20亿元), listing representative investment cases. No board seat is stated for him in any source found.
