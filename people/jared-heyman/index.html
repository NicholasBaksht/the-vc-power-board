Biography

Jared Heyman is listed as Managing Partner of Rebel Fund on the firm's site. He is named as a related person on the Form D filings of Rebel Fund II LP (filed 27 and 28 March 2024) and Rebel Fund III LP (filed 19 March 2026), both of which list Rebel Management, LLC as management company and a principal place of business in San Francisco, California. Before Rebel Fund he founded Infosurv in 1998 in Atlanta, Georgia, an internet survey company for employee and customer market research, and he was founder and chief executive officer of CrowdMed, established April 2013, a crowdsourcing platform for medical case diagnosis. He holds a business degree from the University of Texas. No board seat, IPO count or exit count for him could be sourced, and no individual investment is attributed to him by name in an acceptable source, so notableInvestments is empty.
