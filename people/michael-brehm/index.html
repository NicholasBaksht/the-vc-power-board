Michael Brehm

View Live Interactive Profile →

Biography

Michael Brehm is General Partner and Founder of Redstone, which he co-founded with Samuli Siren. On his Redstone profile page he describes his aim as a firm that is half a software and data company and half run by people, and says he is involved across investment strategies and new initiatives, fundraising for the firm's strategies and support for founders. His profile on the Bridge to Growth site states that he graduated from WHU, joined the investment bank Merrill Lynch, joined VZ-Netzwerke (studiVZ) in mid-2006, has invested in companies since 2006, founded Rebate Networks in February 2010 and founded i2x in 2015. His IdeaLab speaker biography adds that he was Executive Director at studiVZ and served as managing director of i2x until 2022. He is listed as a Member of the Investment Committee of Bridge to Growth, which is an investment committee role and not a board seat. i2x also appears as an exited company on Redstone's own portfolio page.
