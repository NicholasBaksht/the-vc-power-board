Biography

Drew Oetting is a co-founder and partner of 8VC, investing across stages and sectors including vertical software, health delivery and biomanufacturing. Before 8VC he was a partner at Formation 8 and chief of staff to Joe Lonsdale. He holds degrees in mathematics and economics from Claremont McKenna College. In 2020 he co-founded Operation Masks with 8VC partner Jake Medwell to supply PPE to medical workers during the COVID-19 pandemic.
