Biography

Joe Cunningham, MD is a Founding Managing Director of Santé Ventures, which he co-founded in 2006. A board certified internal medicine physician and Fellow of the American College of Physicians, he previously served as Chief Medical Officer of Providence Health System and Executive Director of Providence Health Alliance, both part of Ascension. He was also a healthcare venture partner with Austin Ventures and vice chair of the investment committee of Ascension Health Ventures. He serves on boards at Cleveland Clinic Innovations and the Innovation Growth Board of Mass General Brigham, and chairs innovation efforts for the Texas A&M System. He earned a BS and an MD from Texas A&M University and an MBA from Baylor University.
