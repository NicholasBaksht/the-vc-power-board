Biography

Seth Berman co-founded Susa Ventures in 2013 alongside Chad Byers and Leo Polovets, and the firm announced a $25 million debut seed fund in 2014. Before Susa he was an angel investor and formerly vice president of strategic marketing at The Richemont Group. His bio at Kivu Ventures states that he led Susa's investments in Flexport, Expanse, Chapter and Pulley. He is Founder and General Partner at Kivu Ventures, where his stated focus is AI, AI infrastructure, logistics, digital health, deep tech and fintech, and susaventures.com no longer lists him on its team.
