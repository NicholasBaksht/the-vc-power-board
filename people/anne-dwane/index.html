Biography

Anne Dwane is a General Partner at Village Global, which she co-founded in 2017. She co-founded Military Advantage, later Military.com, which was acquired by Monster, then served as CEO of Zinch and, after Chegg acquired it, as Chegg's Chief Business Officer with P&L responsibility spanning the company's IPO. She holds a bachelor's degree in Marketing and International Management from Georgetown University and an M.B.A. from Harvard Business School, and she serves on the board of Harvard Business Publishing. Forbes placed her at number 20 on its 2026 Midas Seed List.
