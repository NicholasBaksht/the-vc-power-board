Biography

Myles Grote is a Partner at Elevate Ventures. His team-member page states he is focused on supporting Elevate's investment processes and the continued refinement of its investment architecture, that he is an Indianapolis native from the IU Kelley School of Business, and that he has spent 17 years building and investing in technology companies. It names his most recent prior role as Head of Portfolio Success at High Alpha Innovation, says he launched Omnidollar, which Elevate describes as the first global syndicate in Indiana, and says he spent four years as an EIR and mentor with Techstars. He appeared as a Partner at Elevate Ventures on the firm's 2026 Predictions Workshop panel published on 8 December 2025. No individual company has been tied to him by a first-party source, so notableInvestments is empty.
