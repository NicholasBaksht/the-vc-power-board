Biography

Izabel Gallera is a Partner at Canary, a venture capital firm born in Brazil that invests in early-stage Latin American startups. LAVCA's profile of her states that she has led dozens of investments in the region and that, prior to investing, she operated in startups and enterprises across Brazil and North America. TechCrunch reported that Canary, formed in 2017 by Marcos Toledo with Florian Hagenbuch, Mate Pencz, Patrick de Picciotto and Julio Vasconcellos, then brought her in as partner. She previously worked at Endeavor, where she met the Canary partners.
