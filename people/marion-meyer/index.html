Biography

Marion Meyer is listed as Venture Partner, Europe, on the SVG Ventures team page. Her bio describes her as a strategist, business developer and investor with more than twenty years of management and leadership experience spanning strategic development, building and leading operations, and innovation management, with a career that began in the media industry in strategy management, M&A and new business operations. It states she was Head of Business Development at BayWa AG from 2012, Chief Operating Officer of the Cefetra Group from 2016 and Chief Strategy & Innovation Officer of the BayWa Group from 2019. The bio states she has served on various public and private boards and on several board positions of portfolio companies but does not name any of them, so no board seat is recorded. She holds a diploma in Business Administration from Baden-Wuerttemberg Cooperative State University Ravensburg and completed executive management programs at Stanford University and Harvard University.
