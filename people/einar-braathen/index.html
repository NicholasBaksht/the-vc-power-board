Biography

Einar Braathen is listed on the CoinFund team page under Investment with the title Partner. CoinFund's August 2022 announcement of its $300M CoinFund Ventures I fund announces the addition of Einar Braathen from Accel to the CoinFund Venture Investing Team. CoinFund's author index credits him with the firm's investment announcements for Irys, Sindri, Prime Intellect, Giza and STON.fi, and he co-authored the firm's Pluralis announcement with Jake Brukhman and Rishin Sharma. In June 2026 he authored the firm's Edge Markets piece, which states that CoinFund is leading Edge Markets' $29M Series A funding round.
