Biography

Robert Williams is a Partner at Artesian in the VCaaS business, based in Sydney, and joined the firm in 2017. His profile states he leads Artesian's agrifood venture capital investment activities and is a portfolio manager for the GrainInnovate Fund and the SproutX VC Fund. He previously managed an agricultural investment team at Macquarie Infrastructure and Real Assets. He holds a Bachelor of Arts and Bachelor of Commerce from the Australian National University.
