Biography

Barry Sandweiss is a General Partner at Cultivation Capital working with software technology companies. In 2000 he cofounded Lenders One, described on his firm profile as the largest mortgage-banking cooperative in the United States representing nearly 14 percent of US residential originations, and served as its Chief Operating Officer. Lenders One was acquired by Altisource Portfolio Solutions in 2010, after which he became Senior Vice President and served on the Global Leadership team. He has more than 20 years in real estate and real estate finance and cofounded Community Lenders of America. He attended the University of Wisconsin-Madison and is based in St. Louis. No board seat or attributed portfolio company is published on his firm profile. The Form D for Cultivation Capital Tech Fund IV, LP names him as a Managing Director of the fund's general partner.
